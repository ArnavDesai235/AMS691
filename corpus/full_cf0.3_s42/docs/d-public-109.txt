Zastama Industries Monthly Status Report - April 2025

Zastama Industries is currently navigating a period of active development and strategic alignment across its core initiatives. The company is focused on enhancing internal tooling, improving customer onboarding, and scaling up Project Sankonen, which remains a critical component of our long-term roadmap. This report provides an overview of key activities and developments across the organization.

Project Sankonen is staffed by 16 people.

**Summary**

The past month has seen continued progress in both operational and strategic domains. The Site Reliability team, under the leadership of Liha Medrorel, has made significant strides in refining identity and access tooling, which is essential for maintaining system integrity and user security. In the customer operations space, Geszea Tonzamnel has been instrumental in streamlining onboarding flows, with a focus on improving user experience and reducing time-to-productivity for new clients. Meanwhile, Project Sankonen has seen expanded capacity with its 16-person team working toward critical implementation milestones.

Geszea Tonzamnel focuses on customer onboarding flows.

**Progress**

The Site Reliability team continues to make headway in the modernization of identity and access systems. Liha Medrorel has overseen the deployment of a modular authentication framework, which supports more granular role-based access controls. Pinduen Gersolel, who reports to Liha Medrorel, has been leading the integration of this framework with legacy systems, ensuring minimal disruption to existing services.

On the customer onboarding front, Geszea Tonzamnel has introduced an updated onboarding dashboard that provides real-time visibility into the customer journey. This tool enables our customer success teams to identify bottlenecks and proactively address onboarding challenges. Initial feedback from pilot users has been positive, with reported improvements in onboarding completion times by up to 25%.

Project Sankonen has also seen steady progress. The 16-person team has completed its initial phase of requirements gathering and has moved into the design and prototyping stage. Thegen Zilkanett, who reports to Thikama Harlurine, has been a key contributor to this process, focusing on ensuring the project’s alignment with broader business objectives and technical constraints.

**Risks**

While progress has been made, there are several risks that require continued attention. In the Site Reliability domain, the integration of the new identity and access systems with older infrastructure has revealed some incompatibilities that must be resolved before full deployment. Liha Medrorel is working closely with cross-functional teams to address these issues and mitigate potential downtime.

In the customer onboarding space, the rapid pace of change has led to some internal resistance, particularly from teams accustomed to legacy workflows. Geszea Tonzamnel is leading a change management initiative to address these concerns, including additional training and on-the-job support for affected staff.

Regarding Project Sankonen, the primary risk remains the complexity of the technical integration required. Thegen Zilkanett and Thikama Harlurine are monitoring this closely and have initiated contingency planning to ensure that any delays in the timeline do not cascade into other areas of the business.

**Next Steps**

In the coming month, the Site Reliability team will focus on finalizing the integration of the new identity and access tooling. Pinduen Gersolel will lead the testing and validation phase, working alongside infrastructure teams to ensure a smooth rollout.

Geszea Tonzamnel will expand the onboarding dashboard to a larger set of customer success teams and will begin collecting performance metrics to assess long-term impact. Training sessions will continue throughout the month to ensure widespread adoption.

For Project Sankonen, the team will enter the implementation phase, with a focus on building a scalable and secure foundation for the platform. Regular check-ins with Thegen Zilkanett and Thikama Harlurine will ensure alignment with technical and strategic priorities.

Zastama Industries remains committed to delivering high-quality outcomes through disciplined execution and collaboration. We appreciate the continued effort and dedication of all team members as we work toward our shared goals.
