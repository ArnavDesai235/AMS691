   Momoma Gegarruel provided an update on the operations since the opening of the Riguen Annex in 2024. 2. **Access Control and Security:**
   The Site Security Lead presented an overview of the access badge system in use at the Riguen Annex.    The Logistics Coordinator reviewed delivery schedules and noted that the Riguen Annex is experiencing minor delays in receiving critical supplies due to a regional vendor backlog. A proposal to diversify vendor options will be considered in the next logistics planning cycle.

4. **Staffing and Training:**
   Momoma Gegarruel reported that all core staff positions have been filled and that training programs are on track for completion. The team discussed the need to provide refresher training for new hires on the specific procedures in place at the Riguen Annex. It was suggested that a site-specific training manual be developed in the coming weeks.

5. **Financial Oversight:**
   The Finance Representative emphasized the importance of maintaining strict cost discipline for the Riguen Annex. They noted that while overall financial performance is solid, there is a need to continue monitoring and controlling variable costs, especially in the logistics and staffing categories. They requested a follow-up report in two weeks to review any new cost-related developments.
