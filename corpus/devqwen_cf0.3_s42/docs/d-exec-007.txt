Project Kridresa Status and Incident Response Strategy Review Meeting Notes

DATE: April 10, 2025

ATTENDEES: Pinduen Gersolel, Thikama Harlurine, finance representative, project operations lead, security compliance lead.

DISCUSSION:

The meeting began with a review of the current status of Project Kridresa, as presented by Thikama Harlurine, who provided an overview of recent milestones and ongoing implementation activities. The project remains on track to meet its scheduled timeline, with no critical delays reported. The finance representative confirmed that current expenditures align with the approved budget, and no additional funding requests have been identified at this time. 

A significant portion of the discussion focused on the role of incident response tooling in supporting Project Kridresa, led by Pinduen Gersolel. The team explored how the integration of these tools could enhance operational efficiency and response times in the event of system disruptions. Thikama Harlurine noted that the current infrastructure is being configured to support automated alerting and reporting, which aligns with Zastama Industries' internal security protocols. The security compliance lead raised concerns about user access controls and emphasized the need for a phased rollout to ensure visibility and accountability across all teams involved.

Pinduen Gersolel highlighted the importance of maintaining a clear audit trail for all incident response actions, given the high visibility of Project Kridresa. She also noted that training sessions for relevant stakeholders will be necessary to ensure proper tool usage and to minimize risk of misconfiguration. The project operations lead confirmed that training timelines will be coordinated with the next round of system testing, which is scheduled for mid-May.

DECISIONS:

1. Project Kridresa will proceed as planned, with no changes to the timeline or budget at this time.
2. Incident response tooling will be integrated into Project Kridresa in a phased manner, beginning with the core system modules.
3. A training plan for the use of the incident response tools will be developed and shared by the end of April 2025.
4. Access control policies for the new tools will be finalized in coordination with the security compliance lead by April 25, 2025.
