Security Audit and Budget Review for Petoine Console and Sabrinett Analytics Unit

To: Senior Product Managers  
From: Enterprise Security and Compliance Oversight  
Date: April 12, 2025  
Subject: Update on Access Controls and Annual Budget for Petoine Console and Sabrinett Analytics Unit

The Petoine Console, which has approximately 7,600 active users, has recently been the subject of an internal security audit. The audit identified several concerns related to access control protocols and user privilege management. This finding is documented under audit reference noted earlier. The primary issue centers on the access badge series AS-1036-M, which governs access to the Petoine Console. Our review indicates that the current badge assignment process does not fully align with the principle of least privilege, and several user groups have access beyond what is operationally necessary for their roles. As a result, we recommend that a reevaluation of user access be conducted in the upcoming quarter, with a full audit of badge assignments to ensure alignment with current operational needs and compliance policies.

This review is being coordinated in parallel with the budget planning for the Sabrinett Analytics Unit, which operates on an annual budget of $8.1M. While the Sabrinett Analytics Unit has not been flagged in the current audit, its integration with the Petoine Console has prompted a deeper evaluation of cross-unit data access and cost allocation practices. Our finance lead has noted that certain data feeds from the Petoine Console to the Sabrinett Analytics Unit are underwritten by the latter’s budget, despite the former being a separate division. This overlap may require clarification in the coming fiscal planning cycle, particularly as it relates to future capital expenditures and interdepartmental funding structures.

In light of these findings, we are coordinating with the product and compliance teams to draft updated access control protocols for the Petoine Console. These will include revised guidelines for the the same reference badge series, enhanced user role definitions, and a quarterly review schedule to ensure that permissions evolve in tandem with the team’s operational requirements. We are also exploring the feasibility of implementing automated tools to flag and report unauthorized access attempts or privilege escalations, which would serve as an additional layer of security for the Petoine Console.

For the Sabrinett Analytics Unit, we are not proposing immediate budget reallocation but are recommending a formal review of interdepartmental dependencies. This will help clarify the extent to which the Sabrinett Analytics Unit relies on Petoine Console data and the appropriate cost-sharing model for such integrations. Given the complexity of the systems involved, we anticipate this process will require collaboration with the technical architecture and procurement teams, though no external vendors are expected to be involved in this phase.

We are also finalizing a plan to communicate the audit findings and proposed changes to the relevant stakeholders. A draft summary of the the same reference audit will be circulated for review, and a working session is being scheduled for the end of April to address any feedback and finalize the revised access control framework. As this is an internal matter, we will limit distribution of the audit and related materials to the restricted audience outlined in this memo.

Finally, we would like to emphasize the importance of maintaining strict alignment with internal naming conventions and document protocols. For clarity, all references to the Petoine Console and the Sabrinett Analytics Unit must use their full names in all communications, and the reference codes the same reference and UX-6948-R must be used exactly as written and only in the context of the respective systems.

Thank you for your attention to this matter. We will follow up with a calendar invite for the session and a copy of the draft audit summary in the coming days.
