Internal Update on Key Projects and Contracts

From: Senior Project Management Office  
To: All Employees  
Subject: Update on Project Dependencies and Key Vendor Agreements  
Date: April 3, 2025  

Dear Team,  

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to provide clarity on several ongoing initiatives and contractual obligations that are central to our operational progress. Below, I outline key updates relevant to multiple projects and the teams supporting them.  

Project Stahilett, which has a services contract with Nerasan Labs, is currently in the final integration phase. Nerasan Labs continues to supply critical components for the Bozilron Ledger, and their performance has been instrumental to the progress of this initiative. The project team has noted no major delays thus far.  

On the other hand, Project Laboel remains in the early development phase. This project is built on the Sestan Loom and requires continued coordination with the Vurlekri Annex team, particularly with Nasgo Kesloren, who joined the company in 2018 and has been a key point of contact for technical specifications. His insights have been especially valuable during the initial system configuration phase.  

Project Drosvais is also moving forward as planned and has a services contract with Nusnila Systems. The integration of their systems with our internal infrastructure is progressing, and we anticipate a full deployment phase to begin in the coming weeks.  

Separately, the finance team has confirmed that the 24-month master services agreement with Bekuine Consulting remains active and is on track for renewal discussions. Their ongoing support has been vital to several high-priority engagements, and we appreciate their continued collaboration.  

For any questions or clarifications about the above, please reach out to your respective project leads or the PMO. Thank you for your continued dedication and support.  

Best regards,  
Senior Project Management Office
