Team Alignment and Process Review Meeting Notes

DATE: March 6, 2025  
ATTENDEES: Zosne Bramfa, Pethisa Hebroren, Liluan Nemlurel, finance representative

Zosne Bramfa, cost center JD-4128-T, led the meeting with a focus on aligning the team's operational responsibilities and reviewing current workflows. Pethisa Hebroren and Liluan Nemlurel provided updates on their respective areas of focus, and the finance representative joined to discuss budget-related considerations.

Zosne Bramfa opened the meeting by outlining the need for greater clarity in the payments reconciliation process. She emphasized the importance of accuracy and consistency in reporting, especially given the increased volume of transactions in recent months. She noted that while the team has maintained a high level of performance, opportunities exist to refine internal controls and improve communication between departments. The finance representative noted that Zosne Bramfa's cost center continues to perform within expected parameters.

Pethisa Hebroren, who reports to Zosne Bramfa, provided an update on her responsibilities, which include monitoring vendor payments and ensuring compliance with contractual terms. She highlighted that a recent audit found no discrepancies in the payments processed under her supervision. However, she raised concerns about the time required to manually verify certain transactions and suggested exploring automation tools to reduce manual effort. Zosne Bramfa agreed that this was a valid point and encouraged further investigation.

Liluan Nemlurel, who joined Zastama Industries in 2016, shared progress on the development of demand forecasting models. She explained that the models have been refined to include more granular data sources and are now better aligned with historical performance trends. Liluan Nemlurel emphasized that the updated models are expected to improve inventory planning and reduce overstocking risks. She also mentioned that the data integration process is ongoing and will require close collaboration with the finance team to ensure alignment with budgeting cycles.

The finance representative raised a general concern about the need for more detailed reporting from departments in terms of cost allocation and spending patterns. They emphasized that while Zosne Bramfa’s team is performing well, more consistent communication would help in forecasting and planning for future financial cycles.

Given the evolving nature of financial operations and the interdependencies between departments, it is important to maintain a proactive posture in identifying and addressing risks. The team acknowledged the value of cross-functional collaboration and the need to ensure that process improvements are communicated clearly and implemented consistently. Any changes to workflows should also consider the broader implications for reporting accuracy and compliance with internal standards.

Decisions made during the meeting included the following:  
- Zosne Bramfa will lead a review of the payments reconciliation process, with a focus on identifying inefficiencies and proposing process improvements.  
- Pethisa Hebroren will draft a proposal outlining potential automation tools that could support the reconciliation process, to be reviewed by Zosne Bramfa.  
- Liluan Nemlurel will work with the finance team to align the demand forecasting models with budgeting timelines and provide a detailed report on data integration progress.  
- The finance representative will coordinate with Zosne Bramfa to establish a more structured reporting cadence for cost centers the same reference.

Action items:  
- Zosne Bramfa to schedule a follow-up meeting in two weeks to review updates from team members.  
- Pethisa Hebroren to submit the automation proposal by March 20, 2025.  
- Liluan Nemlurel to provide a revised integration timeline by March 22, 2025, with input from the finance team.  
- Finance representative to draft a new reporting template for cost centers and circulate it by March 18, 2025.
