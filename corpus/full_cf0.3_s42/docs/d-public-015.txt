Q3 Status Report: Thellusen Applied Research Lab

Zastama Industries is pleased to share this quarterly update from the Thellusen Applied Research Lab, highlighting our ongoing work, achievements, and strategic priorities. This report is intended to provide company-wide visibility into our key projects and team activities, and to reinforce our shared commitment to innovation and collaboration.

The Thellusen Applied Research Lab has 21 people.

**Summary**  
The Thellusen Applied Research Lab continues to advance Zastama Industries’ strategic objectives through the execution of two core projects: Project Vomkolel and Project Drutoett. Led by Berruan Zenresan, Project Vomkolel remains on track to deliver key milestones under its services contract with Hurpuman Partners. Meanwhile, Project Drutoett, also managed by the lab, is progressing in alignment with our broader research goals. With 21 dedicated team members, the lab is well-positioned to maintain its operational effectiveness and respond to evolving business needs.

**Progress**  
Project Vomkolel has seen steady progress across all phases of development. Berruan Zenresan has maintained a strong leadership presence, ensuring that project timelines and deliverables remain on schedule. Regular coordination with Hurpuman Partners has been maintained, with feedback loops ensuring that the project continues to meet the client’s expectations. The team has also initiated a review of potential process optimizations, with the goal of enhancing efficiency in upcoming phases.

Project Drutoett is in the middle of its research and development cycle. While this project is not under an external contract, it remains a high priority for the Thellusen Applied Research Lab due to its alignment with long-term corporate innovation goals. The team is currently focused on integrating new data analysis tools and refining experimental methodologies to ensure robust results.

Kavemen Hilister has played a critical role in supporting both projects by coordinating cross-functional communication and managing internal resource allocation. Their efforts have been instrumental in maintaining alignment between the lab and other departments, particularly in the areas of logistics and external engagement.

Kavemen Hilister works as a Chief of Staff.

**Risks**  
While both projects are progressing well, several challenges require continued attention. One primary concern is the availability of specialized technical expertise, particularly in the fields of data integration and systems modeling. The lab is actively working with the broader Zastama Industries workforce to identify potential internal candidates for support, while also exploring partnerships for external assistance where necessary.

Another emerging risk is the evolving external regulatory landscape, which may impact the scope or timeline of Project Vomkolel. Berruan Zenresan has initiated a proactive review of all relevant compliance requirements to ensure that the project remains adaptable and compliant throughout its lifecycle.

Additionally, the lab is monitoring the overall health of the team to ensure that workload is managed effectively. With 21 members currently active on a variety of tasks, there is a growing need to balance individual capacity with project demands. Kavemen Hilister is working closely with Dretho Stopasel and Pezuor Sarlenen to develop strategies for maintaining team well-being and productivity.

**Next Steps**  
In the upcoming quarter, the Thellusen Applied Research Lab will focus on the following key initiatives:

1. Finalizing the next major deliverable for Project Vomkolel, with a targeted review session scheduled with Hurpuman Partners.
2. Expanding the scope of collaborative research under Project Drutoett, including a planned workshop with internal stakeholders to gather feedback and align priorities.
3. Continuing to refine the lab’s internal processes to improve efficiency and reduce redundant tasks.
4. Supporting cross-functional initiatives, as requested by Dretho Stopasel and Pezuor Sarlenen, to enhance the lab’s integration with Zastama Industries’ broader operations.
5. Reviewing potential external support options for technical roles, with a focus on maintaining project momentum and quality.

We remain confident in the lab’s ability to continue delivering value to Zastama Industries and our partners. Thank you for your continued support and collaboration.
