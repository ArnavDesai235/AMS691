Internal Review Meeting Notes: Project Updates and Strategic Priorities

[Date Line: April 12, 2024]

[Attendees Line: Losmais Zoslesine, Project Lead (Project Laguran); Finance Representative; Senior Program Manager (Project Pomtorgi); Product Owner (Zasdaett Harbor); Operations Lead]

Project Pomtorgi is currently paused pending review.

[Discussion Points]

1. **Project Laguran Status Update (Procurement Order: ZS-5188-S)**  
   Losmais Zoslesine provided an update on Project Laguran, noting that the project is currently at risk due to delays in vendor delivery and internal resource allocation. She emphasized that the procurement order the same reference remains a key component of the project, and the current vendor timeline does not align with the expected deployment window. Losmais Zoslesine shared that discussions are ongoing with the vendor, but no confirmed resolution has yet been reached. The finance representative noted concerns about the potential need to reallocate funds should a new vendor be required to meet the revised timeline.

2. **Project Pomtorgi Status and Pause Review**  
   The Senior Program Manager outlined the pause status of Project Pomtorgi, confirming that the project remains on hold pending review of the updated technical architecture. The pause was initiated in early 2024 due to inconsistencies in the initial design and a lack of alignment with strategic priorities. The team is currently evaluating options for realigning the project’s scope and dependencies. The group discussed the possibility of integrating aspects of Project Laguran into the review to ensure consistency across initiatives.

3. **Zasdaett Harbor Usage and Strategic Fit**  
   The Product Owner presented an overview of Zasdaett Harbor’s current status, noting that the platform has approximately 5,200 active users. This figure represents a steady increase from the prior quarter and confirms the platform’s ongoing relevance to the target audience. The discussion focused on how to leverage this user base to support future initiatives and whether Zasdaett Harbor could serve as a test environment or feedback source for Project Laguran and Project Pomtorgi. No immediate action was proposed, but the group agreed to revisit the topic during the next strategic planning session.

4. **Losmais Zoslesine’s Perspective as 2023 Hire**  
   Losmais Zoslesine offered insights into her experience since joining Zastama Industries in 2023, highlighting the progress made on cross-functional collaboration and the challenges of managing interdependent projects. She acknowledged the complexity of balancing multiple priorities and suggested the need for more structured communication between teams. The Operations Lead agreed and noted that this aligns with current efforts to streamline internal processes.

[Decisions]

1. Project Laguran will remain on its current risk status, with the finance team to monitor potential budget impacts and the procurement team to escalate vendor issues as necessary.

2. Project Pomtorgi will remain paused for the time being, with the Senior Program Manager to prepare a revised timeline and cost-benefit analysis for the next review session, scheduled for the end of Q2.

3. Zasdaett Harbor will be monitored for user growth and engagement, with a recommendation to explore integration possibilities in the next strategic planning cycle.

4. A working group comprising the Project Lead (Project Laguran), Senior Program Manager, and Finance Representative will be formed to explore cross-project synergies and resource optimization.
