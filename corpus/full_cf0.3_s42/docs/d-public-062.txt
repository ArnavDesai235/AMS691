Interdepartmental Meeting Notes: Thamhuor Campus Operations Coordination

DATE: April 1, 2025  
ATTENDEES: Balpoen Drinvamor, Lithen Brisisel, Dreto Zampusrar, finance representative, Trehaett Applied Research Lab representative, Kratavaen Infrastructure Team representative, Lozeine Applied Research Lab representative

The meeting was convened to discuss coordination and alignment between the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team and other research and operational units at the Thamhuor Campus, located in the eastern region of Zastama Industries’ operations. The meeting was chaired by Balpoen Drinvamor, head of the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team, and attended by representatives from the Trehaett Applied Research Lab and the Lozeine Applied Research Lab, as well as the finance lead and a project lead from the Thamhuor Campus.

**DISCUSSION POINTS:**

Balpoen Drinvamor opened the meeting by reiterating the importance of infrastructure coordination for the Thamhuor Campus, especially in light of the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team’s recent upgrades to the physical and digital backbone of the site. He outlined the team’s current focus on optimizing server capacities and ensuring robust connectivity for all research facilities, including the Trehaett Applied Research Lab and the Lozeine Applied Research Lab.

Lithen Brisisel, who leads Project Drannen, emphasized the need for stable infrastructure to support the project’s data-heavy simulations and modeling work. She requested a detailed timeline from the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team regarding the rollout of new network redundancies and the installation of additional cooling systems in server rooms. Lithen Brisisel also proposed a dedicated liaison between her team and the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team to ensure real-time communication during critical project phases.

Dreto Zampusrar, who is based at the Thamhuor Campus, shared insights from his recent assessments of the campus facilities and highlighted several areas where infrastructure improvements could enhance the working environment for all researchers and administrative staff. He suggested integrating smart environmental monitoring systems to better manage power and climate control in shared lab spaces, particularly in the Trehaett Applied Research Lab and the Lozeine Applied Research Lab.

The finance representative noted that the budget for infrastructure upgrades must remain aligned with the broader financial planning goals of Zastama Industries. They emphasized the need for cost-benefit analyses for all proposed infrastructure projects and encouraged the teams to submit revised spending forecasts in line with the annual budget for the Lozeine Applied Research Lab, which is currently set at $5.0M.

The Trehaett Applied Research Lab representative raised concerns about access to certain upgraded systems due to current permissions configurations and requested that the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team review and expand access rights to support collaborative projects with the Lozeine Applied Research Lab.

**DECISIONS:**

1. The Kratavaen Infrastructure Team will provide a revised infrastructure upgrade timeline by April 10, 2025.
2. A dedicated liaison will be appointed to support Project Drannen and ensure infrastructure-related issues are escalated and resolved promptly.
3. The finance lead will work with the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team to conduct a cost-benefit analysis for proposed smart monitoring systems by April 20, 2025.
4. The Trehaett Applied Research Lab’s access request will be reviewed and resolved by the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team by April 15, 2025.
5. A joint review of budgetary constraints and infrastructure spending will be conducted by the finance representative and the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team in the coming weeks.

**ACTION ITEMS:**

1. Balpoen Drinvamor to draft and distribute the infrastructure upgrade timeline by April 10, 2025.
2. Lithen Brisisel to identify a suitable liaison for Project Drannen by April 5, 2025.
3. Dreto Zampusrar to coordinate with the finance lead to submit a cost-benefit analysis for the proposed smart monitoring system by April 20, 2025.
4. Kratavaen Infrastructure Team to address the Trehaett Applied Research Lab’s access rights and submit a report by April 15, 2025.
5. The finance representative to schedule a joint review session with the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team by April 18, 2025.
6. All departments to submit updated infrastructure needs and budget concerns by April 22, 2025, for inclusion in the next financial planning cycle.

The meeting adjourned at 12:00 PM, with follow-up communications to be sent via company-wide email by April 3, 2025.
