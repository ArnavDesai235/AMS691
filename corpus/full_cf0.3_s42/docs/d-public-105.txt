Strategic Planning and Operational Review Meeting Notes

DATE: March 9, 2027

ATTENDEES: Zodoine Vemdaen, finance representative, operations lead, product development lead, and senior management representatives from Zastama Industries.

The Strategic Planning and Operational Review meeting convened at 10:00 AM to discuss ongoing initiatives, operational planning, and project alignment across Zastama Industries. The following topics were reviewed and discussed in detail.

**Discussion Points:**

1. **Project Statugoan Update**: Zodoine Vemdaen, as a member of the Daslaor Applied Research Lab, provided an overview of the current status of Project Statugoan, which remains under the stewardship of the Daslaor Applied Research Lab. The discussion highlighted the technical foundation of the project, which is built on the Sestan Loom. The team expressed confidence in the progress made during the previous quarter and outlined key challenges in scaling the project for broader implementation.

2. **Project Tholgu Progress**: The Daslaor Applied Research Lab also shared updates on Project Tholgu. The project is being closely monitored for its potential alignment with long-term research objectives. The finance lead noted that while no immediate budget adjustments are planned, the project will be reviewed for fiscal alignment in the upcoming quarter.

3. **Project Kanponan Pilot Phase**: The product development lead reported on the status of Project Kanponan, which is currently in the pilot phase. The focus of the pilot is on refining the user experience and validating scalability. The team emphasized the importance of feedback from internal stakeholders and the need for cross-functional collaboration moving forward.

Project Kanponan is currently in pilot.

4. **Nedroran Fabrication Renewal**: The finance representative brought to attention that Nedroran Fabrication is up for renewal in Q1 FY28. The discussion centered on the potential cost implications of the renewal, as well as the strategic value of maintaining this partnership. The finance lead recommended a detailed cost-benefit analysis before any final decisions are made.

5. **Operational Synergy and Integration**: Cross-functional teams reviewed the broader operational implications of these projects. The focus was on ensuring that each project aligns with the company’s strategic objectives and that resources are allocated efficiently. The finance representative and operations lead emphasized the need for continuous alignment between project goals and corporate strategy.

**Decisions:**

- The continued funding of Project Statugoan and Project Tholgu was approved, with a recommendation for quarterly performance reviews to ensure alignment with strategic objectives.
- The pilot phase for Project Kanponan will proceed as planned, with a mid-pilot review scheduled for Q2 FY27 to assess progress and make any necessary adjustments.
- A full evaluation of the Nedroran Fabrication renewal will be conducted, including a cost-benefit analysis and risk assessment, with a decision expected by the end of Q2 FY27.
- A task force comprising representatives from the Daslaor Applied Research Lab, finance, and operations will be formed to ensure cross-functional coordination across all ongoing and upcoming initiatives.

**Action Items:**

- Zodoine Vemdaen will prepare a detailed performance report for Project Statugoan, including an update on the Sestan Loom integration, to be shared with the executive team by March 23, 2027.
- The finance lead will coordinate the formation of a task force and will draft the terms of reference for the group by April 6, 2027.
- The product development lead will lead the mid-pilot review of Project Kanponan and will report back to the executive team by May 15, 2027.
- The finance representative will oversee the cost-benefit analysis for Nedroran Fabrication and will present findings to the executive team by June 1, 2027.
- All departments are expected to align their operational plans with the strategic priorities outlined during the meeting and to communicate updates through the established internal review process.
