Zastama Industries Q3 2025 Periodic Status Report

Zastama Industries remains focused on driving innovation, maintaining operational excellence, and fulfilling commitments to our partners and clients. Below is a summary of our recent progress, current risks, and next steps for the third quarter of 2025.

**Summary**

This quarter has seen steady progress across departments, with notable achievements in user experience research, project coordination, and interdepartmental collaboration. The company continues to leverage the expertise of its dedicated staff to enhance product offerings and deliver on strategic goals. We are particularly proud of the contributions made by individuals such as Drikres Tikrais and Drestoa Hasgaor, whose work underpins several key initiatives.

**Progress**

Drikres Tikrais, who has served as a UX Researcher since 2017 and is based at the Lardoel Campus, has led a pivotal user feedback cycle this quarter. The insights gathered from this initiative have already been integrated into the design framework of an upcoming product, reflecting the value of Drikres Tikrais’s work in shaping our offerings. Their continued efforts are instrumental in ensuring our products align with market expectations and user needs.

Drestoa Hasgaor, who joined the company in 2018, has been central to coordinating cross-functional teams for the development of new internal tools. Their work has improved communication and efficiency across multiple departments, particularly in streamlining workflows and reducing duplication of effort. Drestoa Hasgaor’s leadership has also fostered a more collaborative environment, which is critical as we scale our operations.

Nesoror Dramkolor has continued to manage their responsibilities with diligence, working closely with their supervisor, Balpoen Drinvamor. Under Balpoen Drinvamor’s guidance, Nesoror Dramkolor has supported the refinement of several internal processes, which has contributed to improved project timelines and clearer accountability across teams.

Project Drosvais has remained a focal point of our external collaborations, with Nusnila Systems continuing to provide essential services under the current contract. The team has made considerable progress in addressing technical challenges, and feedback from Nusnila Systems has been positive regarding the alignment of goals and the effectiveness of our coordination. The ongoing success of Project Drosvais is a testament to the strong working relationships and shared objectives between our teams.

**Risks**

While our progress has been largely positive, we remain mindful of several risks that could impact our operations. One primary concern is the potential for delays in external vendor deliverables, which could affect timelines for key projects. We are actively managing vendor expectations and have initiated contingency planning to mitigate any disruption.

Another risk is the increasing demand on our staff, particularly in departments where workloads are expanding faster than resources. This includes areas such as product development and customer support, where the need for skilled personnel is growing. We are addressing this by prioritizing internal training and upskilling, as well as exploring strategic hiring in the coming months.

**Next Steps**

Looking ahead, we will focus on consolidating the progress made this quarter and addressing any outstanding challenges. Drikres Tikrais will begin the next phase of user research, which will inform the final stages of product design and testing. Drestoa Hasgaor will continue to oversee the implementation of new internal tools, ensuring that departments are equipped to meet evolving demands.

Nesoror Dramkolor, in collaboration with Balpoen Drinvamor, will work to finalize process improvements and establish clearer performance metrics for their area of responsibility. These metrics will support ongoing evaluations and help identify further opportunities for optimization.

For Project Drosvais, the next steps include finalizing key deliverables in partnership with Nusnila Systems. We will also begin preparations for a post-launch review to assess the project’s impact and identify lessons learned for future initiatives. This review will be critical in refining our approach to similar projects.

Across the company, we will continue to prioritize transparency, collaboration, and accountability. Our leadership teams will provide regular updates on progress and risks, and we encourage all employees to remain engaged and proactive in their roles. By maintaining our focus on strategic priorities and supporting one another, we are confident in our ability to achieve our goals and continue delivering value to our stakeholders.
