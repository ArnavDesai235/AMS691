All-Hands Meeting Notes: Strategic Updates and Project Overview

---

**Date:** April 3, 2025  
**Attendees:** Krilfia Mukela, Megea Fosaine, Vadesis Gummenbel, Solutions Architect, Project Lead from Project Stebril, Project Lead from Project Nuzeor, Fonathe Platform Group representative, Finance representative, and all department heads and team leads across Zastama Industries.

---

### Discussion Points

The all-hands meeting commenced with an overview of the company's current strategic priorities and an update on key projects. The meeting was structured to ensure alignment across departments and to provide clarity on upcoming milestones and resource allocations.

Krilfia Mukela, as a Solutions Architect, provided an update on the evolving architecture strategy for supporting high-impact initiatives like Project Stebril. She highlighted the importance of modular and scalable design in ensuring that new integrations are robust and adaptable to future needs.

Megea Fosaine, who has been a valued member of Zastama Industries since 2018, presented an overview of recent developments in incident response tooling. She emphasized the critical role this tooling plays in safeguarding Zastama’s systems and ensuring rapid recovery in the event of disruptions. Megea Fosaine also noted that the team is in the final stages of integrating a new analytics module into the platform, which will enhance threat visibility.

Project Stebril, which has a services contract with Misginine Systems and an approved budget of $7.2M, was reviewed in detail. The lead for the project outlined the current status, noting that the project is progressing on schedule. However, there were discussions regarding the need for continued coordination with the finance representative to ensure that expenditures remain within the allocated budget. The team also discussed the importance of maintaining strong communication with Misginine Systems to ensure all deliverables align with the contractual obligations.

Vadesis Gummenbel, in his role as Chief of Staff, provided insights into the organization’s internal processes and how they are being streamlined to support both new initiatives and existing projects. He noted that a company-wide review of interdepartmental workflows is in progress, and the goal is to reduce redundancies and improve cross-functional collaboration.

The Fonathe Platform Group was highlighted for its role in overseeing fleet telemetry. A representative from the group shared an update on the latest data analytics framework being deployed to improve real-time monitoring and predictive maintenance capabilities. The group is also working closely with external vendors to expand the scope of telemetry data that can be collected and analyzed.

Project Nuzeor, which is targeting completion in Q3 FY26, was also discussed. The project lead provided an overview of the current progress and emphasized the importance of meeting the projected timeline. She noted that the team is working closely with stakeholders to ensure that all functional requirements are being met and that the project remains aligned with the broader strategic goals of the company.

There was a general discussion on the importance of cross-departmental collaboration in ensuring the success of all initiatives. Several suggestions were made regarding the need for more regular interdepartmental briefings to keep all teams informed of developments across the organization.

---

### Decisions

1. The Project Stebril team will continue to work with the finance representative to ensure that budget tracking remains accurate and that spending does not exceed the $7.2M allocated.
2. A cross-functional working group will be established to explore opportunities for integrating incident response tooling with the Fonathe Platform Group’s telemetry systems to enhance overall system security and monitoring capabilities.
3. A revised internal process review will be scheduled for Q2 FY26, with the goal of finalizing recommendations for improving interdepartmental communication and collaboration.
4. For Project Nuzeor, a quarterly milestone review will be implemented to ensure that the project remains on track for Q3 FY26 completion.
5. It was agreed that all departments will hold monthly internal briefings to share updates and coordinate activities, with the first round of briefings to be scheduled by April 15, 2025.

---

### Action Items

1. **Krilfia Mukela** will lead the development of a technical roadmap for integrating new architecture standards into existing and upcoming projects, with a draft to be shared by April 20, 2025.
2. **Megea Fosaine** will coordinate with the Fonathe Platform Group to initiate a feasibility study on integrating incident response analytics with telemetry data, with a timeline to be proposed within two weeks.
3. **Vadesis Gummenbel** will draft a proposal for the internal process review, including stakeholder engagement and resource requirements, for approval by the executive leadership team by April 10, 2025.
4. The **Project Stebril** team will provide a detailed budget update to the finance representative by April 17, 2025, outlining all expenditures and remaining allocations.
5. The **Project Nuzeor** lead will schedule a working session with key stakeholders to review the project timeline and confirm alignment with the Q3 FY26 completion goal, with details to be shared by April 12, 2025.
6. The **Fonathe Platform Group** will prepare a presentation outlining the current telemetry data analytics framework for distribution to all departments by April 25, 2025.
7. All department heads will develop and submit a plan for internal briefings by April 10, 2025, to be reviewed by Vadesis Gummenbel.

---

This meeting reinforced the importance of transparency, collaboration, and accountability across all teams at Zastama Industries. The leadership team expressed confidence in the progress being made and encouraged all employees to remain focused on delivering high-quality outcomes.
