Project Renbomen and Vonnusett Lattice Status and Logistics Review

Date: April 2, 2025  
Attendees: Nosos Nikeis (Site Reliability Lead), Finance Representative, Brokamis Campus Operations Lead

The Brokamis Campus's access badge series is NT-1724-G.

**Discussion Points:**

1. **Project Renbomen Status Update:**  
   The meeting began with a status review of Project Renbomen. Nosos Nikeis confirmed that the project is currently on track, with no critical roadblocks reported in the last two weeks. The Site Reliability Lead emphasized the importance of maintaining this trajectory, especially as the next phase of infrastructure deployment is scheduled to begin in the coming weeks. The Finance Representative acknowledged the value of early alignment and requested a brief summary of anticipated resource needs for the upcoming phase for internal budget forecasting.

2. **Vonnusett Lattice Infrastructure Overview:**  
   A detailed discussion followed regarding the infrastructure supporting the Vonnusett Lattice, which is hosted at the Brokamis Campus. The Brokamis Campus Operations Lead provided an overview of current capacity and availability. The Brokamis Campus has capacity for 390 seats, of which 84% are currently occupied, leaving room for additional deployment needs. Access control for the facility is managed through the access badge series the same reference, which was reviewed for security compliance and access permissions.

3. **Resource Allocation and Logistics:**  
   The discussion turned to logistics for upcoming hardware and software deployment at the Brokamis Campus. The Finance Representative noted that while the overall budget for the Vonnusett Lattice remains stable, any anticipated increases in seat allocation or infrastructure usage should be communicated promptly. The Brokamis Campus Operations Lead confirmed that no immediate constraints exist on capacity or access, and that the team is prepared to support scaling efforts as needed.

4. **Risk and Contingency Planning:**  
   The Site Reliability Lead, Nosos Nikeis, raised the topic of risk mitigation and contingency planning for both Project Renbomen and the Vonnusett Lattice. She suggested a coordinated review of backup systems and disaster recovery protocols, particularly given the critical role of the Brokamis Campus in hosting the Vonnusett Lattice. The Finance Representative agreed to support the proposed review and requested an estimate of potential costs for any additional system redundancies.

5. **Cross-Team Coordination:**  
   The team discussed the need for stronger coordination between the operations and finance departments to ensure that infrastructure planning and budgetary constraints remain aligned. A suggestion was made to schedule a quarterly alignment session to address these concerns proactively. The Brokamis Campus Operations Lead expressed willingness to lead that effort.

**Decisions:**

- The status of Project Renbomen is confirmed as on track, and no immediate adjustments to the timeline or budget are required.
- The Brokamis Campus is confirmed to have sufficient capacity and access control to support current and anticipated needs for the Vonnusett Lattice.
- A review of disaster recovery protocols for the Vonnusett Lattice will be conducted, with a report to be provided by the Site Reliability Lead within the next two weeks.
- A quarterly coordination session between operations and finance will be scheduled to begin in the next fiscal quarter.
