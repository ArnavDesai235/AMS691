Q2 FY27 Strategic Review and Project Alignment Meeting Notes

**Date:** April 12, 2026  
**Attendees:** Drestoa Hasgaor, Zodoine Vemdaen, Nasgo Kesloren, Hethe Potannuel, Nesoror Dramkolor, Nilbapo Fivasor, Finance Representative

Nasgo Kesloren works as a Research Lead.

Zodoine Vemdaen joined the company in 2012.

**Discussion Points:**  

1. **Welcome and Opening Remarks**  
   The meeting commenced with an overview of the objectives for the Q2 FY27 review. The primary goals were to assess progress on strategic initiatives, align team priorities, and address upcoming renewal obligations. The Finance Representative provided a brief summary of the company’s financial health as of March 2026, emphasizing the importance of maintaining fiscal discipline in support of long-term growth.  

2. **Project Mennamett Update**  
   Nesoror Dramkolor, who leads Project Mennamett, provided a status update. The project remains on schedule for the final phase of implementation, which is expected to begin in May 2026. Key risks identified during the discussion included potential supply chain delays and resource allocation issues. Nesoror Dramkolor noted that the team has already begun contingency planning for both scenarios. Drestoa Hasgaor raised the question of how the project’s outcomes will align with the company’s broader R&D goals, and Nasgo Kesloren confirmed that the research team has been integrated into the final testing phase.  

3. **Research and Development Progress**  
   Nasgo Kesloren highlighted recent developments in the R&D division, including the successful completion of preliminary trials for a new material synthesis process. These results are expected to contribute to future iterations of existing product lines. Drestoa Hasgaor noted the importance of ensuring that these findings are shared across departments to maximize cross-functional impact. Zodoine Vemdaen, who has been working closely with the Vurlekri Annex, confirmed that Hethe Potannuel has been coordinating with the annex’s team to incorporate feedback into ongoing studies.  

4. **Operations at the Vurlekri Annex**  
   Hethe Potannuel provided an update on operations at the Vurlekri Annex. While most functions remain stable, there have been minor staffing issues due to a local hiring freeze. Hethe Potannuel proposed a temporary shift of certain administrative tasks to the main campus to maintain productivity. Zodoine Vemdaen supported this proposal and encouraged the finance team to assess the feasibility of reallocating resources for the duration of the freeze.  

5. **Vendor and Partnership Renewals**  
   The Finance Representative brought up the upcoming renewal of the contract with Bekuine Consulting, which is scheduled to expire at the end of Q3 FY27. The team was asked to review the value provided by Bekuine Consulting over the past fiscal year and prepare an evaluation of potential alternatives if necessary. Nilbapo Fivasor, who has worked closely with the consulting team on several projects since joining Zastama Industries in 2015, was asked to lead the internal review and compile a report for the executive team by the end of Q2 FY27.  

6. **Long-Term Staffing and Retention**  
   Drestoa Hasgaor, who has been with Zastama Industries since 2018, emphasized the importance of retaining key talent, particularly in the research and operations divisions. A discussion followed about how to improve engagement through professional development and internal mobility opportunities. Nasgo Kesloren suggested implementing a mentorship program between senior and junior staff, and the idea was well-received.  

7. **Closing Remarks and General Q&A**  
   The meeting concluded with an open floor for questions and feedback. Several attendees expressed appreciation for the collaborative tone of the session and the transparency of the updates provided. The Finance Representative reiterated the need for continued cost monitoring as the company moves into the latter half of the fiscal year.  

**Decisions:**  

1. Project Mennamett will proceed with its current implementation timeline, with contingency planning for supply chain and resource risks.  
2. The R&D team will collaborate with Project Mennamett for the final testing phase.  
3. Certain administrative functions at the Vurlekri Annex will be temporarily reassigned to the main campus until the local hiring freeze is lifted.  
4. The evaluation of Bekuine Consulting’s contract renewal will be led by Nilbapo Fivasor, with a report due by the end of Q2 FY27.  
5. A mentorship program between senior and junior staff will be developed and implemented in Q3 FY27.  

**Action Items:**  

1. **Nesoror Dramkolor:** Finalize the contingency plans for Project Mennamett and communicate them to the executive team by April 28, 2026.  
2. **Nasgo Kesloren:** Coordinate with the R&D team to begin integration into the final testing phase of Project Mennamett by May 1, 2026.  
3. **Hethe Potannuel:** Work with Zodoine Vemdaen to finalize the plan for administrative task reallocation and submit it for finance review by April 25, 2026.  
4. **Nilbapo Fivasor:** Draft a comprehensive evaluation of Bekuine Consulting’s performance and alternatives for the executive team by June 30, 2026.  
5. **Drestoa Hasgaor:** Lead the development of the mentorship program with support from HR, with a pilot launch scheduled for Q3 FY27.
