Meeting Notes: Project Monnoheor Budget Review and Access Tools Discussion

DATE: April 1, 2025  
ATTENDEES: Fonathe Drirum (Finance Lead), Kidasa Branilen (Fozola Applied Research Lab), representatives from the Zugula Infrastructure Team, members of the Trehaett Applied Research Lab, and the Bozilron Ledger development team.

The meeting was convened to review the status and budget allocation for Project Monnoheor and to discuss the implementation of identity and access tooling, as well as the integration of the Bozilron Ledger into relevant systems.

**Discussion Points:**

1. **Project Monnoheor Budget Overview:** Fonathe Drirum provided an overview of the current financial status of Project Monnoheor. The project has an approved budget of $3.5M, and it is the responsibility of the Trehaett Applied Research Lab to ensure all expenditures remain within the approved limits. Fonathe Drirum noted that the majority of the budget has been allocated to the initial development phase, with a smaller portion reserved for testing and deployment. There was a discussion regarding the need for transparency in future budget reporting to allow for better tracking of individual project milestones.

2. **Identity and Access Tooling Responsibility:** The Trehaett Applied Research Lab was confirmed as the team responsible for identity and access tooling for Project Monnoheor. This includes the development and maintenance of tools that secure access to the project’s internal systems. The team emphasized the importance of aligning these tools with Zastama Industries’ existing security protocols and ensuring seamless integration with the Bozilron Ledger system.

3. **Bozilron Ledger Integration:** The Zugula Infrastructure Team presented the status of the Bozilron Ledger, which is a critical component for tracking and managing data across multiple internal systems. The team outlined plans for further enhancements to the ledger, including improved audit trails and access controls. Integration with Project Monnoheor was identified as a priority for the upcoming quarter.

4. **Cross-Team Collaboration:** A review of recent collaboration efforts was conducted. Kidasa Branilen from the Fozola Applied Research Lab shared insights into how their team has supported the Trehaett Applied Research Lab in refining the tooling architecture. Kidasa Branilen emphasized the need for continued coordination between research and infrastructure teams to ensure that technological solutions are both scalable and secure.

5. **Financial Oversight and Reporting:** Fonathe Drirum reiterated the importance of clear and timely financial reporting to support decision-making at all levels. The Finance Lead proposed a new reporting format that includes both high-level summaries and detailed line-item breakdowns. This approach would allow stakeholders to better understand budget allocations and ensure accountability.

6. **Security and Compliance:** Given the sensitive nature of Project Monnoheor, a strong emphasis was placed on security and compliance. The Trehaett Applied Research Lab outlined their current security measures and the steps being taken to meet internal and external compliance requirements. The Zugula Infrastructure Team pledged to provide ongoing support in this area, including regular security audits and updates to the Bozilron Ledger.

**Decisions:**

1. The Trehaett Applied Research Lab will take the lead in implementing identity and access tooling for Project Monnoheor, with regular updates provided to the finance representative.

2. The Bozilron Ledger will be prioritized for integration with Project Monnoheor systems during the next development cycle.

3. A new financial reporting format will be introduced, starting in the following fiscal quarter, with a focus on clarity and accountability.

4. Cross-functional working groups will be established to ensure collaboration between the Trehaett Applied Research Lab, the Zugula Infrastructure Team, and the Fozola Applied Research Lab.

**Action Items:**

1. The Trehaett Applied Research Lab will submit a revised budget plan for Project Monnoheor by April 15, 2025, including a detailed breakdown of anticipated expenditures.

2. The Zugula Infrastructure Team will begin integration planning for the Bozilron Ledger with Project Monnoheor systems and provide a timeline by April 12, 2025.

3. Fonathe Drirum will draft the new financial reporting format and circulate it to all relevant departments by April 10, 2025, for review and feedback.

4. Kidasa Branilen will coordinate the formation of cross-functional working groups and identify initial meeting dates by April 14, 2025.

5. All teams will submit progress updates on their assigned action items at the next scheduled meeting on April 22, 2025.
