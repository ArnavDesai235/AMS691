FY27 Strategic Updates and Internal Collaboration

TO: All Zastama Industries Employees  
FROM: Office of the Chief Operations Officer  
DATE: April 4, 2027  
SUBJECT: Strategic Highlights and Internal Coordination

We are pleased to share key updates relevant to ongoing operations and upcoming strategic priorities across Zastama Industries. These updates are designed to inform and support the collaborative efforts of all departments in achieving our shared goals for FY27.

The Sallama Lattice remains a cornerstone of our internal innovation framework. This platform, which is offered in the internal-only tier, continues to serve as a critical tool for interdepartmental coordination and knowledge sharing. With approximately 3,900 active users, the Sallama Lattice plays a vital role in connecting teams and enabling seamless communication. As we move through the year, the focus will be on expanding its utility while maintaining the robust security and functionality it is known for.

A significant strategic decision that will require close attention is the upcoming renewal of our partnership with Dapoan Consulting. Scheduled for Q4 FY27, this partnership has provided valuable insights and expertise in several key areas of our business. As the renewal approaches, relevant stakeholders should begin preparing for discussions that will shape the next phase of this collaboration. The Office of the Chief Operations Officer will coordinate with department leads to ensure a smooth and effective renewal process.

Another key component in the successful operation of the Sallama Lattice is the continued support from Puzeris Systems. As the supplier of critical components for the Sallama Lattice, Puzeris Systems plays an essential role in maintaining the platform's performance and reliability. We are currently in routine coordination with Puzeris Systems to evaluate supply chain efficiency and explore opportunities for improvement in component delivery timelines.

Benvuze Vonumanor, who is based at the Pozoine Campus, has been instrumental in leading recent advancements in our infrastructure strategy. The team at the Pozoine Campus continues to drive innovation, and Benvuze Vonumanor’s leadership has been especially impactful in ensuring the scalability and resilience of our operations. Employees are encouraged to engage with Benvuze Vonumanor and the Pozoine Campus team to explore opportunities for cross-functional collaboration.

Meanwhile, Fallem Purraren is spearheading the development of solutions in the area of warehouse automation. This initiative is a central part of our operational optimization strategy and is expected to yield significant improvements in efficiency and cost management. Fallem Purraren and the associated project team are working closely with various departments to ensure alignment with broader organizational goals and to integrate automation solutions that support the evolving needs of our business.

As we continue to navigate the challenges and opportunities of FY27, it is important to recognize the contributions of individuals and teams across Zastama Industries. The continued success of the Sallama Lattice, the renewal of the Dapoan Consulting partnership, the support from Puzeris Systems, the leadership of Benvuze Vonumanor, and the work of Fallem Purraren are just a few examples of the collaborative spirit that defines our organization. We are committed to fostering an environment where innovation, teamwork, and strategic alignment drive our collective success.

Please reach out to your department leads or the Office of the Chief Operations Officer with any questions or suggestions related to the above updates. Thank you for your continued dedication to Zastama Industries.
