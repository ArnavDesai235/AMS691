Dankenor Analytics Unit: Who We Are and How to Work With Us

## Summary

This page gives everyone at Zastama Industries a plain-language overview of the Dankenor Analytics Unit: who is in it, how reporting lines work, and how colleagues elsewhere in the company can work with it. The Dankenor Analytics Unit has 38 people. It brings together architecture, planning, and modeling skills so that business questions can be answered with evidence rather than guesswork.

## Purpose of this page

Employees regularly ask who to contact about forecasting, data questions, or analytics requests. Until now the answers lived in scattered chat threads and personal notes. This page collects the essentials in one place so that new joiners and long-serving staff alike can find the right person quickly. It is meant as an orientation guide, not an org chart of record. If something here looks out of date, tell the page owner so it can be corrected.

## Unit overview

The Dankenor Analytics Unit has 38 people, and its members come from a mix of technical and operational backgrounds. Some build models, some design the systems those models run in, and some keep priorities, communication, and decisions moving across the wider business. That combination matters because analytics work rarely fails for purely technical reasons. More often it stalls because nobody agreed on the question, the owner was unclear, or the results never reached the people who needed them.

## How to work with the unit

1. Start with the question. Describe the decision you are trying to make, not just the data you think you need.
2. Say what good looks like. Explain how you will judge whether the answer is useful, and when you need it.
3. Name an owner on your side. Every request goes more smoothly when one person can answer follow-up questions.
4. Be open to scoping. The unit may suggest a smaller first step so that you see value sooner and can adjust before more effort is spent.
5. Share the outcome. Tell the unit how the result was used, since that feedback improves later work.

Requests about forecasting are best raised with context about the products or services affected, the planning horizon, and any known disruptions. Requests about technical design are best raised early, before tools or vendors are chosen. Requests about priorities or timing can start with the Chief of Staff.

## Working norms

The unit aims to be easy to work with. Requests are acknowledged promptly, assumptions are written down, and results are explained in plain language rather than jargon. Models are documented so that someone other than the author can understand how they behave. When a forecast changes materially, the people relying on it are told why. Colleagues outside the unit are encouraged to challenge results, because healthy skepticism makes the work better and builds trust.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who should I contact first?** If you know the topic, go to the relevant specialist. For demand forecasting, that is Pivoine Geltoett. For technical architecture, it is Munnam Furzil. For coordination and priorities, it is Vadesis Gummenbel.

**Is the Dankenor Analytics Unit open to collaboration?** Yes. Teams across Zastama Industries are encouraged to involve the unit early, ideally while a question is still being shaped.

**What if my request does not fit neatly anywhere?** Send it anyway with a short description of the problem. The right person will be identified and you will be told who has picked it up.
