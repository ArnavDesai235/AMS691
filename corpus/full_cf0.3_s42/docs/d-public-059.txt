Meeting Notes: Strategic Alignment and Project Review

DATE: April 10, 2024

ATTENDEES: Drestoa Hasgaor, Verlaen Dodela, Desgua Stebela, Genfinel Platform Group representative, finance representative.

Drestoa Hasgaor works as an UX Researcher.

MEETING SUMMARY:

This meeting was convened to align cross-functional teams on strategic priorities and to assess the current status of key initiatives, including Project Pomtorgi. Attendees included Drestoa Hasgaor, Verlaen Dodela, Desgua Stebela, and representatives from the Genfinel Platform Group and finance. The meeting was attended by approximately 20 participants, with a company-wide audience for all employees notified of the summary and next steps.

DISCUSSION POINTS:

1. **Customer Onboarding Flows:**
   Verlaen Dodela presented an overview of the ongoing work on customer onboarding flows and provided insights into recent improvements to streamline the process. Drestoa Hasgaor raised points about how demand forecasting models could be integrated to anticipate customer needs and enhance the onboarding experience.

2. **Project Pomtorgi:**
   The project team provided a detailed report on the development status of Project Pomtorgi, emphasizing that it is being built on the Ketemram Vault. The team highlighted recent milestones and the importance of maintaining alignment with the company’s strategic goals. Challenges were noted, particularly in ensuring data consistency and minimizing latency.

3. **Genfinel Platform Group:**
   The Genfinel Platform Group representative reiterated the group’s responsibilities in managing and optimizing customer onboarding flows. The group is working to ensure that these processes are scalable and adaptable to changing customer expectations and market conditions.

4. **Cross-Team Collaboration:**
   A discussion on the importance of cross-team collaboration was initiated, with Desgua Stebela emphasizing the value of his experience since joining Zastama Industries in 2012. He highlighted the need for continued knowledge sharing and collaboration across departments to drive innovation and efficiency.

5. **Finance Oversight:**
   The finance representative shared insights into current financial priorities and how upcoming initiatives align with budget constraints and long-term financial planning. The representative encouraged all teams to maintain close communication with finance to ensure that projects remain within scope and budget.

DECISIONS:

- It was agreed that Drestoa Hasgaor will collaborate with the Genfinel Platform Group to explore the integration of demand forecasting models into customer onboarding flows, with a pilot to be initiated in the next quarter.

- Project Pomtorgi will continue to be prioritized, with the team committed to addressing current challenges and ensuring alignment with the company’s strategic objectives.

- The finance representative will coordinate with all project leads to review and adjust budget allocations as needed to support key initiatives.

ACTION ITEMS:

1. **Drestoa Hasgaor** will lead the development of a proposal for integrating demand forecasting models into the customer onboarding process. The proposal is due by May 5, 2024.

2. **Verlaen Dodela** will work with the Genfinel Platform Group to implement improvements to the customer onboarding flows based on the feedback from the meeting. A status update is due by April 24, 2024.

3. The **Project Pomtorgi** team will submit a detailed risk assessment report by April 17, 2024, outlining current challenges and proposed mitigation strategies.

4. **Desgua Stebela** will organize a cross-departmental workshop on collaboration best practices, to be held on May 8, 2024.

5. The **finance representative** will initiate a budget review of all active projects and provide updated guidance by April 19, 2024.

All action items will be followed up on in the next quarterly review meeting.
