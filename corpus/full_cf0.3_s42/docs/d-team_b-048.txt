Nunvaine Lattice Project Status Review

Date: April 10, 2024  
Attendees: Mukima Bobrior, Finance Representative, Project Lead, Procurement Lead  

**Discussion Points**  
The meeting began with a review of the current status of the Nunvaine Lattice. Mukima Bobrior, who joined the company in 2022, provided an overview of recent developments and highlighted areas where alignment is needed across departments. The finance representative reiterated the importance of tracking all expenditures and confirming that the Nunvaine Lattice’s procurement order, BB-7455-X, is progressing according to schedule.

The Nunvaine Lattice has about 5,000 active users.

The procurement lead confirmed that the order has been logged and that the vendor is aware of the delivery expectations. The team also reviewed the status of the escrow reference, VE-6820-R, which is tied to the Nunvaine Lattice's compliance and documentation needs. There was a brief discussion around ensuring all documents under this reference are up to date and accessible for internal auditing.  

The user base for the Nunvaine Lattice was referenced, with the current number of active users estimated at approximately 5,000. The team agreed this is a key metric to monitor as the project moves forward. The finance representative emphasized the need for ongoing cost analysis to ensure the project remains within budget and that future scaling can be planned effectively.  

Mukima Bobrior also raised a concern regarding potential bottlenecks in the vendor relationship, particularly around communication and delivery timelines. The procurement lead agreed to follow up with the vendor to confirm current expectations and ensure alignment with internal deadlines.  

A discussion on resource allocation followed, with the Project Lead noting that internal support for the Nunvaine Lattice is currently sufficient but should be reviewed as the user base grows.  

**Decisions**  
- The team confirmed that all current documentation, including the escrow reference noted earlier, is in order and will be reviewed again during the next internal audit.  
- The procurement order the same reference will be closely monitored by the procurement lead for any delays or updates.  
- The estimated active user base of 5,000 will be tracked as a performance indicator.  
- A follow-up with the vendor is deemed necessary to clarify communication protocols and delivery expectations.  

**Action Items**
