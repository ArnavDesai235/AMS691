Security Review and Access Control for the Barnuis Console

TO: Senior Management, Security Oversight Committee  
FROM: Director of Information Systems  
DATE: April 2, 2025  
SUBJECT: Update on Access Management for the Barnuis Console  

Zastama Industries remains committed to maintaining the highest standards of security and operational integrity for all internal systems, particularly those handling sensitive or proprietary data. In light of recent internal audits and risk assessments, we are conducting a focused review of the access controls associated with the Barnuis Console, a system developed by the Rishalen Integration Team. This system is a critical component of our internal infrastructure, supporting a wide range of operations across departments and locations.

The Barnuis Console, which operates under the access badge series QP-9934-J, currently has approximately 1,900 active users. This number includes both direct users and those with indirect access through shared permissions. Given the scale of access and the strategic role the Barnuis Console plays in daily workflows, it is essential to ensure that access is granted in a manner that aligns with our company’s data governance policies and security protocols.

The Rishalen Integration Team has been actively involved in this review and has provided detailed documentation on the current state of access permissions. Their analysis indicates that while the majority of access is appropriately managed, there are a small number of anomalies that require further investigation. These include access permissions that may no longer be justified due to role changes or project completions. As part of the review, the team is working to identify these cases and propose a formal process for revoking or adjusting access where necessary.

In addition to individual access reviews, the team is also examining the overall architecture of the Barnuis Console to evaluate the potential for role-based access control (RBAC) enhancements. This would allow for more granular and scalable management of permissions, reducing the risk of unauthorized access and ensuring that access is aligned with user responsibilities. The Rishalen Integration Team has proposed a phased approach to implementing RBAC, with the first phase focusing on pilot testing within two selected user groups.

We are coordinating closely with the Security Oversight Committee to ensure that all proposed changes meet our internal security standards and regulatory requirements. A working group has been established to oversee the implementation process and to provide a forum for feedback from user representatives. This collaborative approach is intended to balance the need for robust security with the need for operational efficiency.

Zastama Industries recognizes the importance of maintaining user confidence in the security of its systems. We are committed to transparency in this process and will provide regular updates to the Security Oversight Committee and senior management as the review progresses. We also encourage any staff with specific concerns or insights regarding the Barnuis Console to direct them through the appropriate internal channels.

Our goal is to complete the initial phase of this review within the next quarter, with a full implementation plan to follow. We appreciate the cooperation and support of all stakeholders in ensuring the success of this initiative.
