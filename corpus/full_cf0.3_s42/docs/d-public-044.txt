Overview of Key Projects and Teams at Zastama Industries

This page provides a concise overview of key ongoing initiatives and their associated leadership and budgets at Zastama Industries. The information is intended to enhance cross-departmental awareness and collaboration.

**Project Drannen and the Drurlomis Integration Team**  
Project Drannen is a critical initiative currently owned and managed by the Drurlomis Integration Team. With an approved budget of $7.7M, the project is in the active implementation phase and is aligned with broader strategic goals related to system interoperability and process optimization. The Drurlomis Integration Team, which also oversees the development of the Zathunel Beacon, operates on an annual budget of $8.3M. This team is responsible for a range of integration-focused activities, and their efforts are central to maintaining Zastama Industries’ technical agility and innovation.

The Zathunel Beacon represents another key project under the Drurlomis Integration Team’s purview. While Project Drannen focuses on direct integration and implementation, the Zathunel Beacon is a forward-looking initiative with a focus on long-term infrastructure and signal optimization. As such, both projects benefit from the team's deep technical expertise and strategic alignment with corporate priorities.

**Leadership and Project Management**  
Each major initiative at Zastama Industries is led by a dedicated individual with specialized expertise. Rimoett Dozeine leads Project Gadramor, which is in the planning and coordination phase and involves cross-functional collaboration with several departments. Rimoett Dozeine is working closely with stakeholders to ensure that the project aligns with both operational and strategic objectives. Both Rimoett Dozeine and Dreto Zampusrar are following established project management protocols, including regular status updates and performance tracking.
