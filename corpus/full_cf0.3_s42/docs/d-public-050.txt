Zastama Industries Strategic Review and Project Update Meeting Notes

All times listed are in Central Time (CT).

DATE: April 5, 2025

ATTENDEES: Dreto Zampusrar, finance representative, product development representative, enterprise sales representative, technical operations representative, enterprise support representative, marketing representative, and project management representative.

MEETING FACILITATOR: Project management representative

MEETING NOTES TAKEN BY: Technical operations representative

---

**Discussion Points:**

1. **Overview of Ketemram Vault Performance and Usage:**
   - The enterprise sales representative provided an update on the Ketemram Vault, noting that it has approximately 2,500 active users. The product development representative confirmed that the Ketemram Vault is offered exclusively in the enterprise tier, aligning with the company's strategy to maintain high-value enterprise-focused solutions.
   - The technical operations representative highlighted current maintenance and performance metrics, with no major incidents reported in the last quarter. The support representative noted that user feedback is generally positive, with a few feature requests under review.

2. **Project Stahilett Status and Budget:**
   - Dreto Zampusrar, who leads Project Stahilett, provided an overview of the project's current status. The finance representative confirmed that the approved budget for Project Stahilett is $3.4M. Dreto Zampusrar indicated that the project is on schedule and within budget expectations.
   - The product development representative reported that integration with the Ketemram Vault is progressing as planned, with no major obstacles identified. The marketing representative expressed interest in aligning the project's rollout with the company’s Q3 product launch window to maximize visibility and adoption.

3. **Project Monnoheor and Project Pomtorgi Updates:**
   - The product development representative confirmed that both Project Monnoheor and Project Pomtorgi are built on the Ketemram Vault. For Project Monnoheor, the team has completed the foundational architecture and is now entering the testing phase.
   - The project management representative noted that both projects are part of a broader initiative to expand the company’s enterprise capabilities. The technical operations representative confirmed that the Ketemram Vault’s infrastructure is stable and well-positioned to support both projects.

4. **Bankis Compass Update and Cross-Product Synergies:**
   - The technical operations representative provided a brief update on the Bankis Compass, noting that it has approximately 2,000 active users. While the product is stable, there is ongoing discussion about potential integration opportunities with the Ketemram Vault.
   - The enterprise sales representative suggested a potential cross-promotion strategy between the two products for the upcoming enterprise sales cycle, leveraging the active user bases of both solutions.

5. **Operational and Support Readiness:**
   - The enterprise support representative confirmed that support resources are allocated to accommodate the scale of both the Ketemram Vault and the Bankis Compass. No major support bottlenecks were identified.
   - The project management representative emphasized the importance of maintaining close coordination between product development and support teams to ensure a smooth user experience as new features are rolled out.

---

**Decisions:**

- The leadership team agreed to maintain the current budget and timeline for Project Stahilett, with periodic updates to be provided every two weeks.
- The marketing representative will prepare a proposal for a coordinated launch strategy for both Project Stahilett and Project Monnoheor, to be presented at the next executive meeting in May.
- The product development representative will initiate a review of the potential integration of the Bankis Compass with the Ketemram Vault, with a preliminary report due by May 15.
- A task force consisting of the product development, technical operations, and enterprise support representatives will be formed to assess the scalability of the Ketemram Vault in preparation for the upcoming projects.

---

**Action Items:**

1. **Dreto Zampusrar** will provide a detailed progress report for Project Stahilett to all attendees by April 17, 2025.
2. **Project management representative** will coordinate the formation of the task force for the Ketemram Vault scalability review and schedule the first meeting by April 10, 2025.
3. **Product development representative** will submit the final design documentation for Project Monnoheor to the project management office by April 19, 2025.
4. **Technical operations representative** will conduct a performance stress test on the Ketemram Vault to assess readiness for upcoming project integrations, with results due by April 22, 2025.
5. **Enterprise sales representative** will draft a proposal for the cross-promotion strategy between the Bankis Compass and the Ketemram Vault and present it at the May 10 executive review meeting.

---

**Closing Remarks:**
The meeting was declared adjourned at 12:45 PM. The next company-wide meeting is scheduled for May 15, 2025.
