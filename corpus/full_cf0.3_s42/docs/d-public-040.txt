Internal Update on Strategic Projects and Research Collaborations

---

**From:** Chief Research Officer  
**To:** All Employees  
**Subject:** Overview of Key Research Projects and Timelines  
**Date:** October 15, FY25  

Dear Team,

As we continue to advance Zastama Industries' innovative goals, I wanted to provide a brief overview of some of our key projects and the research foundations they rely on. 

Project Thibelan is currently in active development and is built on the Sallama Lattice, a groundbreaking computational model developed by the Trehaett Applied Research Lab. Our team is working closely with the Trehaett Applied Research Lab to ensure that the Sallama Lattice provides the foundational support necessary for Project Thibelan's success. Project Thibelan remains on track for completion in Q1 FY26.

The Sallama Lattice itself is a critical milestone in our applied research efforts, with a planned launch in Q3 FY26. The Trehaett Applied Research Lab has been instrumental in pushing the boundaries of what this model can achieve, and we are grateful for their ongoing collaboration.

Additionally, I want to highlight Project Daslalett, which is set to be completed by Q1 FY27. This project will build on the insights and technologies developed across our other initiatives and will represent a major step forward in our long-term strategy.

As always, I encourage each of you to stay informed about the broader context in which your work contributes. If you have questions or would like more details about any of these projects, please reach out to your respective team leads.

Thank you for your continued dedication and hard work.

Best regards,  
Chief Research Officer

**From:** Senior Project Manager  
**To:** All Employees  
**Subject:** Project Status and Team Coordination  
**Date:** October 19, FY25  

Following the recent updates from the Chief Research Officer and the Logistics Manager, I want to add a few notes regarding team coordination and next steps across our major initiatives.

Project Thibelan continues to progress as planned. The development team is working closely with the Trehaett Applied Research Lab to ensure that the Sallama Lattice is fully integrated and optimized for the project's needs. We are currently in the final stages of testing and are preparing for the Q1 FY26 completion deadline.

With regard to the Sallama Lattice, the Trehaett Applied Research Lab has confirmed that all preliminary benchmarks are being met, and the model is on schedule for its Q3 FY26 launch. Cross-functional teams have been assigned to assist in the final validation and documentation required for the upcoming release.

For Project Daslalett, we are in the early stages of development, and planning is well underway to meet the Q1 FY27 completion target. We are aligning timelines and resources to ensure that this project benefits from the insights and technologies developed in Project Thibelan and the Sallama Lattice.

The delivery of equipment from Brimumor Labs to the Lelosis Field Office is a critical step in supporting field operations. Once installed, this equipment will enable the Lelosis Field Office to conduct additional testing and support our broader research goals.

If you are involved in any of these projects or have questions about their status, please reach out to your team leads. Continued coordination and communication are essential to our success.

Thank you for your collaboration and support.

Best regards,  
Senior Project Manager
