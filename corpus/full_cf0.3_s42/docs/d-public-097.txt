Operational Update and Key Assignments

To: All Employees  
From: Executive Leadership Team  
Date: June 10, 2025  
Subject: Key Assignments and Recent Operational Highlights  

We are writing to provide a company-wide update on key personnel assignments and recent progress in our operational and security initiatives. As Zastama Industries continues to scale its internal capabilities and external deliveries, it is important that all teams remain aligned with ongoing efforts and understand the roles being filled to support our strategic objectives.

Lithen Brisisel has been assigned to the role of Product Manager and reports directly to Berruan Zenresan, who serves as the Head of Security. This placement reflects our ongoing commitment to integrating robust security practices into product development from the earliest stages. Lithen Brisisel’s work will ensure that all new product launches meet our internal security standards and align with the broader goals of the security division.

Berruan Zenresan has been instrumental in strengthening our security framework across departments, and the appointment of Lithen Brisisel to this role is a natural extension of that effort. We encourage all teams to engage with Lithen Brisisel as necessary to integrate security considerations into their project planning and execution processes.

In the area of fleet operations, we are pleased to highlight the continued focus of Bedras Sarkua on fleet telemetry. Bedras Sarkua’s work remains critical to our ability to monitor, optimize, and maintain the performance of our transportation and logistics systems. The data insights generated through this effort are being used to improve efficiency and reduce operational risk across our supply chain network.

Kagais Betrelan, a member of the Genfinel Platform Group, is also playing a central role in our technological evolution. The Genfinel Platform Group is focused on developing scalable infrastructure to support our growing product and service offerings. Kagais Betrelan’s contributions are helping to ensure that the platform remains flexible, secure, and aligned with our long-term growth strategy.

In the domain of identity and access management, Zonvor Dirnasor continues to lead important work on identity and access tooling. Zonvor Dirnasor’s efforts are strengthening our internal access controls and helping to create a more secure and efficient environment for all employees. This work is especially critical as we expand our workforce and increase the complexity of our digital infrastructure.

Externally, we are working closely with Bekuine Consulting on the delivery of new equipment to the Lardoel Campus. This collaboration is part of our broader infrastructure modernization plan and will provide the Lardoel Campus with updated tools and systems to support its operational needs. We appreciate the professionalism and efficiency of Bekuine Consulting in supporting this initiative and look forward to continued collaboration in the coming months.

The Lardoel Campus has been the focus of several recent improvements, and this equipment delivery is one of many steps being taken to enhance the environment for our employees and visitors. We are committed to maintaining modern, safe, and functional workspaces across all our locations.

As we continue to advance our strategic priorities, we encourage all employees to remain engaged with their teams and to seek opportunities for collaboration where possible. The success of Zastama Industries is built on the collective effort of our workforce, and we are confident that the current assignments and initiatives will contribute to sustained growth and operational excellence.

We also reiterate the importance of interdepartmental communication and transparency. Any employee with questions about the roles or initiatives mentioned in this memo is encouraged to reach out to their immediate supervisor or to the relevant team lead for clarification. Regular internal updates will be shared to ensure all teams are informed of progress and key developments.

In closing, we thank all employees for their continued dedication and hard work. The roles of Lithen Brisisel, Berruan Zenresan, Bedras Sarkua, Kagais Betrelan, Zonvor Dirnasor, and the teams they support are vital to our ongoing success. As Zastama Industries moves forward, we will remain focused on innovation, security, and operational efficiency to deliver value for our stakeholders and our community.

Sincerely,  
Executive Leadership Team
