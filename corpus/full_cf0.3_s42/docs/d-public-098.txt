All-Company Meeting Notes: Strategic Review and Budget Overview

March 15, 2025

Attendees:  
- Kagais Betrelan (Genfinel Platform Group)  
- Gudrosa Ganema (Drurlomis Integration Team)  
- Kavemen Hilister (Chief of Staff)  
- Finance Representative  
- Operations Lead  
- HR Director  
- Department Heads from all locations  

The Genfinel Platform Group has 7 people.

Discussion Points:

1. Budget Review for the Genfinel Platform Group  
   The Genfinel Platform Group operates on an annual budget of $1.0M and has 7 members. Kagais Betrelan provided an overview of the group's current financial status and strategic goals for the remainder of the fiscal year. The group is based at the Lardoel Campus, and discussions focused on how to align their budget with long-term company goals. The finance representative highlighted the importance of maintaining fiscal responsibility while supporting innovative platform development. Kavemen Hilister noted that cross-functional coordination will be essential for maintaining efficiency and transparency.

2. Staffing and Resource Allocation  
   The Genfinel Platform Group’s size of 7 team members was discussed in relation to project scope and deadlines. While the group is operating within its budget, the team has requested a review of resource allocation to ensure that high-priority tasks are not delayed. Kavemen Hilister emphasized the importance of internal communication and recommended a mid-year reassessment of team structure and workflow. There was also a discussion on how to optimize the use of shared resources across departments without overburdening the Genfinel Platform Group.

3. Integration Efforts and Collaboration  
   Gudrosa Ganema provided an update on the progress of the Drurlomis Integration Team’s efforts and how these efforts intersect with the Genfinel Platform Group’s work. The discussion highlighted the need for closer collaboration between the two groups to ensure a seamless integration process. It was noted that the Drurlomis Integration Team and the Genfinel Platform Group, though focused on different objectives, share common infrastructure and workflows that could benefit from unified planning. Kavemen Hilister encouraged both teams to schedule joint planning sessions to identify overlapping needs and avoid duplication of effort.

4. Strategic Planning and Long-Term Vision  
   The Chief of Staff, Kavemen Hilister, led a session on long-term strategic planning for Zastama Industries. This included a review of how the Genfinel Platform Group and the Drurlomis Integration Team fit into the broader vision for the company. The goal is to ensure that all departments are aligned with the company’s strategic priorities while maintaining flexibility to adapt to changing market conditions. The discussion emphasized the importance of maintaining a clear line of sight between team objectives and company-wide goals.

5. Risk Management and Contingency Planning  
   A new discussion was introduced on the need for better risk management and contingency planning across all departments. Kavemen Hilister pointed out that while the Genfinel Platform Group and the Drurlomis Integration Team are performing well, external uncertainties such as vendor delays or resource shortages could impact timelines. The Operations Lead suggested the adoption of a standardized risk assessment process to be applied across departments. The HR Director added that staff readiness and training should be evaluated in parallel to ensure all teams can respond effectively to unforeseen challenges. It was agreed that this topic would be addressed in the upcoming strategic planning document.

Decisions:

1. The Genfinel Platform Group will submit a detailed mid-year review of their budget and staffing needs by April 15, 2025.  
2. The Drurlomis Integration Team and the Genfinel Platform Group will schedule a joint planning session by May 1, 2025, to coordinate their efforts and identify areas of synergy.  
3. A company-wide strategic planning document will be drafted by the Chief of Staff and circulated for feedback by April 30, 2025.

Action Items:

- Kagais Betrelan will coordinate with the finance representative to prepare and submit the Genfinel Platform Group’s mid-year budget and staffing review by the specified deadline.  
- Gudrosa Ganema will lead coordination efforts with the Genfinel Platform Group to schedule the joint planning session.  
- Kavemen Hilister will oversee the drafting of the strategic planning document and ensure that it reflects input from all relevant departments.

Next Steps:

- The next all-company meeting will be held on June 10, 2025, to review progress on the above decisions and action items.  
- Department leads will be responsible for ensuring that their teams are aligned with the strategic planning document and that any concerns or suggestions are included in the feedback process.
