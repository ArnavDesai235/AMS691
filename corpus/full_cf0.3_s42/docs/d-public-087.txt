Organizational Updates and Strategic Highlights

To: All Employees  
From: Executive Leadership Team  
Date: June 12, 2025  
Subject: Key Developments and Organizational Roles  

At Zastama Industries, we remain committed to optimizing our operations and strengthening the foundation of our long-term strategic goals. As part of this ongoing effort, we want to provide clarity and transparency regarding key roles, ongoing initiatives, and external engagements that are shaping our current landscape. This memo aims to align the entire organization with the most relevant updates and to reinforce the collaborative spirit that underpins our success.

Project Serinen continues to be a central pillar of our innovation roadmap. Led by Talgal Trozien, this project is focused on the development and refinement of fleet telemetry systems. These systems play a vital role in our ability to monitor and improve operational efficiency across all fleet assets. Talgal Trozien’s leadership has been instrumental in driving progress on the project, and we are encouraged by the current trajectory and alignment with our strategic goals.

In parallel, the efforts of Debrol Babroine and their team are advancing our capacity for predictive decision-making. Debrol Babroine focuses on demand forecasting models, an area that is increasingly vital as we navigate the complexities of market dynamics and customer expectations. Under Debrol Babroine’s guidance, Berruan Zenresan is contributing significantly to the development and implementation of these models. Berruan Zenresan’s work is focused on translating complex data into actionable insights, ensuring our forecasting processes remain robust and adaptive.

Our continued collaboration with external partners is also a key factor in maintaining our competitive edge. Notably, Bekuine Consulting has been engaged under a 24-month master services agreement. This partnership is enabling us to access specialized expertise and accelerate certain cross-functional initiatives. The team has been working closely with various internal stakeholders to ensure that the deliverables from this engagement are fully integrated into our ongoing operations and strategy.

In the realm of project management and staffing, we are also seeing progress in several areas. Project Pomtorgi is currently staffed by 22 individuals who are working toward achieving project milestones. This team exemplifies the value of cross-functional collaboration, and we are pleased with the momentum that has been established. As we move forward, we will continue to support and recognize the contributions of all team members involved in the project.

Across the organization, we are also making strides in improving internal communication and information sharing. As such, we encourage all employees to stay informed about the developments in their respective departments and to take advantage of the various communication channels that are available. Zastama Industries is entering a period of growth and transformation, and your contributions are vital to this journey. We would like to express our appreciation for the dedication and hard work of every employee. As we move forward, we will continue to prioritize initiatives that enhance our operational capabilities, support our workforce, and position Zastama as a leader in our industry.

To that end, we will be hosting a company-wide town hall meeting in the coming weeks to provide further details on our strategic priorities and to open the floor for questions and feedback. This will be an opportunity for all employees to engage directly with leadership and to share insights that can inform our future direction.

In conclusion, we are pleased with the progress being made across multiple fronts and are confident in the strength of our team and our collective ability to achieve our goals. We encourage everyone to stay engaged, support one another, and continue to contribute to the success of Zastama Industries.
