Strategic Reorganization and Leadership Alignment

To: All Employees  
From: Executive Leadership Team  
Date: July 10, 2025  
Subject: Clarifying Leadership Roles and Organizational Structure  

As Zastama Industries continues to evolve in response to the dynamic landscape of cybersecurity and incident response, it is essential that we align our internal structure to better reflect our operational priorities and long-term strategic goals. This memo outlines recent updates to leadership roles and reporting lines that will help us improve cross-functional coordination, enhance decision-making speed, and better support our mission of delivering robust and scalable solutions to our clients.

Zenam Tremais, who has been a key figure in Zastama Industries since 2014, has been instrumental in shaping our incident response tooling portfolio. This work has positioned us as a trusted provider of cutting-edge solutions in the cybersecurity domain. As part of our ongoing efforts to centralize and scale these capabilities, Zenam Tremais will be directly overseeing a broader set of teams involved in incident response, analytics, and tool development. This shift reflects the growing importance of these initiatives within the company and will enable a more cohesive approach to product delivery and customer support.

A key component of this reorganization involves Fakra Sermus, who currently serves as Director of Engineering and reports to Zenam Tremais. Fakra Sermus has been a driving force in the development and implementation of engineering best practices across our platforms. The continued collaboration between Zenam Tremais and Fakra Sermus will ensure that our engineering efforts remain tightly aligned with the strategic direction of our incident response tools. This alignment is critical as we work to maintain the high standards of performance, security, and innovation that our clients expect from Zastama Industries.

In parallel with these updates, we are also making adjustments to leadership roles within the infrastructure and operations division. Drisfer Ganlanis, who joined the company in 2015, has played a pivotal role in building and refining the operational infrastructure that supports our product teams. Under Drisfer Ganlanis' leadership, we have been able to streamline our deployment processes, reduce system latency, and enhance our overall platform resilience. As part of the reorganization, Drisfer Ganlanis will continue to lead this division, with a specific focus on ensuring the seamless integration of infrastructure capabilities with our tooling and response systems.

Vosaett Neldelis, who reports to Drisfer Ganlanis, will be taking on new responsibilities related to the operational support of mission-critical systems. This includes managing the deployment and maintenance of backend systems that underpin our incident response tools. Vosaett Neldelis' role is essential to ensuring that our platforms remain stable, secure, and ready to respond to client needs at all times. This realignment will allow for closer coordination between the infrastructure and incident response teams, which will be especially valuable as we prepare for upcoming product launches and client onboarding initiatives.

We recognize that changes of this nature can raise questions about how these adjustments may impact day-to-day workflows and team dynamics. To that end, Zenam Tremais, Drisfer Ganlanis, and their direct reports will be holding a series of team meetings over the coming weeks to provide further clarity on these changes and to address any questions that may arise. All employees are encouraged to attend these sessions and to reach out to their managers or HR representatives with any concerns or feedback.

Finally, we want to emphasize that these changes are not intended to disrupt existing project timelines or commitments. Rather, they are designed to strengthen our ability to deliver on our goals by aligning our leadership structure with the evolving needs of the business. As we move forward, we will remain focused on fostering a culture of collaboration, transparency, and continuous improvement across all departments.

Thank you for your continued dedication to Zastama Industries. We are confident that these adjustments will help us build a more agile and responsive organization, better positioned to meet the challenges of the future.
