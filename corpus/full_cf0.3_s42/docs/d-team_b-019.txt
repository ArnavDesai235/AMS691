Project Zasmemine Update and Access Protocol Clarifications

TO: Project Leads and Operations Managers  
FROM: Director of Project Execution  
DATE: April 2, 2025  
SUBJECT: Status of Project Zasmemine and Coordination with Brepemrir Logistics  

Project Zasmemine, which has an approved budget of $3.1M, remains on track for its second phase of implementation. The operations team has confirmed that all infrastructure components required for this phase have been secured, and the contract with Brepemrir Logistics is in active use to manage the necessary transport and storage arrangements. As part of our ongoing coordination with Brepemrir Logistics, the logistics lead and the security team have been working to align delivery schedules with the access protocols required for personnel and equipment to enter the secured work zones.

One of the key aspects of this alignment is the access badge series assigned to Project Zasmemine, which is BE-9245-C. All individuals authorized to work in the project’s secured environments must be issued a badge from this series. The security team has issued a reminder to all relevant personnel to ensure that their access credentials remain valid and are updated with the latest access matrix. Any discrepancies in badge status must be reported immediately to the security office to avoid operational delays.

In parallel, the resource planning team has been monitoring the staffing requirements for both Project Zasmemine and Project Drutoett. While Project Drutoett is staffed by 39 people, the team supporting Project Zasmemine has maintained a stable headcount and continues to operate within its allocated capacity. The finance lead has confirmed that the current expenditure aligns with the projected schedule and that no major reallocations are expected in the short term.

We are also reviewing the cross-project coordination protocols to ensure that shared resources, including warehouse space and transport assets, are efficiently managed. As part of this review, the logistics and project management teams are to submit a joint report by the end of this week outlining how the ongoing work with Brepemrir Logistics can be synchronized with Project Drutoett’s deployment timelines.

Lastly, I would like to emphasize the importance of maintaining clear communication channels between the teams involved in these projects. Given the complexity of the work being carried out, any changes to timelines, vendor delivery schedules, or internal capacity must be communicated promptly to the executive sponsor and the operations review board. This will help ensure that all stakeholders remain informed and that any potential bottlenecks are identified and addressed proactively.
