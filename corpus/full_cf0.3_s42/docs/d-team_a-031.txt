Thanfuan Applied Research Lab Procurement and Security Meeting Notes

**Date:** April 3, 2025  
**Attendees:** Nogem Lorgaett, Fofemis Balzirka, finance representative  

---

The meeting was convened to address concerns regarding the procurement process for the Thanfuan Applied Research Lab and to review security protocols, particularly as they relate to the lab’s operations and personnel. Attendees included Nogem Lorgaett (budget code ZP-3417-M), Fofemis Balzirka (Head of Security, joined the company in 2022), and a finance representative.  

**Discussion Points**  
The meeting began with a review of the Thanfuan Applied Research Lab’s ongoing procurement order, FJ-1478-C. Nogem Lorgaett provided an overview of the research objectives and emphasized the need for timely delivery of specialized materials. The finance representative confirmed that the budget under Nogem Lorgaett’s code, the same reference, had sufficient allocation to cover the remaining expenses for the order. However, they noted a delay in the approval of an invoice from a vendor, which could impact the timeline for material delivery.  

Fofemis Balzirka raised concerns about the security implications of the research being conducted by the Thanfuan Applied Research Lab. She highlighted the importance of controlled access to the lab and reiterated the current protocol for personnel verification. Nogem Lorgaett confirmed that all lab personnel have been cleared through the standard onboarding process and that access is currently restricted to authorized staff only.  

The Head of Security also addressed recent internal security audits, emphasizing that the Thanfuan Applied Research Lab is among the high-priority sites for monitoring. She proposed a review of the lab’s security plan to be conducted in the coming weeks, in coordination with the lab’s lead.  

**Decisions**  
1. The finance team will prioritize the resolution of the invoice delay for procurement order the same reference to prevent any further disruption to the project timeline.  
2. Fofemis Balzirka will coordinate with the Thanfuan Applied Research Lab to schedule a security review of the lab’s facilities and protocols. The review will be conducted by the end of the month.  
3. Nogem Lorgaett will provide a list of all personnel currently working in the Thanfuan Applied Research Lab to the security team for updated access verification.  

**Action Items**  
- The finance representative to follow up with the vendor regarding the invoice for procurement order the same reference.  
- Fofemis Balzirka to draft a revised security review plan for the Thanfuan Applied Research Lab and share it with Nogem Lorgaett by April 10.  
- Nogem Lorgaett to submit a personnel list for the lab to the security team by April 8, ensuring that all individuals listed are currently active and cleared for lab access.  
- A follow-up meeting will be scheduled for April 17 to review the status of the procurement order and the outcome of the security review.  

The meeting concluded with agreement that the Thanfuan Applied Research Lab’s ongoing work requires both financial and security alignment to proceed smoothly.
