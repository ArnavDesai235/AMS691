**From:** Holol Kareman  
**To:** All Employees  
**Subject:** Samopunel Beacon Update and Recognition of Long-Term Service  
**Date:** April 4, 2025  

Dear Colleagues,  

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to provide a brief update regarding the Samopunel Beacon, a project under active development by the Trehaett Applied Research Lab. As you may know, the project is offered in the standard tier and continues to represent a significant area of innovation for our company.

The Trehaett Applied Research Lab has 16 people.

The Trehaett Applied Research Lab, which consists of 16 dedicated members, including myself, is working closely with cross-functional teams to ensure the Samopunel Beacon remains aligned with our product roadmap and customer expectations. We appreciate the continued support and collaboration from all departments as we approach key milestones for the project.  

In addition, I would like to recognize a colleague who has contributed immensely to the success of various initiatives over the years. Dreto Zampusrar, who joined Zastama Industries in 2012, has recently reached a notable anniversary. Dreto Zampusrar’s steadfast commitment and expertise have been instrumental in shaping user experiences across our product lines, and we are grateful for their continued contributions.  

As we continue to refine the Samopunel Beacon, we are also investing in internal processes to ensure that our development cycles remain efficient and that we maintain a culture of transparency and communication. This includes regular check-ins with stakeholders, the use of collaborative tools to track progress, and an ongoing effort to incorporate feedback from both our team and our customers. These steps help us to remain agile and responsive to the evolving needs of our users and the market.  

Please join me in acknowledging Dreto Zampusrar’s service and the ongoing efforts of the Trehaett Applied Research Lab as we advance the Samopunel Beacon. If you have any questions or would like to provide feedback, I welcome your input.  

Best regards,  
Holol Kareman  
Trehaett Applied Research Lab
