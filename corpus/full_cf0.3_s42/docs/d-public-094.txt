**From:** Vosaett Neldelis

**Subject:** Garelor Relay Infrastructure Review and Next Steps  
**Date:** April 4, 2025  

The Garelor Relay is currently operating at optimal capacity, with approximately 5,800 active users supported by the Zoluna Data Center. As part of our ongoing infrastructure review, I am coordinating a cross-departmental assessment of the relay’s hosting environment. Please be advised that we will be forming a task force to evaluate the Zoluna Data Center’s operational readiness and its capacity to support both the Garelor Relay and potential new projects.  

Your continued focus on your departmental responsibilities will be critical. If your team is involved with the Garelor Relay, the Zoluna Data Center, or Project Statugoan, please prepare for a follow-up communication with specific next steps. Thank you for your attention to this ongoing initiative.
