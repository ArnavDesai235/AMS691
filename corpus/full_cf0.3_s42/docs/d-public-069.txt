Overview of Zastama Industries Research and Development Structure and Key Initiatives

Zastama Industries is organized to ensure focused innovation through its research labs and project leadership. This page provides a high-level overview of the current R&D structure, key personnel, and notable initiatives.

---

**Key Reporting Structure**

Zastama Industries maintains a clear chain of command within its research division to facilitate decision-making and accountability. Toperel Brirdoan is directly supervised by Hindas Brukruan. Hindas Brukruan, in turn, reports to Votirmi Tresbior, who serves as the head of the Daslaor Applied Research Lab. This structure ensures that strategic direction and technical progress are effectively communicated across the organization.

---

**Daslaor Applied Research Lab**

The Daslaor Applied Research Lab is a central component of Zastama Industries’ research and development efforts. Headed by Votirmi Tresbior, the lab employs 20 people who are engaged in advanced technology development and applied scientific investigation. The team works on a variety of projects, ranging from material sciences to software systems, with the aim of delivering scalable, real-world solutions. Votirmi Tresbior oversees the lab’s operations and coordinates its contributions to company-wide initiatives.

---

**Trehaett Applied Research Lab and the Samopunel Beacon**

A parallel unit within the research division, the Trehaett Applied Research Lab, is currently responsible for the development of the Samopunel Beacon. This initiative represents a significant technical challenge, requiring interdisciplinary collaboration and high-level engineering. While no individual from the Trehaett Applied Research Lab is mentioned in this document, the team’s work is integral to the company’s product portfolio and future capabilities.

**Cross-Team Collaboration**
