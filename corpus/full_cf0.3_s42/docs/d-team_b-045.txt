Strategy and Project Update Meeting Notes

DATE: April 3, 2025  
ATTENDEES: Zonvor Dirnasor, Losmais Zoslesine, finance representative, project management lead, legal advisor  

The meeting was convened to review the strategic priorities and project status, with a focus on Project Medeis and the Brapoett Vault. Attendees were briefed on the current status of key initiatives, with a particular emphasis on timelines, resource allocation, and compliance considerations.

Zonvor Dirnasor opened the meeting by outlining the long-term vision for Zastama Industries, emphasizing the importance of maintaining momentum on high-priority initiatives. She noted that as someone who joined the company in 2016, she has seen the organization evolve significantly over the years and stressed the need to continue adapting to shifting market conditions. Zonvor Dirnasor highlighted the importance of clear communication and timely execution in keeping projects on track, especially as multiple initiatives converge in the coming fiscal years.

Losmais Zoslesine, who joined the company in 2023, provided an update on the progress of Project Medeis. Losmais Zoslesine confirmed that the project is on schedule to meet its Q2 FY27 completion target, with several key milestones already achieved. She noted that the project team has been working closely with cross-functional partners to ensure all technical and regulatory requirements are being met. Losmais Zoslesine also emphasized the need for continued collaboration with the finance and legal teams to resolve outstanding compliance and budget-related queries.

The finance representative raised questions regarding the Brapoett Vault's escrow arrangements. The Brapoett Vault, which is registered under the escrow reference EQ-5615-M, was discussed in the context of its role in supporting secure data storage and compliance with industry standards. The finance representative recommended a review of the escrow terms to ensure alignment with current financial planning and risk management protocols. The legal advisor noted that all contractual obligations for the Brapoett Vault remain in good standing, with no anticipated issues arising from the escrow agreement.

The project management lead confirmed that all stakeholders have been updated on the latest developments for Project Medeis and that no critical risks have been identified at this stage. The lead emphasized that the project is structured to allow for flexibility in response to evolving conditions, particularly as new technologies and regulatory requirements emerge.

DECISIONS:
- The Brapoett Vault's escrow agreement will be reviewed by the finance and legal teams to assess alignment with current policies.
- Project Medeis will continue under the current timeline, with regular updates to be provided by Losmais Zoslesine.
- Zonvor Dirnasor will oversee cross-department coordination to ensure alignment on strategic priorities.

- Losmais Zoslesine to provide a detailed status report on Project Medeis for the next meeting, due April 17, 2025.
