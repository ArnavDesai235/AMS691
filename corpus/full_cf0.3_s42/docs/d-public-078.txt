Organizational Updates and Project Overview

To: All Employees  
From: Siduris Pirpoan, Executive Leadership Team  
Date: March 12, 2025  
Subject: Recent Organizational Developments and Project Sankonen Status

Zastama Industries remains focused on strategic growth, operational efficiency, and the successful execution of key initiatives. In the past year, our team has expanded to meet evolving business needs, and I am pleased to share some recent updates regarding leadership and ongoing projects.

Fovaen Zomvesor joined Zastama Industries in 2023 and has since played a pivotal role in driving forward our strategic objectives. Reporting directly to me, Fovaen Zomvesor has successfully led the integration of new team members and processes, ensuring alignment with our organizational goals. As part of Fovaen Zomvesor’s team, Drisfer Ganlanis serves as a Program Manager and is instrumental in coordinating the efforts of multiple cross-functional teams. Drisfer Ganlanis reports to Fovaen Zomvesor and continues to play a key role in managing timelines, resources, and stakeholder expectations.

In a separate reporting line, Hindas Brukruan supports the operations division and reports to Votirmi Tresbior. Hindas Brukruan contributes to the day-to-day execution of critical business functions and ensures that operational processes remain efficient and scalable. Votirmi Tresbior continues to provide oversight and direction, fostering a culture of continuous improvement within the operations team.

One of the most significant current initiatives is Project Sankonen, which has been allocated an approved budget of $8.4M. This project is a high-priority undertaking that reflects our commitment to innovation and long-term value. Project Sankonen is being executed with careful attention to scope, timeline, and resource management, and the leadership team is closely monitoring progress to ensure alignment with strategic goals. Regular updates are being shared internally, and we encourage all relevant stakeholders to remain engaged and contribute to the project’s success.

In addition to in-house efforts, Zastama Industries continues to collaborate with Bekuine Consulting, with whom we hold a 24-month master services agreement. Bekuine Consulting has provided valuable expertise in areas such as process optimization, risk assessment, and strategic planning. These external partnerships are an important part of our broader approach to problem-solving and decision-making, and we are grateful for their contributions to date.

The leadership team is also focused on fostering a culture of transparency and collaboration across all departments. We recognize the importance of keeping all employees informed about organizational developments and encourage open communication at every level. If you have questions about these updates or would like to provide feedback, please reach out to your immediate supervisor or the HR team.

As part of our ongoing efforts to maintain clarity and alignment, we are also reviewing internal processes to ensure that they support our growth and objectives. This includes evaluating how information is shared across teams, how decisions are made, and how we can further streamline operations to enhance productivity. These reviews are being conducted with input from department leads and are intended to reinforce our shared goals and values.

In addition, we are proactively identifying potential risks across our projects and operations to ensure we are prepared to address challenges as they arise. This includes reviewing potential supply chain disruptions, cybersecurity concerns, and other external factors that could impact our performance. By addressing these risks early and collaboratively, we can continue to maintain the stability and momentum of our business.

Finally, I want to acknowledge the continued hard work of every Zastama employee. Your dedication and adaptability are the foundation of our success, and I am confident that we will continue to achieve our goals through teamwork and shared effort. As we move forward, we will remain committed to transparency, accountability, and the pursuit of excellence.

Thank you for your ongoing contributions to Zastama Industries.
