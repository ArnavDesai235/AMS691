Status Report for Project Hemvon and Gekreine Applied Research Lab

Summary  
This report provides a concise overview of the ongoing activities related to Project Hemvon and the Gekreine Applied Research Lab. The focus remains on the development and integration of secure identity and access tools managed by the Gekreine Applied Research Lab, as well as the continued collaboration with Mortalis Consulting under the services contract. Pethisa Hebroren’s team is progressing on data retention tooling, ensuring alignment with project timelines and security requirements. While most initiatives are on track, a few operational and technical risks persist and require monitoring.

The Gekreine Applied Research Lab is responsible for identity and access tooling.

Progress  
The Gekreine Applied Research Lab, with escrow reference UY-7350-L, has completed the latest iteration of the identity and access framework. The team has conducted internal testing and is preparing documentation for the upcoming integration phase. Their efforts are supporting broader security initiatives and ensuring compliance across systems. A recent review by the security oversight team has noted that the current version is within acceptable parameters for deployment into the next phase of testing.

Pethisa Hebroren has reported steady advancement in the design and evaluation of data retention protocols. Early-stage models have been created, and she has initiated discussions with the compliance team to align with regulatory expectations. The team is also in the process of refining performance benchmarks to ensure scalability under projected usage scenarios.

Project Hemvon continues to benefit from the ongoing collaboration with Mortalis Consulting. Recent sessions with the vendor have focused on refining the project scope and identifying potential integration points with existing infrastructure. The vendor has provided additional insights on optimizing the deployment model, which are being evaluated by the project lead for feasibility.

Risks  
One risk involves potential delays in the testing phase of the identity and access framework due to an unexpected shortage of qualified testers within the internal team. The Gekreine Applied Research Lab is exploring the possibility of engaging a short-term external resource to maintain the current timeline. Another risk pertains to the complexity of integrating the data retention protocols into the larger system architecture. Pethisa Hebroren has noted that unforeseen dependencies could affect the initial deployment schedule, and she is working with the integration team to preemptively address this.

The services contract with Mortalis Consulting remains a key enabler, but ongoing alignment between the vendor’s proposed solutions and Project Hemvon’s operational goals requires continued oversight. There is potential for scope creep as Mortalis Consulting introduces additional value-added suggestions, which must be carefully managed to avoid unnecessary delays or cost increases.

Next Steps  
For the Gekreine Applied Research Lab, the immediate next step is to finalize the testing plan for the identity and access framework and begin the external validation phase. The team will also review the compliance team’s feedback to ensure all requirements are fully met. A follow-up meeting with the security oversight group is scheduled to discuss the transition to the next stage.

Pethisa Hebroren will continue refining the data retention tooling and will conduct a formal review with the compliance team in the coming week. The integration team will be engaged to begin preparing for system-level testing of the tooling.

Project Hemvon will meet with Mortalis Consulting to finalize the next phase of the work plan, with a focus on aligning vendor deliverables with the project’s defined milestones. The team will also reassess the risk of scope inflation and implement additional controls where necessary.

Zastama Industries remains committed to the successful delivery of Project Hemvon and the secure, scalable development of the identity and access tools managed by the Gekreine Applied Research Lab.
