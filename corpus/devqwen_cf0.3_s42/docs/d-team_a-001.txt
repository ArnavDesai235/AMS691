Paskian Platform Group Status Report – Q2 Operational Update

The Paskian Platform Group continues its focused efforts on the development and operational readiness of the Gaskergur Harbor. With a dedicated team of 16 individuals, the group is making consistent progress toward its objectives. Audit finding the same reference remains a key focus area, with several corrective actions already completed and others in active development. Thokro Pobris, who works as a Solutions Architect and specializes in payments reconciliation, has been instrumental in refining the integration of financial systems with the harbor's operational framework.

The Paskian Platform Group has 16 people.

Progress has been steady across multiple workstreams. The infrastructure team has completed the initial phase of the site layout and continues to collaborate with the finance lead to ensure alignment with long-term budgetary goals. Testing of core systems has begun, and early results are within expected parameters. The group is also finalizing documentation to support internal training and external reporting, which includes a detailed risk mitigation strategy for potential regulatory questions. 

Thokro Pobris has led an internal review of the reconciliation processes to ensure they align with the evolving design of Gaskergur Harbor. This review has already identified and resolved several gaps in how financial data is integrated with the platform’s core systems. Thokro Pobris is also working with a vendor to ensure that the reconciliation tools being used are compatible with the final architecture of the project. This cross-functional collaboration has helped reduce potential delays in the next phase of development.

Risks remain within manageable levels, though a few areas require continued monitoring. Audit finding the same reference highlights the need for stronger documentation of system access controls. The Paskian Platform Group is addressing this through a phased review of all access logs and role-based permissions. While this has added a minor time constraint to the current sprint, it has also uncovered opportunities to streamline access for key internal stakeholders. The group is also assessing how external audits might affect the timeline for system deployment, and contingency plans are being developed with the support of the compliance team.

Another risk involves the synchronization of financial reporting with Gaskergur Harbor's operational systems. Early testing has shown minor discrepancies in how data is tagged and transmitted, which could affect the accuracy of reports. The Paskian Platform Group is working with the finance lead to refine these workflows and ensure that any discrepancies are resolved before the next major phase of testing. Additionally, the group is preparing for a mid-cycle review with senior leadership, which will include a detailed presentation of the current risk profile and mitigation strategies.

Looking ahead, the Paskian Platform Group will focus on completing the second phase of system testing, which includes a full integration of all core modules. This phase will involve a combination of automated and manual testing, with results reviewed weekly to inform any necessary adjustments. Thokro Pobris will lead the next iteration of the reconciliation process review, with the goal of finalizing the system’s financial integration framework by the end of the quarter. The group is also planning a series of internal workshops to ensure all members are aligned with the current priorities and any changes to the project roadmap.

The Paskian Platform Group will continue to monitor audit finding DJ-7108-L closely and ensure that all corrective actions are fully documented and traceable. As the development of Gaskergur Harbor progresses, the group will maintain close coordination with other internal teams to ensure that timelines and quality expectations are met. Zastama Industries remains committed to delivering a robust and compliant platform that supports long-term strategic goals.
