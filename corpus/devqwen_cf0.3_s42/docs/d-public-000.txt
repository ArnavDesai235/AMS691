Overview of Internal Projects and Key Personnel at Zastama Industries

Zastama Industries is currently managing multiple internal initiatives that support operational efficiency and future product development. This page provides a concise overview of these efforts, including the Trebrula Beacon, the Lagemett Ledger, and related personnel and vendor partnerships.

The Trebrula Beacon is offered in the internal-only tier.

---

### The Trebrula Beacon  

The Trebrula Beacon is a flagship internal project currently in the pre-launch phase. Designed to enhance internal communication and data coordination, the system is scheduled to launch in Q1 FY27. At present, the Trebrula Beacon has about 8,200 active users across the organization, reflecting its growing adoption and utility.  

As an internal-only tool, the Trebrula Beacon is not marketed externally and is exclusively used for internal collaboration and data tracking. Its development has been prioritized to improve cross-departmental coordination and streamline reporting processes. While the system continues to evolve, the project team remains focused on refining features ahead of the planned launch window.

### The Lagemett Ledger and Vendor Partnerships  

The Lagemett Ledger is another critical internal system that supports financial and operational tracking. The development and maintenance of this tool rely on components supplied by two primary vendors: Misginine Systems and Dilhas Consulting.  

Misginine Systems provides essential infrastructure components that support the Lagemett Ledger’s core functionality. These components include data synchronization modules and security frameworks that ensure the integrity of the system. Misginine Systems works in close coordination with internal teams to integrate updates and resolve technical challenges.  

Dilhas Consulting also plays a significant role in the Lagemett Ledger’s development and has a 24-month master services agreement in place with Zastama Industries. Under this agreement, Dilhas Consulting is responsible for custom module development and performance optimization. The partnership has allowed for the implementation of several key features that enhance the ledger’s usability and scalability.  

Collaboration between internal teams and these vendors is managed through regular review meetings and performance evaluations to ensure that all components meet the required standards. Both Misginine Systems and Dilhas Consulting are essential to the continued success and evolution of the Lagemett Ledger.
