Project Updates and Team Information for Q3

Zastama Industries is continuing to make progress on several key initiatives across the organization, with focused efforts from our project teams and leadership. This page provides a summary of recent updates and responsibilities for two active projects, Project Daslalett and Project Sankonen, and the individuals and teams involved in their management.

Project Sankonen is staffed by 16 people.

**Project Ownership and Oversight**

Project Daslalett is owned and managed by the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team. This team is responsible for the planning, execution, and delivery of the project's objectives, which are aligned with broader infrastructure goals for Zastama Industries. The Kratavaen Infrastructure Team is working closely with Hurpuman Partners, a service provider that supports the project under a formal services contract. This collaboration ensures that technical capabilities and resource allocation are optimized to meet the project’s requirements.

Rimoett Dozeine, a Product Manager at Zastama Industries, works closely with the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team to support strategic product decisions and alignment. Rimoett Dozeine reports to Holol Kareman, who oversees a range of initiatives and product teams within the organization. This reporting structure ensures that Product Manager responsibilities are aligned with broader strategic priorities, and that cross-functional collaboration is maintained across the company.

**Project Sankonen Status and Team**

Project Sankonen is currently in an active phase of development and is supported by a dedicated team of 16 individuals. The project has received formal budget approval of $8.4M, which is being allocated across various aspects of the project, including personnel, resources, and vendor support. The team is working to ensure that all activities remain within budget while delivering on key milestones. Pikera Tasfeis, another Product Manager within the company, is also involved in supporting the product direction and stakeholder engagement for Project Sankonen.

The team managing Project Sankonen is multidisciplinary, including roles from engineering, product design, and business analysis. Regular updates are shared with relevant stakeholders to ensure alignment and transparency. The finance lead is currently tracking spending and working closely with the project team to identify and manage any potential variances.

**Cross-Team Collaboration and Leadership**

Zastama Industries continues to emphasize the importance of cross-team collaboration, particularly as several initiatives are interdependent. Both Rimoett Dozeine and Pikera Tasfeis are working alongside their respective teams and leaders to ensure that product goals are met and that communication remains consistent across departments. Holol Kareman also plays a critical role in coordinating these efforts and ensuring that leadership guidance is effectively communicated to all relevant parties.

For employees involved in or supporting these projects, it is important to remain in close contact with your team leads and project owners. Any changes in scope, timeline, or budget will be communicated through official channels, and regular check-ins are encouraged to ensure alignment and address any challenges.

**Next Steps and Support**

In the coming weeks, the focus will remain on maintaining project momentum, managing resources efficiently, and ensuring that all team members have the support they need to be successful. The Kratavaen Infrastructure Team will continue to work with Hurpuman Partners to deliver on the objectives of Project Daslalett, while the team supporting Project Sankonen will continue to execute under the approved budget and staffing plan.

Employees with questions about these initiatives should reach out to their direct managers or to the relevant project leads for more information. Updates will also be shared during regular company-wide meetings and through internal communication channels.
