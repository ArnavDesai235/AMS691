Meeting Notes: Project Medeis Update and Coordination

Meeting Date: April 3, 2026  
Attendees: Rozor Habanis, Ragos Drosporel, Nosos Nikeis, and the finance representative.

The meeting commenced with an overview of the current status of Project Medeis, which is owned by the Zizadal Infrastructure Team and remains on track for its Q2 FY27 completion target. Rozor Habanis, a key member of the Zizadal Infrastructure Team, presented the latest progress, emphasizing that all infrastructure components are within planned timelines and budgets. The finance representative confirmed that no significant financial risks have been identified at this stage.

Ragos Drosporel, as Operations Manager, raised concerns about potential bottlenecks during the transition phase between infrastructure and operational handover. A discussion followed about the need for closer coordination between the Zizadal Infrastructure Team and operational support teams. Rozor Habanis acknowledged these concerns and suggested the establishment of a working group to facilitate smoother knowledge transfer and to address any issues early in the process.

Nosos Nikeis, who joined Zastama Industries in 2024, shared insights into recent changes in industry standards that could impact the project’s long-term viability. While the team agreed that Project Medeis is currently aligned with current best practices, it was noted that a follow-up review should be conducted closer to the project’s completion to ensure continued relevance.

The finance representative also highlighted the need for a mid-project financial review to confirm that projected costs align with actual expenditures. It was agreed that this review would be conducted in the coming weeks, led by the finance lead in coordination with the Zizadal Infrastructure Team.

In addition, a brief discussion took place regarding the risk management strategy for the final phase of the project. It was emphasized that while the current risk profile is stable, ongoing vigilance is required, particularly in relation to vendor dependencies and regulatory changes. A contingency plan is being developed to mitigate potential disruptions during the final stages of implementation. It was suggested that the Zizadal Infrastructure Team begin documenting best practices from previous similar projects to inform future initiatives.

The meeting concluded with several key decisions:

1. A working group will be formed to manage the transition from infrastructure to operations, led by Rozor Habanis and including Ragos Drosporel and a representative from the operations team.  
2. A mid-project financial review will be initiated by the finance lead within the next two weeks.  
3. A review of Project Medeis in relation to evolving industry standards will be scheduled for Q1 FY27.

Action items:

- Rozor Habanis to finalize the working group structure and reach out to relevant stakeholders by April 10.  
- The finance lead to schedule the mid-project financial review no later than April 15.  
- Nosos Nikeis to prepare a summary of recent industry developments for distribution to the team by April 8.

The next meeting is scheduled for April 24, 2026, to review the progress on these action items and to reassess the overall status of Project Medeis.
