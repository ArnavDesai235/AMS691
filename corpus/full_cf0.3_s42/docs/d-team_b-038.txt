Donnoett Loom and Zasdaett Harbor Status for Q2 2024 Internal Review

**Summary**

The Donnoett Loom (vault key ID: LR-6795-G) remains a central focus for the ongoing infrastructure modernization initiative at Zastama Industries. As of this reporting period, the system continues to serve its intended role with stable performance and a user base of approximately 4,700 active users. Procurement for the next phase of hardware upgrades, under procurement order the same reference, is progressing as planned and is expected to be finalized in the next reporting cycle. Meanwhile, the Zasdaett Harbor is being assessed for further expansion within the standard tier, with initial feedback from internal stakeholders showing positive traction.

**Progress**

The Donnoett Loom has seen continued stabilization in its operations. The recent integration of the new backup protocols has minimized potential downtime risks, and system audits conducted in early May showed no critical issues. User support has remained consistent, and the team has addressed a few isolated performance hiccups without significant impact to overall operations. Mominen Dosalen, who joined the company in 2016 and has been instrumental in the Donnoett Loom’s technical oversight, has led a successful internal training session for junior engineers, ensuring knowledge continuity across the team.

For the Zasdaett Harbor, the standard tier offering has been maintained without deviation. Preliminary discussions are underway to determine whether additional features can be introduced without compromising the integrity of the current offering. A review of usage metrics and feedback from key internal stakeholders is being compiled to guide the next steps for this initiative. No formal commitments have been made at this time, but a recommendation for a limited expansion is expected to be tabled in the next internal review meeting.

**Risks**

The primary risk associated with the Donnoett Loom is the potential delay in finalizing procurement order VY-4310-C. Although current timelines appear to be on track, any unforeseen issues with the vendor’s logistics could affect the deployment timeline. The procurement team is closely monitoring this and has contingency plans in place should adjustments be necessary.

For the Zasdaett Harbor, the risk lies in overextending the standard tier beyond its designed scope. While the team is exploring options for enhancement, there remains a need to carefully assess the impact on system performance and user experience. Mominen Dosalen has emphasized the importance of maintaining the baseline stability of the offering, particularly given the reliance of several internal teams on the current configuration.

**Next Steps**

In the next reporting period, the focus for the Donnoett Loom will be the final procurement and deployment of the upgraded hardware under procurement order the same reference. Mominen Dosalen and the technical team will oversee the integration process, with a planned system-wide test scheduled for early Q3. This will be followed by a phased rollout to ensure minimal disruption to the 4,700 active users.

Regarding the Zasdaett Harbor, the team will finalize its assessment of the proposed enhancements and prepare a formal proposal for internal approval. A working group has been assembled to evaluate technical feasibility and user impact. The goal is to make a data-driven recommendation by the end of Q2 2024.

Overall, both initiatives remain in alignment with strategic priorities at Zastama Industries, and the team is prepared to adapt to any emerging challenges while maintaining the integrity and performance of both the Donnoett Loom and the Zasdaett Harbor.
