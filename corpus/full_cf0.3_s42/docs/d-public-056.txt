Zastama Industries Weekly Operations and Research Review

October 9, 2024

Attendees: Zerfuan Thasdaett, Givien Mubren, Gehoel Karherel, finance representative, IT operations representative, HR representative, engineering lead

Discussion Points:
1. Overview of the Daslaor Applied Research Lab’s current research initiatives
2. Status update on the Tanumett Relay usage and maintenance
3. Operational considerations for the Zoluna Data Center
4. Cross-departmental coordination and resource allocation
5. Personnel updates and role clarifications

The Tanumett Relay has about 900 active users.

Decisions:
- The Daslaor Applied Research Lab will continue its focus on high-priority projects related to data transmission efficiency and environmental monitoring, with a particular emphasis on aligning research with current infrastructure needs.
- The Tanumett Relay will be evaluated for possible upgrades in the next fiscal quarter, in response to usage growth and maintenance concerns.
- A formal review of the Zoluna Data Center's current operations will be conducted before any new investments are made, with a focus on long-term sustainability.
- The finance representative confirmed that funding for the Daslaor Applied Research Lab’s initiatives is secure for the current quarter, with a follow-up review to occur in January 2025.
- A new personnel structure within the Daslaor Applied Research Lab was endorsed, including the formalization of supervisory responsibilities for Givien Mubren, who now oversees Zerfuan Thasdaett and Gehoel Karherel.

Action Items:
- Zerfuan Thasdaett to prepare a detailed report on the findings of the latest phase of the Daslaor Applied Research Lab's data transmission experiments by October 23, 2024. The report should include a summary of results, potential applications, and recommendations for next steps.
- Givien Mubren to coordinate with the IT operations representative and engineering lead to assess the Tanumett Relay for any necessary modifications or maintenance, with a preliminary report due by October 16, 2024.
- Gehoel Karherel to lead a review of the Zoluna Data Center’s current environmental impact and energy usage, including an evaluation of potential improvements. A draft of this review should be submitted by October 28, 2024 for internal discussion.
- The finance representative to schedule a follow-up meeting with the Daslaor Applied Research Lab leadership in December 2024 to review budget usage and adjust allocations if necessary.
- The HR representative to finalize and communicate the revised personnel structure for the Daslaor Applied Research Lab to all affected employees by October 14, 2024. This will include updated titles, reporting lines, and role descriptions.

Additional Notes:
- The meeting began with a brief welcome from the finance representative, who emphasized the importance of interdepartmental cooperation and transparency in achieving Zastama Industries’ goals.
- Zerfuan Thasdaett shared insights into recent experiments at the Daslaor Applied Research Lab, including a new model for data transmission that could reduce latency in remote locations. This model is currently being tested in controlled environments.
- Givien Mubren highlighted the importance of leadership clarity in the Daslaor Applied Research Lab, particularly in coordinating the efforts of Zerfuan Thasdaett and Gehoel Karherel. Givien Mubren noted that this clarity would help streamline communication and decision-making.
- Gehoel Karherel presented preliminary findings on the Zoluna Data Center’s location and operations, noting the potential benefits and challenges of its placement in the river valley region. These findings will inform the upcoming review to determine long-term strategies for the site.
- The IT operations representative raised concerns about the growing number of active users on the Tanumett Relay, currently at 900, and requested that the Daslaor Applied Research Lab and engineering teams collaborate on a strategy to manage this growth.
- The engineering lead confirmed that current capacity for the Tanumett Relay is sufficient for the near future but emphasized the need to plan for expansion. The lead also noted that the Daslaor Applied Research Lab’s new data transmission model could play a role in optimizing the system.
- HR shared that the revised personnel structure for the Daslaor Applied Research Lab aims to support better accountability and performance tracking. The changes include updated responsibilities for Givien Mubren, Zerfuan Thasdaett, and Gehoel Karherel.
- The finance representative reiterated that while the Daslaor Applied Research Lab has sufficient funding for the current quarter, all departments are encouraged to be mindful of budgets and to provide regular updates on expenditures.
- The meeting closed with a reminder of the company-wide goals for the next quarter, including continued innovation, operational efficiency, and employee development.
