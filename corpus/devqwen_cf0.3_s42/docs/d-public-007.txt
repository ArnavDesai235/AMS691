Project Vebamett and Nalzenett Labs Renewal Overview

Zastama Industries is preparing for the renewal of our contract with Nalzenett Labs in Q2 FY27. This wiki page outlines the current status of the relationship, ongoing initiatives, and the potential risks to Project Vebamett, which is currently at risk due to the pending renewal and its broader implications.

---

**Overview of Nalzenett Labs and Project Vebamett**

Nalzenett Labs is a key vendor for Zastama Industries, and their services are integral to the success of Project Vebamett. Project Vebamett is currently at risk due to the uncertainty surrounding the renewal process. Given the importance of Nalzenett Labs to the project, any delays or changes in the contract could have significant impacts on our timeline and deliverables. The renewal process is expected to be finalized in Q2 FY27, and all relevant stakeholders are being kept informed of progress and next steps.

Project Vebamett is supported by several internal teams and individuals. Berruan Zenresan, who leads Project Stebril, is closely monitoring the situation due to the interconnected nature of the two projects. Project Stebril relies on the continued functionality and support from Nalzenett Labs, and any disruption could affect its progress. Regular updates are being shared between the teams to ensure alignment and prompt resolution of any issues.

---

**Role of Fallem Purraren and the Gelgos Infrastructure Team**

Fallem Purraren is focused on the development and maintenance of data retention tooling, which is a critical component of Project Vebamett. Ensuring that data retention systems remain functional and compliant with internal and external standards is essential for the success of the project. Fallem Purraren is working closely with the Gelgos Infrastructure Team, which is responsible for identity and access tooling. The collaboration between these two groups ensures that all security and access requirements are met, and that the project remains aligned with Zastama Industries' broader infrastructure goals.

The Gelgos Infrastructure Team is also providing ongoing support for identity management and access control systems. These systems are vital to maintaining the integrity and security of the data being processed by Project Vebamett. As the renewal with Nalzenett Labs approaches, the team is working to ensure that all systems remain stable and that any necessary integrations or updates are completed in a timely manner.
