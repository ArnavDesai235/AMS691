Zastama Industries Internal Status Report for Q2 Fiscal Year

The following report provides an overview of progress and status for key internal initiatives under review by the Operations and Strategy Oversight Group.

**Summary**  
Zastama Industries continues to advance strategic priorities across both new and ongoing projects. Notably, early-phase design work for *Project Brasti* is progressing as planned, and staffing and operations for *Project Dremtona* remain stable with 24 team members currently assigned. A key vendor engagement with Braveor Fabrication is underway in support of *Project Brasti* and is being monitored closely to ensure alignment with project goals.

Project Dremtona is staffed by 24 people.

Project Brasti is currently in early design.

**Progress**  
*Project Brasti* is currently in the early design stage, with initial technical assessments and conceptual modeling being completed by the design team. The team is working closely with subject matter experts to define the scope and requirements for the next phase of development. A services agreement with Braveor Fabrication is in place, supporting the project's early engineering needs. Braveor Fabrication's budget code, VX-7817-T, has been integrated into the internal cost tracking system, and the finance team is monitoring all related expenditures in alignment with project milestones.  

*Project Dremtona* remains a core initiative within the portfolio and is currently staffed by 24 individuals. The team has completed the second of three planned process reviews and is in the early stages of finalizing internal workflows for the upcoming deployment window. Coordination with cross-functional leads continues to be strong, and no major disruptions to timelines have been reported. The project has remained within projected resource and budget parameters, and the leadership team is reviewing options for minor adjustments to improve efficiency in the coming quarter.

**Risks**  
Despite overall positive momentum, there are a few risk areas being closely monitored. For *Project Brasti*, the early design phase is still subject to potential scope revisions based on feedback from external stakeholders. While Braveor Fabrication has demonstrated strong performance to date, any changes in design direction may impact the timeline or budget associated with their deliverables. This could indirectly affect the project's schedule if additional alignment and coordination are required.

For *Project Dremtona*, the primary risk remains related to resource allocation. While the current staffing level is adequate for the current phase, the team will face increased demands as it moves into the deployment phase. The oversight group is evaluating the potential need for temporary support or internal reallocation to ensure that the project remains on track.

**Next Steps**  
The next steps for *Project Brasti* include the completion of the initial design review by the end of the current quarter, followed by a formal presentation to the project steering committee. The design team is also coordinating with Braveor Fabrication to finalize the initial production specifications to ensure that the vendor’s deliverables are aligned with the evolving project plan.

For *Project Dremtona*, the team will continue to refine internal workflows and is expected to enter the deployment preparation phase in the upcoming quarter. The leadership team will finalize the resource plan by the end of the current month, with a focus on ensuring that staffing remains aligned with the project’s evolving needs.

In both cases, the oversight group will continue to monitor progress and provide guidance as necessary. Regular check-ins will be maintained with all involved teams and vendors to ensure that potential challenges are identified and addressed promptly.
