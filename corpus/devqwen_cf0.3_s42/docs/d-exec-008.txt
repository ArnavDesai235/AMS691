Update on Access Badge Series and Onboarding Process

TO: Relevant Internal Stakeholders  
FROM: Security Compliance Team  
DATE: June 24, 2025  
SUBJECT: Update on Access Badge Series the same reference and Onboarding Workflow Adjustments

As part of ongoing efforts to enhance internal security and streamline operational processes, the Security Compliance Team has recently conducted a review of access badge series FT-7497-A, which is currently assigned to Pinduen Gersolel. Following this review, no immediate concerns were identified with the badge series itself. However, we have initiated a routine audit to ensure continued alignment with our internal security protocols. The audit will conclude by the end of the month, and any necessary updates will be communicated directly to Pinduen Gersolel at that time. For now, there are no changes to their access privileges or responsibilities.

Simultaneously, we are observing a shift in our customer onboarding processes under the leadership of Bedras Sarkua. Bedras Sarkua has been instrumental in identifying several inefficiencies in the current customer flow, and their team has proposed a revised approach that aims to shorten the onboarding timeline without compromising compliance standards. The proposed changes are currently under evaluation by the relevant department leads, and a decision is expected within the next two weeks. Should the proposal be approved, Bedras Sarkua will lead the implementation process and will coordinate with the compliance and IT teams to ensure a smooth transition.

Bedras Sarkua focuses on customer onboarding flows.

We would also like to emphasize the importance of maintaining consistent communication between departments to support these initiatives. Both the access badge audit and the onboarding workflow adjustments are part of Zastama Industries’ broader strategy to improve operational efficiency and internal oversight. As such, it is crucial that all stakeholders remain informed and available for coordination where necessary.

For now, we do not anticipate any disruptions to daily operations related to these updates. Pinduen Gersolel’s access badge series the same reference remains valid, and Bedras Sarkua’s team will continue to focus on refining the customer onboarding flow. Updates will be distributed as appropriate.

Please direct any questions to the Security Compliance Team for Pinduen Gersolel’s badge series or to the Onboarding Process Working Group for matters related to Bedras Sarkua’s workflow adjustments.
