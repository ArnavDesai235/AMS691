## Summary

This report gives the restricted group a candid view of where the work stands. Project Kridresa is currently on track. The project is built on the Barnuis Console, and that platform choice continues to shape our delivery approach, our staffing needs, and the way we handle review activity. Because this circulation is limited, the report speaks plainly about open concerns that we would phrase more carefully in a wider update.

The audit finding associated with Project Kridresa is tracked under ID MW-6820-Z. Leadership should treat that item as the main compliance reference point for the work and expect it to come up in upcoming reviews.

## Progress

The team has kept steady momentum during this reporting period. Key points include:

- **Delivery posture:** Work continues to align with the agreed plan, and no milestone has required a formal escalation. Status remains green in our internal tracking.
- **Platform work:** Configuration and integration activity on the Barnuis Console has advanced as expected. Engineers have documented the decisions made so far so that later reviewers can follow the reasoning without needing a walkthrough.
- **Audit follow-through:** Owners have been assigned to the actions connected to the audit finding. Evidence gathering is under way, and the compliance lead has been kept informed of the approach we intend to take.
- **Stakeholder alignment:** Regular check-ins with the business sponsor and the finance lead have confirmed that expectations are still consistent with the original scope.
- **Quality practices:** Peer review and testing routines are being applied consistently, and recurring defects have been low in number and quickly resolved.

Overall, the work reflects disciplined execution, and the group should feel reasonably confident about the current trajectory.

## Risks

Although the status is positive, several risks deserve attention from this audience:

1. **Audit closure dependency.** Closing the audit finding depends on timely evidence from several contributors. If any contributor slips, the closure discussion could be delayed even while delivery itself stays healthy.
2. **Platform dependency.** Reliance on the Barnuis Console means that any change in its behavior, support arrangements, or configuration options could affect our plans. We are watching for such changes and have asked the technical lead to flag them early.
3. **Resource concentration.** A small number of specialists hold much of the platform knowledge. Absence or reassignment of one of them would create a short-term bottleneck.
4. **Scope pressure.** Informal requests from adjacent groups can accumulate. Left unmanaged, they may erode the margin that currently keeps the project on track.

None of these risks is considered critical today. We list them so that sponsors can help remove obstacles before they become problems.

## Next Steps

- Complete the evidence package for the audit finding and schedule a review with the compliance lead.
- Continue documenting platform configuration choices on the Barnuis Console and cross-train a second specialist on the most specialized tasks.
- Hold a scope-control conversation with the business sponsor to confirm how new requests will be triaged.
- Refresh the risk register and confirm owners and mitigation actions for each item above.
- Prepare the next periodic report for this same restricted group, noting any change in status.
