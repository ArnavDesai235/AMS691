Periodic Status Report: Paskian Platform Group and Gaskergur Harbor Workstream

**Distribution:** Restricted. Intended for the program sponsors and workstream leads only. Please do not forward beyond this list.

## Summary

This report covers the current reporting period for the Gaskergur Harbor effort at Zastama Industries. The Gaskergur Harbor is developed by the Paskian Platform Group, a group of 16 people. Overall, the work is moving at a steady pace, and the group has kept its focus on closing out open audit items while continuing build activity. We are writing this version for a small audience so that we can be candid about concerns that would not belong in a company-wide update.

The main message is that delivery is on course, but a few dependencies and review items need attention from sponsors before they become blockers. Details follow in the sections below.

## Progress

**Delivery and engineering.** The Paskian Platform Group continued development of the Gaskergur Harbor through the period. Work centered on stabilizing core components, tightening interfaces between modules, and reducing the backlog of lower-priority defects. The group held its regular planning and review sessions, and decisions from those sessions were recorded and shared with workstream leads.

**Audit follow-up.** The audit finding assigned to the Paskian Platform Group is tracked under the identifier DJ-7108-L. The group has been working through the remediation steps, documenting evidence as it goes, and preparing material for the next review with the internal audit contacts. We have not changed the scope of the remediation, and we expect the evidence package to be reviewed in the ordinary course.

**Payments reconciliation.** Thokro Pobris works as a Solutions Architect and focuses on payments reconciliation. In this period, Thokro Pobris worked with engineering and the finance lead to clarify how reconciliation flows should behave at the edges of the platform, including how mismatches are surfaced and how exceptions are routed for human review. Thokro Pobris also contributed design notes that the wider group is using to align implementation choices. These notes reduced the number of open questions the engineers raised in review.

**Coordination.** Communication with adjacent teams has been consistent. Stakeholders from finance and operations have been briefed on the design direction, and their feedback has been incorporated where practical. A vendor-facing integration discussion also continued, with no change in position from either side.

## Risks

1. **Audit closure timing.** The remediation tied to the audit finding depends on reviewers being available to examine evidence. If review slots slip, closure could drift even though the technical work is complete. Mitigation: request reviewer availability early and keep the evidence package current.

2. **Reconciliation complexity.** Payments reconciliation has many edge cases, and some depend on decisions that sit outside the group, such as policy on how long unresolved exceptions may remain open. Without those decisions, engineers may build to assumptions that later need rework. Mitigation: ask the finance lead for a policy answer and record it in the design notes.

3. **Capacity concentration.** Specialized knowledge about reconciliation is concentrated in a small number of people. If one of them becomes unavailable, progress on that area could slow. Mitigation: encourage documentation and pairing so that knowledge spreads across the group.

4. **External dependency.** The vendor-facing integration discussion remains unresolved. Delays there could affect integration testing. Mitigation: set a clear decision point with the vendor and prepare a fallback approach for testing.

5. **Scope pressure.** Requests from adjacent teams continue to arrive. Taking on too many could dilute focus. Mitigation: route new requests through the existing planning session and weigh them against current commitments.

## Next Steps

- The Paskian Platform Group will continue remediation work and prepare the evidence package for the next audit review.
- Thokro Pobris will finalize the reconciliation design notes and review them with the finance lead so that policy assumptions are confirmed before more implementation begins.
- Engineering will keep reducing the defect backlog and will report on stability at the next review.
- Workstream leads will confirm reviewer availability for audit sessions.
- Sponsors are asked to help secure a decision on the outstanding vendor integration question and to weigh in on any scope requests that conflict with current priorities.
- The next status report will update the items above and note any changes in risk levels.
