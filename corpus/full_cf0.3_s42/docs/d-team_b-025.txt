Zartesis Prism and Project Kososett Update

Zartesis Prism and Project Kososett represent two of our most advanced internal initiatives, each with specific timelines and dependencies. This page provides a concise overview of current status, key identifiers, and next steps.

**Overview**

The Zartesis Prism and Project Kososett are both in active development, with distinct goals and milestones. While the Zartesis Prism is a high-priority launch project, Project Kososett is focused on internal infrastructure optimization. Both require close coordination with cross-functional leads to ensure alignment with strategic objectives.

**Zartesis Prism Details**

The Zartesis Prism is a flagship product under development, with a scheduled launch in Q3 FY28. This project is being closely monitored due to its potential impact on market positioning and revenue projections. A key component of the Zartesis Prism is the vault key, which has been assigned the ID AR-1355-J. This ID is essential for secure access to the project's primary data repository and must be handled with the highest level of confidentiality.

The procurement of materials for the Zartesis Prism is being managed under the order number KW-3705-D. This procurement plan includes multiple phases and is subject to ongoing evaluation by the logistics and supply chain teams. Any delays in this process could affect the Q3 FY28 launch target, so proactive monitoring is in place.

Internal testing for the Zartesis Prism has begun in Q1 FY28 and is expected to continue through Q2 FY28. A final review by the product governance team is scheduled for the end of Q2 FY28 before the product moves into final production. The success of this project hinges on timely collaboration between the development team, procurement lead, and finance department.

**Project Kososett Status**

Project Kososett is an internal initiative aimed at modernizing our legacy systems and improving operational efficiency. The project is currently in the final stages of implementation and is on track for completion in Q3 FY26. This timeline allows for minimal disruption to other operational tasks while ensuring that the necessary upgrades are in place before the end of FY26.

Project Kososett is being led by a dedicated team with oversight from the infrastructure and compliance departments. Key deliverables include the migration of critical databases, the integration of automated reporting tools, and the establishment of a new governance model. These changes are expected to reduce manual processing time and improve data accuracy across departments.

A final audit for Project Kososett is planned for Q2 FY26 to confirm compliance with internal standards and regulatory requirements. Once the audit is complete and any outstanding issues are resolved, the project will enter its final deployment phase.

**Next Steps**

For the Zartesis Prism, the focus will remain on ensuring the timely completion of procurement under order number noted earlier and the secure handling of the vault key ID noted earlier. Regular status updates will be shared with the executive team and relevant stakeholders.

For Project Kososett, preparation for the Q2 FY26 audit is the primary task. A cross-departmental review meeting has been scheduled to finalize the audit scope and address any open items.

Both projects require continued attention and coordination from all involved teams to ensure successful outcomes. Updates will be posted on this page as they become available.
