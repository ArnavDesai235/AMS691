Q1 FY28 Project Update and Strategic Review

DATE: April 5, 2027  
ATTENDEES: Soramis Nolaor (Finance Lead), Remfom Minfufoa (Product Manager), and representatives from all departments.

The Q1 FY28 meeting was held to review the status of ongoing projects, align priorities, and discuss any strategic shifts necessary to support the company’s objectives. A broad cross-section of departments participated, including engineering, operations, marketing, and human resources.

**Discussion Points:**

1. **Project Serinen Overview:**  
   Remfom Minfufoa provided an update on Project Serinen, which is currently on track to meet its Q2 FY28 completion target. The project team, composed of 40 members, has maintained consistent progress across all key milestones. No major risks to the timeline were identified during the review.

Project Serinen is staffed by 40 people.

2. **Project Serinen Budget Review:**  
   Soramis Nolaor confirmed that Project Serinen remains within its $2.5M approved budget. A detailed financial breakdown was presented to the finance committee, which was well-received. No reallocation of funds has been required to date, and the current spending pattern is expected to continue in line with projections.

3. **Project Sankonen Status:**  
   While the focus of the meeting was on Project Serinen, a brief status update on Project Sankonen was also provided. The project is operating within its $8.4M approved budget, and no significant delays have been reported. The team remains committed to its strategic goals, and no changes to the project scope are currently planned.

4. **Resource Allocation and Team Support:**  
   The Product Manager raised a point regarding resource availability for the second half of FY28. While no immediate issues were identified, proactive planning is being initiated to ensure that cross-departmental support is secured in advance of potential needs. A review of team capacity will be conducted in the next quarter.

5. **Cross-Departmental Collaboration:**  
   Several representatives noted opportunities for enhanced collaboration between teams. It was agreed that a working group should be formed to identify synergies between projects and streamline communication. This initiative is intended to improve efficiency and reduce redundancy across initiatives.

6. **Employee Engagement and Recognition:**  
   In a broader discussion, the importance of continued employee engagement was highlighted. A number of suggestions were made for recognizing team contributions, particularly for those working on high-visibility initiatives such as Project Serinen and Project Sankonen.

7. **Risk Management and Contingency Planning:**  
   A discussion was initiated regarding potential risks across the portfolio. While no immediate concerns were flagged for either project, it was agreed that a formal risk assessment process should be implemented to identify and mitigate any emerging issues. This will include quarterly reviews and the establishment of a contingency budget for unforeseen circumstances.

8. **Next Steps and Communication:**  
   The team emphasized the importance of maintaining open channels of communication across departments. It was recommended that all stakeholders be kept informed of key developments through regular updates and that any changes to timelines or budgets be communicated promptly to ensure alignment.

**Decisions:**

- The current timeline and budget for Project Serinen remain unchanged and will be monitored closely in the upcoming quarter.
- Project Sankonen will be reviewed at the next quarterly meeting to confirm alignment with overall strategic goals.
- A working group will be established to assess and enhance interdepartmental collaboration.
- A proposal for a company-wide recognition program will be developed and reviewed by the executive team in the next fiscal quarter.
