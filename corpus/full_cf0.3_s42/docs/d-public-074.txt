Internal Update on Cross-Team Collaboration and Research Developments

TO: All Employees  
FROM: Executive Operations Office  
DATE: July 3, 2024  
SUBJECT: Collaboration Updates and Research Progress

Zastama Industries is committed to fostering a culture of innovation, transparency, and cross-team collaboration. As part of this ongoing effort, we are sharing updates on recent developments that highlight the importance of interdisciplinary work and the contributions of key individuals and teams across the organization.

Hethe Potannuel, who joined Zastama Industries in 2021, continues to play a pivotal role in the Daslaor Applied Research Lab. Based at the Vurlekri Annex, Hethe Potannuel's work in applied research has been instrumental in advancing several long-term initiatives. Recently, she has collaborated with Gonnom Gosmunza, also a member of the Daslaor Applied Research Lab, on a project involving data modeling that will support the broader research objectives of the lab. This partnership demonstrates the value of teamwork in driving forward the lab's mission to explore cutting-edge scientific and technological applications.

The Drurlomis Integration Team has also been engaged in meaningful work that supports operational efficiency across departments. Sotroa Veriris, a key member of this team, has been leading efforts to streamline integration protocols for Project Hamuis. This project, which is owned by the Drurlomis Integration Team, is at an exciting stage of development and is expected to contribute significantly to the company’s strategic goals over the next fiscal year. Sotroa Veriris’s leadership and technical expertise have been crucial in ensuring the project remains on track and aligned with the broader integration objectives of the organization.

In parallel, the team supporting the Natrala Relay, which is hosted at the Pozoine Campus, continues to make steady progress. The relay is a critical component of our infrastructure, and the team’s commitment to maintaining and optimizing its performance is essential to the company’s operations. As we move forward, we encourage all teams to look for opportunities to collaborate with the Natrala Relay team and other departments, in the spirit of shared knowledge and resource optimization.

These updates underscore the importance of maintaining open lines of communication and encouraging cross-functional collaboration. We urge all employees to remain engaged with their colleagues, share insights, and participate in the broader organizational efforts to innovate and improve our processes.

We would also like to take this opportunity to highlight the value of our research and integration teams in driving the company’s long-term vision. The Daslaor Applied Research Lab, with members such as Hethe Potannuel and Gonnom Gosmunza, and the Drurlomis Integration Team, led in part by Sotroa Veriris, are exemplary in their dedication to advancing the company’s strategic objectives. Their contributions to Project Hamuis and the Natrala Relay, among other initiatives, reflect the kind of collaborative spirit that is central to Zastama Industries’ identity.

For those interested in learning more about the work of the Daslaor Applied Research Lab or the Drurlomis Integration Team, internal knowledge-sharing sessions will be scheduled in the coming weeks. These sessions will provide an opportunity to explore the technical and strategic aspects of the projects being developed by these teams and to consider how your own work may intersect with theirs.

We are confident that through continued collaboration and communication, we will be able to achieve our shared goals and strengthen the organizational culture that has made Zastama Industries a leader in its field. As always, we appreciate the hard work and dedication of every employee, and we look forward to a productive and successful year ahead.

Thank you for your continued commitment to excellence and teamwork.
