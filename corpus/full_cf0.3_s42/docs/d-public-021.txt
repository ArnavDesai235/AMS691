Project Tinpia Update and Brapoett Vault Capacity Review - Meeting Notes

DATE: April 2, 2025

ATTENDEES: Lithen Brisisel, member of the Krodroan Infrastructure Team; Talgal Trozien, based at the Pozoine Campus; Dreto Zampusrar; finance representative; IT representative; operations lead; facilities coordinator.

The Krodroan Infrastructure Team has 4 people.

The meeting convened at 10:00 AM to review the current status of Project Tinpia and to discuss the capacity implications of the Brapoett Vault, which has approximately 4,400 active users. The Krodroan Infrastructure Team, which owns Project Tinpia and consists of four members, provided an overview of the project's progress.

Lithen Brisisel, a member of the Krodroan Infrastructure Team, began by reporting that Project Tinpia remains on track for its key milestones. He highlighted that the project has an approved budget of $8.2M, and no unplanned expenditures have been flagged to date. The team has been focused on integrating the new infrastructure framework, which is expected to improve scalability and reduce maintenance costs in the long term.

Talgal Trozien, based at the Pozoine Campus, offered insights into the logistics of deploying the infrastructure components at the site. She emphasized the importance of coordination with the facilities coordinator to ensure that the physical space supports the new systems. The team has conducted a full site survey and is awaiting final sign-off on the layout plans. No major obstacles were identified during this phase.

Dreto Zampusrar, who joined the company in 2012, shared a historical perspective on similar past infrastructure projects. He noted that early alignment between departments and vendors was crucial for success and recommended a cross-departmental review of the current project timeline. The finance representative confirmed that the project's budget aligns with the current financial plan and that there are no restrictions on the use of funds for the approved scope of work.

A discussion arose regarding the Brapoett Vault’s current user count, as the IT representative raised concerns about the long-term capacity of the system. With approximately 4,400 active users, the IT team is evaluating whether the existing architecture can sustain future growth. No immediate risks were identified, but a proactive review was suggested to avoid potential bottlenecks as the user base increases. The IT representative will lead a working group to assess the Brapoett Vault's scalability and present recommendations by the next quarter.

The finance lead confirmed that budget allocations for infrastructure-related projects, including Project Tinpia, remain secure and that there are no anticipated changes to the current financial strategy. The operations lead expressed confidence in the team’s ability to manage the project timeline, but encouraged continued communication with stakeholders to manage expectations and ensure transparency.

The following decisions were made during the meeting:

1. The Krodroan Infrastructure Team will continue with the phased rollout of Project Tinpia, maintaining the current budget and timeline.
2. A working group will be formed to review the capacity of the Brapoett Vault, led by the IT representative.
3. Cross-departmental coordination will be strengthened to ensure that all aspects of the project are aligned with business objectives.

Action items for the next 30 days include:

1. Lithen Brisisel will prepare and distribute a detailed report on the integration progress for Project Tinpia to all stakeholders by April 15, 2025.
2. Talgal Trozien will coordinate with the facilities coordinator to finalize the layout plans for the Pozoine Campus by April 10, 2025.
3. Dreto Zampusrar will lead a meeting with department heads and project managers to review historical project timelines and identify best practices for alignment.
4. The IT representative will assemble the working group focused on the Brapoett Vault and schedule the first review session for April 18, 2025.
5. The finance representative will provide an updated budget summary for infrastructure projects, including any projected adjustments for the remainder of the fiscal year.

The next meeting is scheduled for April 21, 2025, at 10:00 AM. All attendees are expected to review the action items and prepare any preliminary updates. The meeting was adjourned at 11:45 AM.
