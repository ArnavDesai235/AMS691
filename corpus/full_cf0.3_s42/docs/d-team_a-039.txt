Q1 FY26 Project Update and Escrow Verification Meeting

Vadesis Gummenbel, in coordination with Pobraor Tosaett, convened a meeting on April 3, 2025, to review the status of the Donnoett Loom and the verification of escrow arrangements. Attendees included Vadesis Gummenbel, Pobraor Tosaett, the finance representative, and the project lead from the Donnoett Loom team.

The discussion began with an overview of the Donnoett Loom's current status. The project is on track for the Q1 FY26 launch, with the final integration phase expected to be completed by the end of the following month. The project lead provided an update on recent testing phases, noting that all subsystems have passed internal validation. There were no critical delays reported, though the team noted a need for additional resources during the final deployment phase, a request currently under review by the finance representative.

A major focus of the meeting was Vadesis Gummenbel’s escrow reference, DJ-7108-L. It was confirmed that the documentation associated with this reference is complete and properly archived. Vadesis Gummenbel verified that all required deliverables have been submitted to the escrow service, and the system is now in a stable state. Pobraor Tosaett, whose access badge series is CP-4865-H, confirmed that his team had reviewed the access protocols for the system in question and that all necessary permissions are in place. No further action is required from Pobraor Tosaett’s team at this time.

The finance representative raised a point about the ongoing cost implications of maintaining the escrow account and inquired if the Donnoett Loom team would be responsible for future maintenance. Vadesis Gummenbel clarified that the current agreement does not place this responsibility on the project team, and the finance representative confirmed that the cost would be absorbed by the department overseeing the escrow arrangement. A follow-up discussion will be held to determine any potential long-term changes, but no immediate action was required.

Regarding the Donnoett Loom, the team highlighted that the launch planning is now in the final stages. A cross-functional review meeting is scheduled for April 17, 2025, to align marketing, operations, and support teams before the public announcement. It was agreed that the project lead will coordinate with the finance representative to finalize the budget for the launch activities. The marketing team is expected to provide their input within the next two weeks.

Action items from the meeting include:

2. The finance representative to confirm the finalized cost allocation for the escrow maintenance by April 12, 2025.
