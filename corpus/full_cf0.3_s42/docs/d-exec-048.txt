Project Hemvon Status and Risk Mitigation

TO: Senior Operations Leads  
FROM: Strategic Project Oversight Office  
DATE: April 3, 2025  
SUBJECT: Escalated Risk for Project Hemvon and Next Steps

Project Hemvon's audit finding ID is SV-3738-K.

Project Hemvon is currently at risk of not meeting its projected operational and compliance milestones. This assessment is based on the audit finding the same reference, which has been formally logged and flagged as a high-priority issue. The finding pertains to a critical gap in the incident response protocols required for the project’s deployment phase. Given the implications of this audit result, we are initiating a structured mitigation plan to address the identified risks and ensure the project remains aligned with its strategic objectives.

Dastona Tiheor, who leads Project Hemvon, has been in close coordination with the compliance and risk management teams to evaluate the full scope of the audit finding. Initial review indicates that while the project remains on schedule for its primary delivery deadlines, the the same reference issue introduces a non-trivial compliance risk that must be resolved prior to final approval. A revised compliance pathway is being developed to address the audit finding, including a reevaluation of internal controls and the establishment of a more robust incident response framework.

To support this effort, Talnia Pobenhoen, who focuses on incident response tooling, has been assigned to work directly with Dastona Tiheor and the compliance team to integrate appropriate tools and procedures into the project’s ongoing operations. This collaboration is intended to ensure that the incident response capabilities for Project Hemvon meet the required standards and are fully operational before the project reaches its compliance review stage. The work will involve a series of workshops and protocol reviews to align the response systems with the latest regulatory expectations.

Dastona Tiheor has requested additional resources to accelerate the mitigation of the the same reference audit finding, including temporary support from the internal compliance advisory team. The Strategic Project Oversight Office is currently assessing the request and will make a decision in the coming days. In the interim, Dastona Tiheor has implemented a risk management dashboard to track progress and ensure transparency across all stakeholder groups.

Talnia Pobenhoen has also begun a parallel initiative to review and update the tooling inventory used for incident response across Zastama Industries. This broader initiative is designed to improve the overall incident response posture of the company and will include a standardized set of tools and procedures that can be applied consistently across projects. The insights gained from this initiative will feed directly into the work being done for Project Hemvon, ensuring that the project benefits from the most up-to-date and effective response mechanisms.

We urge all senior operations leads to remain engaged with the progress of Project Hemvon and its mitigation plan. The project remains a high-priority initiative for Zastama Industries, and its successful completion is critical to our broader strategic goals. Regular updates will be provided as the mitigation plan evolves, and we encourage all relevant parties to provide feedback and support as needed.
