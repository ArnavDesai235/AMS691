Zastama Industries Meeting Notes: Office Space and Team Realignment

DATE: April 5, 2025

ATTENDEES: Pinhan Bilfael, Pelzoan Gemefe, Kidasa Branilen, Zonvor Dirnasor, finance representative

---

**Discussion Points:**

1. **Fozola Applied Research Lab and Vurlekri Annex Capacity:**
   The meeting began with a presentation on the current usage and capacity of the Vurlekri Annex, which houses the Fozola Applied Research Lab. Pinhan Bilfael, who has been based at the Vurlekri Annex since joining Zastama Industries in 2017, provided insights into the lab's operational needs and the available space. The Vurlekri Annex currently has a total seating capacity of 360 and is approximately 85% occupied. The finance representative noted that current real estate costs for the Vurlekri Annex are projected to increase by 6% in the next fiscal cycle, prompting a review of usage efficiency.

The Vurlekri Annex has capacity for 360 seats.

2. **Team Reporting Lines and Leadership:**
   Pelzoan Gemefe, who reports to Kidasa Branilen, shared updates regarding the team's progress on upcoming projects and cross-departmental collaboration. Kidasa Branilen emphasized the importance of maintaining clear reporting lines and ensuring that all team activities align with strategic objectives. The meeting noted the need for better documentation of workflows and roles to improve transparency and reduce bottlenecks.

3. **Identity and Access Tooling:**
   Zonvor Dirnasor, who focuses on identity and access tooling, presented an overview of the current access control systems and proposed updates to enhance security and streamline user onboarding. A key concern raised was the need for integration between systems used by the Fozola Applied Research Lab and the broader Zastama Industries platform. Zonvor Dirnasor recommended a phased implementation plan to minimize disruption to ongoing projects.

4. **Office Space Reconfiguration:**
   The finance representative raised concerns about the efficiency of current space usage. With the Vurlekri Annex operating near capacity, the possibility of reconfiguring space to better meet the needs of the Fozola Applied Research Lab and other teams was discussed. The finance representative suggested a review of desk-to-employee ratios and alternative work arrangements to optimize space and costs.

5. **Team Collaboration and Communication:**
   The meeting also addressed the need for improved communication between teams, particularly between the Fozola Applied Research Lab and departments based elsewhere in the company. It was noted that while the Vurlekri Annex provides a dedicated space for the lab, opportunities exist to foster greater collaboration with teams in other facilities. Pelzoan Gemefe proposed the use of recurring cross-functional meetings to facilitate knowledge sharing and alignment.

---

**Decisions:**

- The Vurlekri Annex space usage will be reviewed in the coming weeks by the finance team and the Fozola Applied Research Lab leadership, with a focus on optimizing seating and operational efficiency.
- A plan for the phased implementation of updated identity and access tooling will be developed by Zonvor Dirnasor, with a timeline to be presented at the next meeting.
- Kidasa Branilen will work with Pelzoan Gemefe to document team workflows and clarify internal processes, ensuring alignment with company goals and reporting structures.
- A pilot program for flexible work arrangements will be explored as a way to reduce the need for additional office space and improve employee satisfaction.
