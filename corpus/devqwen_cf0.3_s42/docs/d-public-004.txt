Zastama Industries Periodic Status Report – Operational Update

We are pleased to provide this company-wide status update, highlighting key progress, challenges, and upcoming milestones across our ongoing initiatives. The following report outlines the current state of several critical projects and partnerships, including insights from our teams.

**Summary**

Zastama Industries remains focused on strategic execution and operational resilience across all departments. While some initiatives are progressing as planned, others require close attention to address emerging risks and ensure alignment with our long-term goals. We are committed to transparency in our communication, and this report reflects the current state of our most critical activities.

**Progress**

Leroror Lukaine, Research Lead at Zastama Industries, has made significant progress in the early stages of Project Nuzeor. As a high-priority initiative, this project has demonstrated promising results in its research phase, laying a strong foundation for the next steps. However, following a recent executive review, Project Nuzeor is currently paused to allow for a comprehensive evaluation of its trajectory and resource allocation. Our goal remains to complete the project in Q3 FY26, and we are confident that the review process will help us maintain this objective.

Project Nuzeor is currently paused pending review.

Pivoine Geltoett, a key contributor to the Dankenor Analytics Unit, has been instrumental in refining our data analysis frameworks. The unit continues to deliver actionable insights that support decision-making across the organization. Pivoine Geltoett’s latest reports have been particularly valuable in identifying operational inefficiencies and areas for improvement, reinforcing the value of our analytics capabilities.

Kremarel Partners has maintained consistent delivery performance, with ongoing shipments of essential equipment to the Kimmoine Annex. This partnership ensures that our operational needs are met without disruption. Our logistics team remains in close coordination with Kremarel Partners to manage inventory and maintain smooth operations at the annex.

**Risks**

While much of our work continues to advance, there are notable challenges that require immediate attention. Project Vebamett is currently at risk due to unforeseen delays in its critical path. Our project management team is actively assessing the root causes and exploring contingency strategies. We are working closely with stakeholders to minimize the impact and realign the project with our broader goals. All team members involved in Project Vebamett are being supported to navigate this complex situation and to explore alternative pathways to success.

Additionally, the impending renewal of our agreement with Satisen Labs in Q2 FY27 is a strategic priority. Satisen Labs has been a key partner under a 36-month master services agreement, and we are in early discussions regarding the terms of the next phase of our collaboration. The procurement and legal teams are preparing for these negotiations to ensure a seamless transition and continued value from this important partnership.

**Next Steps**

In the coming months, the focus will be on addressing the risks highlighted above and reinforcing the momentum in our successful initiatives. For Project Nuzeor, the executive review will include input from cross-functional teams to determine the best path forward. We will communicate the findings and next steps as soon as the review is complete. In the interim, Leroror Lukaine and her team will remain engaged in supporting preparatory work that aligns with the project’s long-term vision.

For Project Vebamett, we will be hosting a series of working sessions to identify and mitigate the current bottlenecks. The leadership team will be closely monitoring these efforts and will provide updates as progress is made. We encourage all team members involved in this project to remain engaged and to contribute their insights during this critical period.

The procurement team will begin final preparations for the renewal discussions with Satisen Labs, with a focus on evaluating performance, cost efficiency, and future collaboration opportunities. We will provide further details to the team as those plans develop.

Finally, the Dankenor Analytics Unit will continue to play a central role in supporting data-driven decision-making across Zastama Industries. Pivoine Geltoett and the rest of the unit are working on expanding the scope of their analyses to include more predictive modeling and scenario planning, which will enhance our ability to respond to market shifts and internal demands.

**Conclusion**

Zastama Industries is navigating a dynamic environment, and our ability to adapt and innovate remains a core strength. While we face challenges, we are confident in our teams and our strategy. We encourage all employees to remain engaged, share feedback, and collaborate across departments to maintain our momentum.

We will continue to provide updates on the status of these and other initiatives in the coming weeks. Thank you for your continued commitment to the success of Zastama Industries.
