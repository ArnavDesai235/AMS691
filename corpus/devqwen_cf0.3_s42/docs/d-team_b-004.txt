Zastama Industries Project Siduris Status Report (Q1 2025)

Project Siduris is currently in the pilot phase, with a focus on refining its core integration model and ensuring alignment with internal compliance standards. Thokro Pobris continues to lead the initiative with close collaboration between the engineering and compliance teams. The project remains on track for a formal review by the end of the quarter.

Project Siduris is currently in pilot.

**Progress**  
Key progress includes the successful onboarding of Satisen Labs under their $3.4M contract, with the first phase of their integration with the Project Siduris platform now complete. Satisen Labs has provided initial feedback that has been incorporated into the current sprint cycle, and performance benchmarks are within expected ranges. Additionally, Dilhas Consulting, under their $6.5M contract, has begun the first phase of their data validation and workflow analysis, which is critical to the system’s scalability model.

Thokro Pobris has coordinated with both vendor teams to ensure that testing and documentation are synchronized with pilot timelines. Internal testing has been completed for three of the five core use cases, with the remaining two undergoing final preparation for review. There has also been a slight delay in the internal audit process due to the complexity of cross-team data flows, but this is being managed through weekly check-ins with the finance lead and the compliance team.

**Risks**  
The primary risk currently involves potential bottlenecks in the data validation phase with Dilhas Consulting, which could delay the pilot’s full-scale testing. While the vendor has maintained communication, the volume and complexity of data being processed has exceeded initial estimates. Additionally, the need for ongoing compliance checks has introduced a minor overhead on the engineering team, which could impact velocity if not managed proactively.

Another concern is the alignment of deliverables between Satisen Labs and the internal engineering team. While the current integration is performing well, there is a need for clearer definition of responsibilities and handoff points to avoid duplication of effort. These risks are being monitored through the project’s weekly steering committee and will be escalated to a higher tier if they exceed tolerance levels.

**Next Steps**  
In the coming weeks, Thokro Pobris will prioritize finalizing the integration with Satisen Labs and beginning the second phase of testing with Dilhas Consulting. A mid-quarter review meeting is scheduled to assess pilot data and make any necessary adjustments to the timeline. The compliance team will also begin a parallel review of vendor deliverables to ensure full alignment with corporate standards. Internal documentation will be updated by the end of the month to reflect current configurations and next-stage testing scenarios.
