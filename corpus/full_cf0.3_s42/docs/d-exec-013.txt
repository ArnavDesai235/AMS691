Kragura Stenmori, as her direct supervisor, provides strategic direction and oversight to ensure that the work aligns with broader organizational goals.

Pethisa Hebroren’s responsibilities include evaluating and implementing tooling solutions that support long-term data archiving, retrieval, and deletion processes. **Collaboration and Next Steps**

All data retention tools managed by Pethisa Hebroren are subject to periodic compliance reviews to ensure they meet internal and external regulatory standards. Any findings from these reviews are communicated to Kragura Stenmori for escalation or resolution as needed.
