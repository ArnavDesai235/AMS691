Meeting Notes: Stonfe Analytics Unit Review and Strategic Planning

---

**Date:** April 12, 2023  
**Attendees:** Krilfia Mukela, finance representative, analytics lead, operations manager, HR representative  

**Discussion Points:**

1. Krilfia Mukela provided an overview of the Stonfe Analytics Unit’s performance since 2019, highlighting key milestones and challenges. She emphasized the growth in team size, which has now reached 30 people, and the continued focus on optimizing data-driven decision-making across departments. The finance representative noted that the cost center the same reference has remained within budgetary constraints for the past two financial quarters.  

2. The analytics lead discussed the unit’s evolving role in supporting both internal operations and external vendor partnerships. There was a general consensus that the unit has been successful in delivering actionable insights, but further alignment with organizational objectives is necessary. The HR representative shared feedback from team members regarding resource allocation and work-life balance, noting that workload distribution has been a recurring concern.  

3. The operations manager highlighted the importance of maintaining a balance between innovation and operational efficiency. Discussions centered on potential process improvements, including the possibility of integrating new tools or refining existing workflows to reduce redundancy. The finance representative reiterated the need to ensure that all initiatives remain aligned with the cost center’s financial boundaries.  

4. Krilfia Mukela raised the topic of cross-departmental collaboration, suggesting that the Stonfe Analytics Unit could play a more active role in supporting strategic projects. The team agreed that better communication channels and structured feedback loops would help in this regard. It was also noted that the unit’s current composition—30 members—allows for flexibility, but further specialization may be beneficial in the coming months.  

**Decisions:**

- The Stonfe Analytics Unit will continue to operate under the cost center UE-8581-F, with a focus on maintaining financial discipline while supporting strategic initiatives.  
- A new collaboration framework will be developed to improve coordination between the analytics unit and other business units.  
- Krilfia Mukela will lead the effort to assess potential workflow optimizations, in consultation with the operations manager and finance representative.  
- The HR representative will facilitate a team feedback session to address concerns regarding resource allocation and workload management.  

**Action Items:**

1. Krilfia Mukela to draft a revised collaboration framework for the Stonfe Analytics Unit by May 1, 2023, incorporating input from all attendees.  
2. Finance representative to provide updated budget projections for the cost center the same reference by April 25, 2023.  
3. Operations manager to coordinate a review of current workflow processes with the analytics lead, with findings due by May 10, 2023.  
4. HR representative to schedule a team feedback session no later than May 5, 2023, and to report on outcomes by May 15, 2023.  
5. All attendees to provide input on the unit’s strategic priorities for the next fiscal year by April 28, 2023.
