Project Thedraman Overview and Dronmeman Beacon Schedule

Project Thedraman is currently under the ownership and supervision of the Nomfotesa Risk Office. As a key initiative for the organization, it is being managed with close attention to ensure alignment with strategic objectives. Kelhupi Rabralor, a member of the Nomfotesa Risk Office, is playing an active role in the oversight and coordination of the project's activities. The procurement order for Project Thedraman is AW-7613-E, and this reference is being used in all relevant documentation for tracking and accountability.

**Dronmeman Beacon Schedule**

The Dronmeman Beacon is a key component of the broader initiative and is scheduled to launch in Q3 FY28. Coordination between the project team and relevant departments is underway to ensure that all dependencies are met and that the launch can proceed as planned. Updates on the progress of the Dronmeman Beacon will be shared with the Nomfotesa Risk Office for continued oversight and alignment with the overall strategy.
