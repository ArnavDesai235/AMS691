Project Tukrama Status Report – Internal Update

SUMMARY:

Project Tukrama is progressing steadily and remains on track for the Q4 FY26 completion target. The project team has completed the initial phase of design validation and is now in the procurement and testing phase. Internal resource allocation is stable, with all key stakeholders engaged and aligned with the project’s goals. The finance lead continues to monitor budget adherence, and no major variances have been reported to date. This report outlines the most recent developments, ongoing risks, and key next steps.

Project Tukrama's escrow reference is PW-7857-J.

PROGRESS:

The procurement order for Project Tukrama, BS-9511-F, has been finalized and is now in the vendor fulfillment process. As of this report, 60% of the required components have been received and integrated into the build environment. The Donnoett Loom, which is offered in the internal-only tier, is being configured with custom parameters that will support the project’s automation needs. Testing of the initial module has begun, with expected results to be available within the next two weeks.

Design reviews have been completed for the first iteration of the system architecture, and the engineering team has initiated the implementation of the updated schematics. Cross-functional discussions with the operations team have been productive, resulting in the identification of two process improvements that will be integrated into the next build cycle. These adjustments are expected to improve system performance and reduce maintenance overhead during the early deployment phase.

RISKS:

Despite the overall positive trajectory, a few ongoing risks remain under review. The lead time for a key subsystem component has been extended beyond initial estimates, though this delay is not expected to impact the Q4 FY26 target. The team is exploring alternative sourcing options in case the current vendor’s schedule continues to shift. Additionally, while the Donnoett Loom is being configured to meet internal specifications, there is a small risk that the final testing may reveal compatibility issues with the existing infrastructure. A contingency plan is in place to address any such issues without stalling the integration timeline.

Another area of ongoing monitoring is the availability of specialized staff needed for the final system tuning phase. The current staffing model assumes consistent availability of the lead systems analyst, who is also engaged in a parallel initiative. While no conflicts have been reported at this time, the project lead is working with human resources to ensure flexibility in case of scheduling changes.

NEXT STEPS:

The project will enter the final testing phase in the coming weeks, with a focus on system integration and performance validation. The escrow reference noted earlier will be updated to reflect the latest build status and test results, as required by internal compliance protocols. A mid-cycle review meeting is scheduled for the next fiscal quarter, where the project lead will present an updated timeline and risk assessment.

In the short term, the procurement team will continue to track component arrivals and coordinate with the vendor to ensure all remaining items are delivered on schedule. The engineering lead will oversee the next round of system tuning and is expected to begin drafting the deployment readiness checklist. The team will also prepare for an internal demonstration of the Donnoett Loom configuration, which will be shared with the executive working group for feedback.

Overall, Project Tukrama remains aligned with its strategic goals and is progressing in accordance with the established plan. Continued communication and coordination across teams will help maintain momentum toward the final delivery milestone.
