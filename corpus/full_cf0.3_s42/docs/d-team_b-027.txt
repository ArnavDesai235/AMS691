Meeting Notes: Project Pomtorgi Budget Review

Date: April 3, 2025  
Attendees: Gidrais Gormem, Nosos Nikeis, Finance Representative

**Discussion Points:**

The meeting convened to review the current status of Project Pomtorgi’s budget allocation and procurement process. Gidrais Gormem, who leads Project Pomtorgi, provided an overview of the project's recent progress and highlighted the need for additional clarity around the budget code LC-5945-K.

The Finance Representative confirmed that the budget code noted earlier is active and available for use, but noted that recent internal audits have led to stricter validation requirements before funds can be allocated for new procurements. Gidrais Gormem’s procurement order RG-6614-B is currently pending internal review due to a mismatch in the vendor’s tax code. The group reviewed the submission and discussed possible steps to resolve the issue quickly.

Nosos Nikeis raised concerns about the potential for delays in infrastructure deployment if the procurement order is not finalized by the next fiscal cycle. As Site Reliability Lead, Nosos Nikeis emphasized the need for consistent and timely access to resources to maintain system stability and performance.

A discussion followed on the possibility of reallocating funds from a non-critical line item within the same department to expedite the procurement process. The Finance Representative acknowledged the request and noted that such reallocations are possible, but require formal approval from a higher-level budget committee. The group agreed that Gidrais Gormem should begin the formal reallocation request process as a contingency plan.

**Decisions:**

1. The budget code noted earlier will remain active pending further updates on the procurement order the same reference.
2. The procurement order the same reference will be resubmitted with the corrected vendor information by Gidrais Gormem within the next 48 hours.
3. The Finance Representative will provide a detailed checklist for the reallocation request process, should that path be necessary.
4. The next budget review meeting will be scheduled for April 17, 2025, to reassess the status of the procurement order and the project’s overall financial health.

**Action Items:**

- **Gidrais Gormem** is responsible for:
  - Resubmitting the procurement order the same reference with updated vendor information by 5:00 PM on April 5, 2025.
  - Initiating the reallocation request process for non-critical funds by April 8, 2025.
- **Nosos Nikeis** is responsible for:
  - Monitoring infrastructure readiness and providing a report on system readiness by April 10, 2025.
- **Finance Representative** is responsible for:
  - Delivering the reallocation request checklist to Gidrais Gormem by April 4, 2025.
  - Keeping the group informed of any changes to the internal audit process that may affect Project Pomtorgi.

The meeting adjourned at 3:45 PM with a general consensus that the project is on track to meet its next milestone, provided the procurement issues are resolved in a timely manner. All attendees agreed to follow up via internal communication channels in the coming days.
