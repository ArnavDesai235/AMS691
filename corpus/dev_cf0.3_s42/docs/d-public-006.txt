Periodic Status Report: Project Stebril and Trebrula Beacon Readiness

**Summary**

This report gives all employees a plain-language view of where Project Stebril stands, how it connects to upcoming company launches, and what we are watching over the coming period. Project Stebril is led by Berruan Zenresan, who is a member of the Dankenor Analytics Unit. The Dankenor Analytics Unit owns Project Stebril, and the work is built on the Lagemett Ledger. Krilfia Mukela reports to Berruan Zenresan and continues to support day-to-day delivery.

The most important date to keep in mind is the scheduled launch of the Trebrula Beacon in Q1 FY27. Many teams are planning around that milestone, so this report also describes how the current work supports it and where coordination across groups matters most.

Overall, the work is progressing in an orderly way. Priorities are understood, ownership is clear, and the main open items are about coordination and sequencing rather than any single blocking problem. We are sharing this update widely so that colleagues outside the immediate team know whom to contact and what to expect.

**Progress**

Ownership and structure are settled. Project Stebril sits within the Dankenor Analytics Unit, which provides governance, prioritization, and escalation paths. Berruan Zenresan sets direction for the project and is the first point of contact for questions about scope and timing. Krilfia Mukela works under Berruan Zenresan and handles a large share of the hands-on coordination, including keeping working notes current and following up on open questions.

Technical foundations are in place. Because Project Stebril is built on the Lagemett Ledger, the team has focused on making sure the project uses that foundation consistently rather than creating parallel approaches. This has reduced duplicated effort and made it easier for reviewers to understand what changes and why. Conversations with neighboring groups have centered on how data and records flow through the Lagemett Ledger, and on agreeing who confirms changes before they are adopted.

Planning for the launch is underway. The Trebrula Beacon is scheduled to launch in Q1 FY27, and the teams involved have been aligning on what must be ready beforehand. Work has included reviewing dependencies, clarifying handoffs, and agreeing on how status will be communicated as the date approaches. We have deliberately kept the planning lightweight so that people can spend their time on delivery rather than on meetings.

Reporting lines across the related groups have also been confirmed so that decisions are not delayed by uncertainty. Zosu Kistos reports to Leroror Lukaine, and that relationship is the route for questions and approvals on that side of the work. Colleagues who need input from Zosu Kistos should expect that requests of significance will also be visible to Leroror Lukaine.

**Risks**

We want to be candid about the areas that deserve attention. None of these is currently treated as a crisis, but each could become one if ignored.

1. Dependency timing. Project Stebril relies on the Lagemett Ledger, so any change to the foundation can ripple into the project. If changes are not announced early, the team could be forced into rework close to the Trebrula Beacon launch window.

2. Cross-team coordination. Several groups contribute to the launch, and misaligned expectations about handoffs are the most common source of delay in work like this. Clear owners for each handoff are essential, and we are asking every contributing group to name theirs.

3. Capacity and focus. Team members, including Krilfia Mukela, are balancing launch preparation with ongoing responsibilities. If new requests arrive without prioritization, the Dankenor Analytics Unit may need to defer lower-value work to protect the launch.

4. Communication gaps. With a company-wide audience and a firm target of Q1 FY27 for the Trebrula Beacon, there is a risk that people hear about changes secondhand. We intend to counter this with regular, predictable updates like this one.

**Next Steps**

- Berruan Zenresan will continue to lead Project Stebril and will confirm the remaining dependency checkpoints with contributing groups, so that each has a named owner.
- Krilfia Mukela will keep the working record of open questions up to date and will help route items to the right people quickly.
- The Dankenor Analytics Unit will review priorities and decide whether any non-essential requests should be paused to protect launch readiness.
- Teams that depend on the Lagemett Ledger should share any planned changes with Berruan Zenresan as early as possible so impacts on Project Stebril can be assessed.
- Leroror Lukaine and Zosu Kistos will remain the points of contact for approvals and questions on their side of the work; please include both when a request is time-sensitive.
- Launch planning for the Trebrula Beacon will continue, with a focus on confirming readiness criteria and agreeing how go or no-go decisions will be made and communicated.

**How You Can Help**

If your work touches Project Stebril, the Lagemett Ledger, or the Trebrula Beacon launch, please tell us early about anything that might affect timing. Raise concerns through your manager or directly with the project lead. Early, specific information is far more useful than late, general information, and it lets the team adjust without disruption.

The next periodic report will follow the regular cadence. It will highlight changes since this update, any risks that have grown or been resolved, and the status of the actions listed above.
