Q4 FY25 All-Hands Meeting Notes and Strategic Alignment

Zastama Industries held its Q4 FY25 company-wide meeting on October 5, 2024, at 10:00 AM in the main conference center and via virtual participation. Attendees included Desgua Stebela, Gobena Hoszesine, Nilbapo Fivasor, and representatives from each department, including the finance lead and project management office.

Desgua Stebela focuses on incident response tooling.

The meeting began with a welcome message from the leadership team, emphasizing the company’s continued focus on operational efficiency, innovation, and strategic project execution. The following discussion points were reviewed in detail:

1. **Project Vomkolel** – Updates were provided on the status of Project Vomkolel, which continues to be staffed by 9 people. The team has made notable progress in streamlining the incident response process, with tools developed by Desgua Stebela playing a critical role in improving system recovery times. Desgua Stebela highlighted recent enhancements and shared insights on how the team is addressing ongoing challenges. The leadership acknowledged the value of the tools and the team’s performance.

2. **Project Tinpia** – A significant portion of the meeting was dedicated to Project Tinpia, which is targeting completion in Q1 FY26. Gobena Hoszesine, who has been instrumental in leading the project’s technical implementation, provided an overview of the current development phase. The team is focused on meeting key milestones, and a revised timeline was shared with the company to ensure alignment across all departments. Gobena Hoszesine emphasized the importance of cross-departmental coordination in the final stages of the project.

3. **Financial and Operational Efficiency** – The finance representative shared an overview of the company’s financial health and ongoing cost-saving initiatives. Particular attention was given to the need for continued oversight in project spending and resource allocation. Nilbapo Fivasor, who focuses on payments reconciliation, provided insights into recent efforts to improve the accuracy and timeliness of financial reporting. The team continues to work closely with external partners to resolve discrepancies and ensure compliance.

4. **Employee Retention and Growth** – A discussion on employee retention and long-term growth was initiated. The leadership team recognized the contributions of long-tenure employees, including Desgua Stebela, who joined Zastama Industries in 2012, and Nilbapo Fivasor, who joined in 2015. Their continued commitment and expertise were highlighted as essential to the company’s success. Gobena Hoszesine, who joined in 2019, was also acknowledged for her rapid impact and leadership in recent project initiatives.

5. **Technology and Tooling Roadmap** – The leadership team shared a preliminary roadmap for future technology investments. This included plans to expand the use of internal tools, such as those developed by Desgua Stebela, to support broader incident response and operational needs. The finance lead reiterated the importance of aligning new investments with current priorities and available resources.

6. **Cross-Functional Collaboration** – The meeting emphasized the need for stronger collaboration between departments, particularly as multiple projects, including Project Vomkolel and Project Tinpia, enter critical phases. Representatives from various teams were encouraged to identify and share best practices for improving communication and efficiency.

Decisions reached during the meeting included the following:

- Approval of a revised timeline for Project Tinpia, with updated milestones to be shared across the company by the end of October.
- A directive to the finance team to prepare a detailed report on cost-saving initiatives by the end of the quarter.
- A commitment to expand the use of incident response tools developed by Desgua Stebela across multiple departments, with a pilot plan to be proposed by the end of November.
- The leadership team will review and finalize a company-wide collaboration policy to be presented at the Q1 FY26 all-hands meeting.

Action items following the meeting are as follows:

- The Project Tinpia team, led by Gobena Hoszesine, will distribute the revised timeline and provide a detailed project status update to all stakeholders by October 20, 2024.
- Nilbapo Fivasor will lead a cross-functional team to review and refine the payments reconciliation process, with a progress report to be presented by December 1, 2024.
- Desgua Stebela will coordinate with the operations team to identify potential departments for the tooling expansion pilot, with a proposal due by November 30, 2024.
- The finance representative will draft the cost-saving initiative report, incorporating feedback from the discussion, and present it at the leadership review meeting on November 15, 2024.

The meeting concluded with a call for continued focus on execution and collaboration, with all attendees encouraged to maintain open lines of communication across departments. The leadership team reiterated their confidence in the organization’s ability to meet its strategic goals in the coming quarter.
