Project Update and Resource Allocation Meeting Notes

DATE: 2023-10-02  
ATTENDEES: Kestoen Pismiett, Getaine Hebruen, finance representative

DISCUSSION POINTS:

Kestoen Pismiett led the discussion on Project Dremtona, providing an overview of the project’s current phase and alignment with strategic goals. As the project lead, Kestoen Pismiett emphasized the importance of cross-departmental coordination, particularly with logistics and IT, to ensure timely delivery. Given Kestoen Pismiett’s experience since joining the company in 2019, the team expressed confidence in the current trajectory but noted the need for ongoing risk assessments.

Getaine Hebruen provided a status update on warehouse automation initiatives. She highlighted how automation efforts are interlinked with several projects, including Project Krabehien. Getaine Hebruen noted that the current automation roadmap aligns well with long-term efficiency targets but requires additional infrastructure planning, particularly in the second quarter of 2024.

The finance representative outlined the current budgetary constraints and emphasized the need for clarity on resource requests from both Project Dremtona and Project Krabehien. A concern was raised about the staffing levels of Project Krabehien, which is currently staffed by 20 people. The finance representative requested more granular data on the utilization of this team and how their work contributes to overall KPIs.

Additional discussion centered on the interdependencies between the automation roadmap and the broader organizational goals. The team acknowledged the potential for bottlenecks if infrastructure timelines are not tightly coordinated with project milestones. The need for a clear escalation path for technical roadblocks was also highlighted, with a suggestion to formalize this process in the next phase of planning.

DECISIONS:

1. The team agreed that Kestoen Pismiett will coordinate a cross-departmental risk assessment workshop for Project Dremtona by the end of the month.
2. Getaine Hebruen will draft a revised timeline for warehouse automation integration, to be reviewed at the next meeting.
3. The finance representative will prepare a revised resource allocation model incorporating feedback from the meeting.
