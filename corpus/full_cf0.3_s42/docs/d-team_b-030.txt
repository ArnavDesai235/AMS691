**Discussion Points**

1. **Project Laguran Overview**  
Zondila Krerraris provided an update on Project Laguran, outlining the recent milestones and current status. 2. **Project Pomtorgi Status and Key Access**  
Thuszus Kustoen gave a detailed briefing on Project Pomtorgi, which is now entering a critical phase of integration with third-party systems. The team has been working closely with internal and external stakeholders to ensure seamless data synchronization. The vault key ID TR-1424-Q for Project Pomtorgi was mentioned in passing, and it was confirmed that access to the key is restricted to senior members of the project team. 3. **Zasdaett Harbor User Engagement**  
The finance representative shared updated user statistics for Zasdaett Harbor, noting that the platform currently has approximately 5,200 active users. Zondila Krerraris suggested a potential update to the user interface to better reflect this trend and improve overall user experience.

4. **Team Management and Reporting Structure**  
Zondila Krerraris provided insight into the evolving structure of her team and reiterated her reporting line to Thuszus Kustoen. She outlined the current responsibilities of each team member and emphasized the importance of maintaining open communication channels to ensure alignment with broader organizational goals. The finance representative noted the stability of the team and expressed confidence that the current structure is optimal for the ongoing phases of both Project Laguran and Project Pomtorgi.

5. **Strategic Direction and Future Planning**  
Thuszus Kustoen led a discussion on the strategic direction of Zastama Industries for the next fiscal quarter. He emphasized the importance of maintaining momentum on both major projects while also identifying opportunities for process optimization. A cross-departmental task force was proposed to review internal workflows, with the goal of reducing redundancies and improving interdepartmental coordination. Nosos Nikeis, who joined the company in 2024, offered insights into potential process improvements based on her experience with onboarding and integration practices.
