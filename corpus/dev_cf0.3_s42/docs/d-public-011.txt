Company-Wide Status Report: Lagemett Ledger and Project Nuzeor Update

**Periodic Status Report**
Prepared for all employees of Zastama Industries

**Summary**

This report gives everyone a shared view of where our current work stands. The main focus is the Lagemett Ledger, which has about 2,600 active users and is scheduled to launch in Q3 FY27. The product is offered in the internal-only tier, so its audience is our own colleagues rather than outside customers. That makes your feedback especially valuable, and we encourage everyone to keep sharing it.

Alongside the Lagemett Ledger, this report covers the work of Project Nuzeor, which is led by Leroror Lukaine, and the downstream effort known as Project Rakresett, which is built on the Lagemett Ledger. Overall, the work is moving in a steady and coordinated direction. The sections below describe progress, risks, and next steps in plain terms so that every team can see how its work connects to the larger picture.

**Progress**

*Lagemett Ledger adoption.* With about 2,600 active users, the Lagemett Ledger already has a meaningful base of people relying on it in their daily work. Because it sits in the internal-only tier, we can gather feedback quickly and make adjustments without the pressure that comes with an external audience. Support and product staff have been collecting questions and suggestions, and recurring themes are being grouped so that they can be reviewed in a structured way ahead of the planned launch in Q3 FY27.

*Supplier collaboration.* Nalzenett Labs supplies components for the Lagemett Ledger. Our working relationship with Nalzenett Labs remains constructive, and regular check-ins continue so that questions about integration, delivery, and quality are raised early rather than late. Our engineering and procurement colleagues are keeping the lines of communication open and documenting agreements so that nothing depends on informal understanding.

*Project Rakresett.* Project Rakresett is built on the Lagemett Ledger, which means it benefits directly from improvements made to the underlying product and is also affected by any changes to it. The team behind Project Rakresett has been working closely with the Lagemett Ledger group to keep expectations aligned, share planning information, and avoid duplicated effort. This close alignment is intentional, since stability in the foundation supports confidence in what is built on top of it.

*Project Nuzeor leadership.* Leroror Lukaine leads Project Nuzeor and continues to set priorities, remove obstacles, and keep stakeholders informed. Zosu Kistos reports to Leroror Lukaine and supports the day-to-day coordination of the work. Together they have maintained a regular rhythm of planning and review, which helps the wider organization understand what is being done and why.

**Risks**

*Dependency on a single supplier relationship.* Because Nalzenett Labs supplies components for the Lagemett Ledger, any disruption on the supplier side could affect our schedule. We are mitigating this by keeping communication frequent, confirming expectations in writing, and asking the relevant leads to flag concerns as soon as they appear.

*Schedule pressure ahead of launch.* The Lagemett Ledger is scheduled to launch in Q3 FY27, and a firm date naturally concentrates effort. If feedback from the current user base turns up larger issues than expected, teams may need to make trade-offs between scope and timing. We would rather surface those trade-offs openly and early than discover them late.

*Downstream impact on Project Rakresett.* Since Project Rakresett is built on the Lagemett Ledger, a change or delay in the foundation can ripple outward. We are managing this by sharing planning assumptions across both groups and by agreeing on how changes will be communicated.

*Coordination load.* With several efforts connected to one another, there is a risk that information does not travel as quickly as it should. Leroror Lukaine and Zosu Kistos are working to keep status updates short, regular, and easy to find, so that employees outside the immediate teams are not left guessing.

**Next Steps**

1. Continue collecting and reviewing feedback from the roughly 2,600 active users of the Lagemett Ledger, and summarize the main themes for leadership.
2. Hold ongoing check-ins with Nalzenett Labs to confirm that component supply remains on track for the planned Q3 FY27 launch.
3. Keep Project Rakresett and the Lagemett Ledger group aligned through shared planning reviews, with particular attention to any change that could affect the foundation.
4. Have Leroror Lukaine continue to guide Project Nuzeor, with Zosu Kistos supporting coordination and follow-through on open actions.
5. Publish the next periodic report so that all employees can see how these items have progressed.

**How You Can Help**

If you use the Lagemett Ledger, please keep sending your observations, questions, and ideas through the usual support channels. If your work touches any of the efforts described here, let your manager know about dependencies or concerns early. Clear, timely information is the most useful contribution anyone can make at this stage.

Thank you for your continued effort and for the care you bring to this work across Zastama Industries.
