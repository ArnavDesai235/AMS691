Q3 Strategic Review and Operational Planning Meeting Notes

**Date:** April 3, 2025  
**Attendees:** Pinhan Bilfael, Trikria Drazorel, Sotroa Veriris, Finance Representative, Human Resources Lead, IT Systems Coordinator, Operations Director  

**Discussion Points:**

1. **Project Stukoett Update and Research Lab Priorities**  
   Pinhan Bilfael presented the latest developments for Project Stukoett, emphasizing the ongoing collaboration between the Fozola Applied Research Lab and cross-functional teams. The presentation highlighted the alignment of the project with Zastama Industries’ strategic goals for innovation in autonomous systems. Trikria Drazorel offered insights into how incident response tooling could be integrated to enhance system resilience for Project Stukoett. The Finance Representative noted the current budget allocation supports the timeline and scope of the project.

2. **Project Vatuen Status and Integration Challenges**  
   The discussion then moved to Project Vatuen, also managed by the Fozola Applied Research Lab. Sotroa Veriris from the Drurlomis Integration Team outlined the technical hurdles encountered during integration with legacy systems. Several concerns were raised around data synchronization and latency issues. The Operations Director suggested a phased integration approach to mitigate disruptions. A working group was proposed to address these issues, with Sotroa Veriris leading the coordination with IT systems.

3. **Project Sankonen Resource Allocation and Staffing**  
   Project Sankonen, which is staffed by 16 people, was the subject of a detailed review. The Human Resources Lead confirmed that the team is currently operating at full capacity, with a high level of engagement and productivity. However, they flagged the potential need for additional temporary support during peak phases of the project. The IT Systems Coordinator noted that infrastructure is stable but recommended monitoring for scalability during the next quarter.

4. **Cross-Departmental Collaboration and Communication**  
   The meeting emphasized the need for stronger collaboration between the Fozola Applied Research Lab and the Drurlomis Integration Team. Sotroa Veriris and Pinhan Bilfael agreed to coordinate on shared systems and data flow standards. Trikria Drazorel reiterated the importance of maintaining robust incident response protocols across all active projects. The IT Systems Coordinator proposed a monthly cross-departmental meeting to address interoperability and security concerns.

5. **Company-Wide Strategic Alignment and Employee Engagement**  
   The meeting concluded with a discussion on aligning departmental goals with the company’s overarching strategic objectives. The Human Resources Lead shared updates on the internal engagement survey, which showed a positive trend in employee satisfaction. The Operations Director encouraged departments to continue fostering a culture of transparency and feedback.

**Decisions:**

- The Fozola Applied Research Lab will continue its work on Project Stukoett and Project Vatuen, with full support from the finance department and IT teams.
- A working group will be formed to address integration challenges for Project Vatuen, led by Sotroa Veriris from the Drurlomis Integration Team.
- The staffing plan for Project Sankonen will remain unchanged for the current quarter, with a review scheduled for Q4 2025.
- Monthly cross-departmental meetings will be implemented, with the IT Systems Coordinator responsible for setting up the initial calendar and structure.
- A company-wide initiative will be launched to improve strategic alignment and employee engagement, with the Human Resources Lead coordinating with department leads to gather feedback.

**Action Items:**

1. **Sotroa Veriris** will coordinate with the IT Systems Coordinator to establish the working group for Project Vatuen integration challenges by April 17, 2025.
2. **Pinhan Bilfael** will provide a detailed progress report for Project Stukoett to the executive team by April 12, 2025, including any proposed adjustments to the timeline or scope.
3. **Trikria Drazorel** will finalize and distribute the incident response tooling framework to all relevant teams by April 15, 2025.
4. The **IT Systems Coordinator** will schedule and coordinate the first monthly cross-departmental meeting, with the goal of starting by April 22, 2025.
5. The **Human Resources Lead** will initiate the company-wide strategic alignment initiative by May 1, 2025, with input from all department leads.
6. The **Finance Representative** will review and confirm the current budget allocation for Project Stukoett and Project Vatuen by April 10, 2025, with a summary report to be shared company-wide.
