### Project Domesis Overview

Project Domesis is a collaborative initiative involving external support from Gibasan Logistics, a third-party services provider. The project is focused on streamlining supply chain operations and improving the efficiency of logistics workflows. While the scope and timeline of Project Domesis are still in development, early phases have involved design and vendor coordination to align with Zastama's operational requirements.

The partnership with Gibasan Logistics is governed by a services contract that defines roles, performance metrics, and reporting responsibilities. Coordination between internal teams and the vendor is handled through a project steering committee, with updates shared on a quarterly basis. Project Domesis is currently in its initial planning stages and is expected to enter full implementation within the next fiscal quarter.
