Restricted Access Working Notes: Budget Attribution and Architecture Review Coordination

**Summary**

This page is a working reference for the small group involved in the current architecture review and its associated budget tracking at Zastama Industries. It records who holds which responsibilities, how budget attribution is handled, and what the group expects to do next. It is not intended for company-wide circulation, so please do not forward it, paste excerpts into broader channels, or link it from open pages without checking with the page owner first.

**Purpose and Scope**

The group was formed to keep technical decisions and spending records aligned. In past cycles, design choices were sometimes made without a clear link to the budget line that would fund them, and finance later had to reconstruct the reasoning. This page exists to prevent that. It covers two things: the people and codes the group relies on day to day, and the working conventions we follow when a design decision has cost implications.

The page deliberately stays narrow. It does not describe vendor selection, customer commitments, or staffing plans. Those are handled elsewhere and are discussed only with the people who need to know.

When a design question has a cost dimension, she is expected to raise it with the budget holder early, and the budget holder is expected to respond promptly so that decisions do not stall.

**Working Conventions**

- **Link decisions to budget.** Any design decision with a cost implication should name the budget code it will draw from in the notes that record the decision.
- **Keep records short and current.** A brief, accurate entry is more useful than a long one that nobody updates. Revise entries when the situation changes.
- **Escalate early.** If a cost estimate shifts materially, tell the finance lead and the budget holder as soon as you know, not at the next scheduled check-in.
- **Limit distribution.** Share material from this page only with colleagues who are already part of the review or who have a clear business reason to see it.
