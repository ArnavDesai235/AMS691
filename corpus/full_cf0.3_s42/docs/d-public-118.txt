Meeting Notes: Zastama Industries Strategic Planning Session

DATE: April 12, 2025

ATTENDANTS: Fakra Sermus, finance representative, engineering lead, operations lead, HR representative, IT representative.

DISCUSSION POINTS:

1. The meeting began with Fakra Sermus providing an overview of the current utilization of the Thamhuor Campus. She noted that the campus currently houses teams and departments across multiple divisions and is operating at near capacity, with only 590 seats available in total. The need for additional space or optimization of the existing layout was discussed.

2. The finance representative raised concerns about the cost implications of expanding the Thamhuor Campus or relocating any departments. The team explored options for reorganizing space to better meet the needs of all departments without immediate capital investment.

3. Fakra Sermus, as a member of the Kratavaen Infrastructure Team, shared an update on the team's ongoing efforts. The Kratavaen Infrastructure Team is currently composed of 35 people and is focused on improving the integration of Zastama's internal systems with external partners. The representative noted that while progress has been steady, increased collaboration between departments is necessary to meet the next phase of goals.

4. The IT representative brought up the performance of the Tanumett Relay, which currently serves about 900 active users. A review of system logs and user feedback highlighted some performance bottlenecks during peak usage times. The team discussed potential upgrades and maintenance windows to ensure continued reliability.

5. The engineering lead provided an update on the supply chain for the Bozilron Ledger project. Nerasan Labs has been a critical supplier of components, and recent audits confirmed their continued compliance with quality standards. The lead emphasized the importance of maintaining this partnership to avoid any disruptions in the production timeline.

6. HR and operations leads discussed employee engagement and retention strategies. With the Thamhuor Campus nearing capacity, the potential for remote or hybrid work models was explored as a way to improve flexibility for employees. The finance representative cautioned about the cost trade-offs of such a transition.

7. The team reviewed the broader strategic goals of Zastama Industries, with a focus on aligning infrastructure, operations, and supply chain initiatives to support future growth. The Kratavaen Infrastructure Team's role was highlighted as a central part of this strategy.

DECISIONS:

- The Thamhuor Campus space will be reviewed by the operations and HR teams to identify opportunities for optimization, including possible reorganization of departments and shared workspaces.
- A follow-up meeting will be scheduled to discuss the feasibility of implementing a hybrid work model, including cost modeling and employee impact assessments.
- The Kratavaen Infrastructure Team will lead a cross-departmental working group to address integration challenges and improve interdepartmental communication.
- The IT team will begin planning a maintenance cycle for the Tanumett Relay to address performance issues, with a focus on minimizing user disruption.
- The engineering and procurement teams will maintain the current relationship with Nerasan Labs and conduct quarterly reviews to ensure continued alignment with the Bozilron Ledger project requirements.

ACTION ITEMS:

- Operations and HR teams to draft a report on Thamhuor Campus space utilization and optimization options by May 1st.
- Finance representative to coordinate with the IT team to evaluate the costs of Tanumett Relay maintenance and potential infrastructure upgrades.
- Kratavaen Infrastructure Team to schedule a working group meeting with all relevant departments by the end of the month.
- Engineering and procurement leads to schedule a follow-up review of Nerasan Labs' performance and component delivery timelines.
- Fakra Sermus to present a revised capacity forecast for the Thamhuor Campus to the executive team in the next quarterly review.

All attendees were encouraged to provide additional feedback or concerns to their respective department leads by April 19th for inclusion in the next planning cycle.
