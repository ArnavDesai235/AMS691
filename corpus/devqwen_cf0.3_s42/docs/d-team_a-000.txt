Strategic Planning and Operational Review Meeting Notes

DATE: 28 April 2025  
ATTENDEES: Liluan Nemlurel (Demand Forecasting Lead), Operations Director, Finance Representative, Renzer Field Office Manager

The meeting began with a review of current operations at the Renzer Field Office, which has a capacity for 240 seats and is associated with audit finding ID TM-1841-G. The Operations Director highlighted recent challenges in resource allocation and utilization at the site, noting the need for improved forecasting to align with demand projections.

Liluan Nemlurel presented an overview of the demand forecasting models currently in use, emphasizing the importance of aligning predictions with seasonal and project-specific fluctuations. She noted that the models are being adjusted to accommodate the upcoming Feltarel Relay launch, which is scheduled for Q4 FY26. The Finance Representative expressed concern about budget constraints and potential underutilization of the Renzer Field Office if current planning does not evolve to support scalable operations.

The team then discussed the implications of the audit finding the same reference. The Renzer Field Office Manager provided an update on corrective measures being taken in response to the audit, stating that internal processes are being reviewed to ensure full compliance with operational standards. The Operations Director suggested a cross-functional review of the audit findings to identify any systemic issues that may affect other sites.

A review of the Feltarel Relay project timeline followed, with a focus on ensuring that the team is prepared for the Q4 FY26 launch. Liluan Nemlurel reiterated the need for precise forecasting to support the project’s resource needs during the critical ramp-up phase. The Finance Representative agreed to provide additional data on expected costs for staffing and infrastructure in the lead-up to the launch.

The meeting then turned to the topic of workforce planning, with a particular focus on how the Renzer Field Office can be better utilized. The Operations Director proposed a scenario-based planning approach, which would allow for greater flexibility in deploying staff based on real-time demand signals. Liluan Nemlurel supported this idea, offering to refine the demand forecasting models to incorporate scenario-based variables.

A decision was made to form a working group consisting of the Operations Director, Liluan Nemlurel, and the Finance Representative to develop a revised planning framework that addresses the audit findings and supports the launch of the Feltarel Relay. The group will also explore options for optimizing the use of the Renzer Field Office’s full capacity of 240 seats.

Action items resulting from the meeting include:
- The working group to draft a revised planning framework within the next four weeks.
- Liluan Nemlurel to update the demand forecasting models to include scenario-based variables, with a timeline of eight weeks.
- The Finance Representative to prepare a cost-benefit analysis of different staffing and infrastructure scenarios for the Feltarel Relay, due in six weeks.
- The Renzer Field Office Manager to coordinate with the audit team to provide a status update on corrective actions related to the same reference by the end of May.

The meeting concluded with a commitment to maintain regular communication and to reconvene in six weeks to review progress on the action items. Zastama Industries remains focused on ensuring that all operational and project planning activities are aligned with corporate goals and compliance standards.
