Ronembu Kestazel's work centers on identity and access tooling, with a particular focus on improving the security and efficiency of user authentication processes. Recent updates include the successful implementation of a new multi-factor authentication protocol. This milestone has been reviewed internally and is currently under testing by a small group of subject matter experts. Ronembu Kestazel has also initiated a process to align Zastama's IAM policies with industry best practices, including a review of password policies and access permissions. Tolmael Zespereor’s efforts are focused on fleet telemetry, specifically the enhancement of data gathering and analysis capabilities for remote monitoring systems. Both teams are actively working to mitigate these risks through phased implementation and cross-functional coordination.

Ronembu Kestazel will finalize the updated IAM policies for broader distribution. Meanwhile, Tolmael Zespereor will prepare the data retention strategy for stakeholder review and begin drafting a plan for full-scale telemetry deployment.
