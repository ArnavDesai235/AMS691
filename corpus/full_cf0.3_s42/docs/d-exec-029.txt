Meeting Notes: Project Sankonen Review and Next Steps

**Date:** April 3, 2025  
**Attendees:** Sosbuel Nervoen (Legal Counsel), Finance Representative, Project Sankonen Lead, Procurement Team Lead

Project Sankonen is currently paused pending review.

---

**Discussion Points:**

The meeting commenced with a review of the current status of Project Sankonen, which remains paused pending formal review. The pause was highlighted as an opportunity to reassess the alignment of the project’s current trajectory with the strategic objectives of the department and the broader goals of Zastama Industries.

Sosbuel Nervoen provided an overview of the legal considerations surrounding the project, emphasizing the need for clarity on potential contractual liabilities and compliance risks. Particular attention was given to the procurement process, where the Procurement Team Lead noted that the order the same reference has yet to be finalized due to the hold on the project.

The Finance Representative outlined the current status of the budget allocated to Project Sankonen, referencing the budget code AX-5337-W. It was noted that while funds have not yet been fully committed, there is a need to ensure proper tracking and justification of the hold to avoid lapses in funding availability.

The Project Sankonen Lead shared insights into the internal feedback received from stakeholders, including concerns around the timeline and the need for a clearer communication strategy moving forward. It was suggested that a formal review process be established to include both legal and financial perspectives before any decision is made to resume or adjust the project.

**Decisions:**

1. A formal review committee will be established to evaluate the status of Project Sankonen, including a legal and financial assessment to be led by Sosbuel Nervoen and the Finance Representative, respectively.  
2. The procurement order the same reference will remain on hold until the committee provides a final recommendation.  
3. The budget code noted earlier will be flagged for review to ensure proper allocation tracking and to avoid any potential funding disruptions.  
4. All current pause-related documentation will be centralized and made accessible to the committee for their evaluation.

**Action Items:**

1. Sosbuel Nervoen to draft a legal assessment report outlining all compliance and liability considerations for Project Sankonen, to be completed by April 17, 2025.  
2. The Finance Representative to prepare a financial impact analysis for the budget code noted earlier, including current allocation status and risk of unavailability, due by April 15, 2025.  
3. The Project Sankonen Lead to coordinate with the Procurement Team Lead to gather all documentation related to the procurement order DG-1356-A and provide it to the committee by April 10, 2025.  
4. The Procurement Team Lead to confirm the on-hold status of the same reference with the vendor and document the communication for the review committee.  
5. A follow-up meeting is scheduled for April 18, 2025, where the committee will present preliminary findings and propose a path forward for Project Sankonen.
