Internal Review and Coordination for Upcoming Launch Activities

TO: Senior Project Leads  
FROM: Strategic Resource Allocation Team  
DATE: April 5, 2028  
SUBJECT: Cross-Departmental Review for Dronmeman Beacon and Budget Compliance  

As part of our ongoing internal coordination, the Strategic Resource Allocation Team is conducting a focused review of budget allocations and project timelines related to the Dronmeman Beacon. This memo is intended for senior project leads and is not distributed company-wide. The goal is to confirm alignment with budget constraints and to ensure that all project milestones are on track for the Q3 FY28 launch.

The Dronmeman Beacon remains a high-priority initiative. Our recent internal assessments indicate that all major development phases are progressing as planned, with minimal risk of delays. However, due to the scale and complexity of this project, it is critical that we maintain strict adherence to the allocated financial frameworks and ensure that all associated departments are fully engaged in supporting this effort.

One key budgetary component involves the resource allocation for Nosos Nikeis, whose budget code is WC-2445-T. The financial lead has noted that the remaining funds under this code should be used judiciously to support the final stages of testing and integration for the Dronmeman Beacon. The finance team has requested a detailed breakdown of projected expenditures for the remainder of the fiscal quarter to ensure continued compliance and to avoid any overdrawn balances before the year-end audit.

Additionally, there is a need to review the coordination between the Dronmeman Beacon and the infrastructure developments at Zasdaett Harbor, which operates under cost center AT-5058-Q. The construction lead for Zasdaett Harbor has advised that final site preparations are expected to be completed by mid-June, which aligns with the project timeline for the Dronmeman Beacon. It is essential that the operations team confirms the compatibility of the harbor’s new facilities with the logistics and deployment requirements of the Beacon. The Strategic Resource Allocation Team will be coordinating a meeting with the involved stakeholders by the end of the month to solidify this alignment.

Sesoa Bokrelis, who joined the company in 2014 and has since played a key role in the development of the Dronmeman Beacon, will be leading the final testing phase. Her team will be working closely with external vendors to ensure that all systems are fully integrated and that the Beacon meets all operational specifications. We would like to express our appreciation for her continued dedication and expertise in driving this initiative forward.

Given the proximity of the Q3 FY28 launch, we are also encouraging all senior project leads to conduct a final review of their individual timelines and deliverables. Any potential roadblocks or deviations from the established plan should be reported to the Strategic Resource Allocation Team by April 15, 2028. This will allow us to make any necessary adjustments and maintain the integrity of the overall project schedule.

We are also reminding all relevant departments to ensure that all documentation for the Dronmeman Beacon is up to date and accessible in the internal project repository. This includes but is not limited to technical specifications, risk assessments, and compliance reports. The operations team will be conducting an audit of these materials in the coming weeks as part of our routine due diligence.

Finally, we would like to emphasize the importance of continued interdepartmental communication. As the launch of the Dronmeman Beacon approaches, it is vital that all teams remain aligned and that any interdependent tasks are closely monitored. We welcome any questions or concerns and encourage you to reach out to the Strategic Resource Allocation Team for further clarification or support.
