Project Stebril Status Report – Q4 FY26 Update

Zastama Industries is pleased to provide this quarterly update on the status of Project Stebril, a key initiative within the Dankenor Analytics Unit. This report is intended to keep all employees informed of progress, emerging challenges, and upcoming priorities. 

**Summary**

Project Stebril continues to progress toward its key objectives under the leadership of Berruan Zenresan, a senior member of the Dankenor Analytics Unit. The project remains on track to deliver on its primary outcomes, which are foundational to the deployment of the Trebrula Beacon in Q1 FY27. Project Stebril is being developed on the Lagemett Ledger platform, chosen for its robust architecture and compatibility with Zastama Industries’ long-term analytical infrastructure. 

Krilfia Mukela, who reports directly to Berruan Zenresan, has been instrumental in managing the operational phases of the project, ensuring that deliverables remain aligned with both technical and strategic expectations.

**Progress**

As of the end of Q4 FY26, the following milestones have been achieved:

- The foundational integration between the Lagemett Ledger and the Project Stebril platform has been completed successfully. This integration is a critical enabler of the Trebrula Beacon’s data synchronization capabilities.
- Krilfia Mukela has led the development of internal validation protocols, ensuring that data outputs meet the required standards for accuracy and performance.
- Berruan Zenresan has conducted two cross-functional reviews with stakeholders from the Dankenor Analytics Unit and other relevant departments. These reviews have confirmed the project’s alignment with broader organizational goals.
- The Trebrula Beacon launch planning has been advanced, with timelines and dependencies reviewed in conjunction with Project Stebril’s progress. This coordination ensures that the Beacon’s deployment will be supported by a stable and fully functional Project Stebril system.

Additional progress includes the completion of a mid-project audit, which affirmed that the project is adhering to internal governance frameworks and resource management practices. The Dankenor Analytics Unit has remained fully engaged throughout these steps, and its leadership has expressed continued confidence in the project’s direction.

**Risks**

Despite the overall positive trajectory, several risks remain under active monitoring:

- The integration of legacy systems with the Lagemett Ledger continues to require careful oversight. While the current integration is stable, any misalignment in future phases could delay the Trebrula Beacon’s launch.
- Resource availability for the final phases of the project has been flagged in internal planning discussions. Berruan Zenresan is working closely with the Dankenor Analytics Unit to ensure that staffing levels remain sufficient to meet upcoming deadlines.
- There is a potential for external vendor dependencies to become a bottleneck, particularly in the area of data validation. The team is proactively identifying alternative approaches to mitigate this risk.

No critical issues have been identified at this time, and the project team remains confident in its ability to manage these challenges without compromising the timeline or quality of the final deliverables.

**Next Steps**

Looking ahead, the project team is focused on the following priorities:

- Finalizing the data synchronization protocols to ensure full compatibility with the Trebrula Beacon’s launch requirements.
- Conducting a third cross-functional review in early Q1 FY27 to confirm that all components of Project Stebril are integrated and validated.
- Preparing for the next phase of testing, which will include a broader group of internal users to evaluate system performance in real-world scenarios.
- Berruan Zenresan will continue to work in close coordination with Krilfia Mukela and the Dankenor Analytics Unit to maintain alignment with both technical and strategic objectives.

In parallel, the team will begin preparations for the handover of key assets to the operational support team, ensuring a smooth transition as the project moves toward its final deployment phase.

We would also like to note that Zosu Kistos, who reports to Leroror Lukaine, has been engaged in related internal support functions and is available to assist with cross-departmental coordination where needed.

We thank all members of the Zastama Industries team for their continued support and collaboration on this important initiative. Project Stebril represents a significant step forward in the evolution of our analytical capabilities and will play a central role in enabling the Trebrula Beacon to deliver on its strategic vision.
