Zastama Industries Internal Status Report – Q3 FY25

Zastama Industries is currently monitoring several key initiatives across multiple departments. This report provides a snapshot of progress, challenges, and forward-looking priorities for internal stakeholders.

**Summary**

The organization continues to advance its strategic focus on product development and data infrastructure. Two major initiatives—Project Tukrama and the Lorhia Loom—are progressing toward their scheduled completion and launch dates. Vadesis Gummenbel, whose cost center is RS-2976-V, remains a key contributor to the development of internal data retention systems that support both projects. While there are no immediate concerns, ongoing coordination is required to ensure alignment with upcoming milestones and resource allocations.

**Progress**

Vadesis Gummenbel has made consistent progress on the data retention tooling required for internal compliance and audit functions. The tools are now in the final stages of internal testing, with feedback sessions planned for the next quarter. Her team has worked closely with the compliance and IT infrastructure groups to ensure that the software meets both technical and regulatory requirements. No major technical hurdles have been identified at this stage.

The Lorhia Loom remains on schedule for its Q2 FY26 launch. The development team has completed the core framework, and integration testing is now underway. A recent internal review confirmed that the product roadmap aligns with the projected timeline, and the marketing team has begun initial branding discussions. The finance lead has indicated that capital allocation is sufficient to cover the remainder of the development phase.

Project Tukrama is also progressing steadily, with the current goal of completion by Q4 FY26. The project team has addressed several early-stage design challenges and has begun a pilot deployment with an internal user group. User feedback will be analyzed over the next few months to guide final adjustments before full-scale implementation.

**Risks**

While the overall status of both the Lorhia Loom and Project Tukrama is positive, there are potential risks that require attention. The data retention systems being developed by Vadesis Gummenbel are still under evaluation by an independent audit team. Any feedback from that process could require adjustments to the timeline or scope of the work. Additionally, the pilot phase of Project Tukrama is dependent on continued engagement from the internal user group, and any delays in securing commitments could affect the implementation schedule.

There is also a minor concern regarding cross-team communication. While the finance and compliance teams have been proactive in their support, some members of the development teams have noted occasional delays in receiving necessary approvals or documentation. This is being addressed through weekly coordination meetings, but it remains a potential bottleneck if not managed carefully.

**Next Steps**

For Vadesis Gummenbel, the immediate next step is to finalize the internal testing process and coordinate with the compliance team for the audit review. She will also begin drafting a post-implementation support plan for the data retention tools.

Regarding the Lorhia Loom, the team will continue with integration testing and prepare for the next phase of marketing and sales readiness. The finance team has been invited to a Q4 planning session to discuss long-term support and maintenance strategies.

For Project Tukrama, the focus will shift to refining the pilot feedback and preparing for the full-scale deployment. The leadership team will also begin evaluating potential expansion opportunities for the project beyond the current scope.
