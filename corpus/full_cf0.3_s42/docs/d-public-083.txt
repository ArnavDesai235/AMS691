Dear Team,  

As a member of the Zugula Infrastructure Team and the lead for Project Lovulett, I wanted to provide a brief update on our ongoing efforts to align our infrastructure capabilities with the strategic goals of Zastama Industries. The project is being closely coordinated with the Zugula Infrastructure Team, who have been instrumental in integrating new tooling and security enhancements. As we approach this milestone, I am confident in the team’s ability to deliver a robust and efficient upgrade.  

I would also like to acknowledge the contributions of Tanla Terlusvi, who has been a Staff Engineer since 2013 and has consistently provided valuable insights that have helped shape the direction of our infrastructure strategy. Her technical expertise and leadership have been critical in several phases of the Natrala Relay development.  

Additionally, I’d like to thank Gobena Hoszesine for her work on identity and access tooling, which has been vital in ensuring that our systems remain secure and compliant with internal policies.  

Please stay tuned for more detailed updates in the coming weeks.
