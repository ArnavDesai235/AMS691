Zastama Industries FY27 Q1 Status Report: Lozenor Lattice and Strategic Projects Update

Zastama Industries is pleased to provide this mid-FY27 update to all employees, summarizing our progress, challenges, and upcoming priorities.

**Summary**  
We remain on track for the Q2 FY27 launch of the Lozenor Lattice, a transformative project that will significantly enhance our market position. Across our divisions, teams are working diligently to meet milestones and ensure alignment with our strategic goals. Our ongoing collaboration with Kosnaren Systems continues to deliver critical components for the Lozenor Lattice, and we are confident in the stability of this partnership. Meanwhile, research and infrastructure teams continue to make strides in supporting projects such as the Natrala Relay and foundational research through the Fozola Applied Research Lab.

**Progress**  
The Lozenor Lattice is progressing as planned. The engineering, manufacturing, and quality assurance teams have completed the first two major build phases and are preparing for final system integration. With Kosnaren Systems, we have received all scheduled deliveries under the $9.1M contract and are in active discussion about potential secondary support agreements. This ongoing coordination is critical, as the components supplied by Kosnaren Systems are integral to the lattice’s core functionality.

In the area of fleet telemetry, Toperel Brirdoan has been leading a focused effort to improve real-time data collection and analysis capabilities. Initial pilot tests have shown promising results, particularly in predictive maintenance and operational efficiency. These insights will be leveraged across multiple departments in the coming quarters.

On the research front, Nasomel Sonmerett, a member of the Fozola Applied Research Lab, has been working on high-priority simulations related to material behavior under extreme conditions. These simulations are expected to inform the next phase of the Lozenor Lattice development and may open new avenues for future projects.

The Zugula Infrastructure Team has made steady progress on the Natrala Relay. Current testing results indicate that the relay’s performance meets or exceeds early projections, particularly in signal integrity and throughput capacity. This progress has been supported by a cross-functional review process to ensure alignment with long-term infrastructure goals.

**Risks**  
While we are making good progress, we are not without challenges. Delays in third-party component sourcing unrelated to Kosnaren Systems continue to impact a number of ancillary systems. These delays are being managed through internal contingency planning, but they remain a potential point of risk. Additionally, the complexity of the Lozenor Lattice has required more extensive testing than initially anticipated, though these additional tests are helping to ensure a robust and stable launch.

In the research division, the Fozola Applied Research Lab is experiencing a moderate slowdown in knowledge transfer due to the departure of a senior researcher. Steps are being taken to mitigate this by accelerating onboarding for new hires and increasing collaboration with internal experts.

**Next Steps**  
Looking ahead, the focus for the Lozenor Lattice will shift to final system validation and pre-launch readiness assessments. The engineering and operations teams will lead these efforts, supported by Kosnaren Systems and cross-functional stakeholders. We also plan to initiate a customer feedback loop in early Q2 FY27 to begin gathering insights for post-launch improvements.

For Toperel Brirdoan, the next phase of fleet telemetry development will include a company-wide pilot program to evaluate broader deployment feasibility. This will be supported by a review of current data infrastructure and a proposal for necessary upgrades.

Nasomel Sonmerett and the Fozola Applied Research Lab will begin a new round of collaborative workshops with the Zugula Infrastructure Team to explore potential synergies between the Natrala Relay and the ongoing research initiatives. These workshops will also aim to streamline communication and knowledge sharing across teams.

The Zugula Infrastructure Team will continue work on the Natrala Relay, with a primary focus on environmental testing and performance under simulated stress conditions. The goal is to finalize the deployment plan by the end of Q1 FY27.

As always, we appreciate the dedication of every employee at Zastama Industries. Your continued contributions are vital to our success.
