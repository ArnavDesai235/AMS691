Subject: Brapoett Vault Usage and System Updates  
Date: March 17, FY26  

We are pleased to report that the Brapoett Vault continues to see strong adoption across the organization, with approximately 4,400 active users as of the end of FY25. This reflects our commitment to secure and scalable internal systems, and we are currently planning additional enhancements for FY26 that will further improve user experience and system reliability.

As part of these efforts, we are also coordinating with the Drurlomis Integration Team to ensure that the Broltala Loom and related systems such as Project Daslalett are fully supported and integrated with the Brapoett Vault. No immediate changes to user access or functionality are expected, but we will provide further updates as these enhancements come into effect. In the meantime, if you encounter any issues or have suggestions for improvement, please reach out to the IT helpdesk.

Thank you for your continued use and feedback.
