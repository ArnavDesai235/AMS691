Q3 Strategic Review and Operational Planning Meeting Notes

Date: May 2, 2025  
Attendees: Givien Mubren, Bedras Sarkua, Kavemen Hilister, and the finance representative.

Givien Mubren works as a Product Manager.

---

**Discussion Points:**

1. **Operational Status of Vurlekri Annex:**  
   Givien Mubren, based at the Vurlekri Annex, provided an overview of the site’s performance since its opening in 2023. The team has exceeded initial productivity expectations, with a 12% increase in output from the previous quarter. Challenges remain in supply chain coordination, particularly with raw material delivery times. The team is investigating alternative suppliers to reduce dependency on the current logistics partner.

2. **Progress on Project Daslalett:**  
   Givien Mubren also detailed the status of Project Daslalett, which has been operating under a services contract with Hurpuman Partners since the start of the year. The project has encountered delays due to unexpected complexity in integrating legacy systems. Givien Mubren emphasized the importance of maintaining transparency with Hurpuman Partners and has initiated weekly status calls to keep them informed. A risk assessment is currently in progress to identify potential mitigation strategies.

3. **Pozoine Campus Operations:**  
   Bedras Sarkua, based at the Pozoine Campus, reported that the site is functioning within expected performance metrics. The team has completed the quarterly maintenance review and is preparing for the upcoming software rollout. Bedras Sarkua noted that the rollout will require a temporary shift in workflow and requested approval to schedule a training session for all relevant staff.

4. **Company-Wide Coordination and Communication:**  
   Kavemen Hilister, the Chief of Staff, highlighted the need for improved communication between the Vurlekri Annex and the Pozoine Campus. A recent audit revealed a 15% variance in reporting formats, which has led to confusion in consolidated reports. Kavemen Hilister proposed the adoption of a single, standardized template for all operational reports, to be reviewed by the finance lead.

---

**Decisions:**

- A working group will be formed to develop a unified reporting format, with representation from both the Vurlekri Annex and the Pozoine Campus.
- The finance representative will oversee the implementation of the template and ensure alignment with budgeting and forecasting processes.
- Kavemen Hilister will coordinate the working group and schedule the first meeting for May 15, 2025.
- Bedras Sarkua will proceed with the scheduled training session for the Pozoine Campus software rollout, with the final schedule to be shared by May 10, 2025.
- Givien Mubren will provide an updated risk assessment report for Project Daslalett by May 14, 2025, to be shared with Hurpuman Partners and the executive team.

---

**Action Items:**

- Givien Mubren to submit the risk assessment report for Project Daslalett by May 14, 2025.
- Bedras Sarkua to confirm the training schedule and share with the Pozoine Campus team by May 10, 2025.

- The finance representative to review the proposed template and provide feedback by May 12, 2025.
- All attendees to provide feedback on the reporting template by May 13, 2025 to facilitate finalization before the working group meeting.

This meeting was held to ensure alignment across Zastama Industries' key operational sites and projects, with a focus on improving efficiency and communication. The next company-wide meeting is scheduled for July 2025.
