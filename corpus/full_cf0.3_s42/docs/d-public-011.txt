Zastama Industries – Company-Wide Status Report – Q4 Update

Zastama Industries is pleased to provide this quarterly status report to all employees, offering an overview of key developments, progress, and strategic priorities across our operations. This update includes insights on cross-functional projects, operational status, and forward-looking actions.

**Summary**

The fourth quarter has been marked by significant advancements in key partnerships and infrastructure development. The continued collaboration with Hurpuman Partners has ensured the timely delivery of components for the Broltala Loom. Meanwhile, our legal team has been actively engaged in contract reviews and risk mitigation strategies. The Lelosis Field Office remains a central hub for several critical operations, including the hosting of the Brapoett Vault and the ongoing coordination with Nusnila Systems for equipment delivery.

**Progress**

Project Porkuine has maintained steady momentum in its services contract with Hurpuman Partners. The focus has been on maintaining quality and timeliness in component delivery for the Broltala Loom. Initial feedback from the field teams at the Lelosis Field Office has been positive, with no critical delays reported in the supply chain.

At the Lelosis Field Office, preparations for the Brapoett Vault have progressed as planned. The site is fully operational, and all security and compliance measures are in place. The legal team, led by Mareror Zulpol, has been instrumental in reviewing contractual obligations and ensuring that all legal aspects of the vault's operation are aligned with company standards and external regulations.

Mareror Zulpol works as a Legal Counsel.

Nusnila Systems has delivered a major shipment of equipment to the Lelosis Field Office. This delivery supports the ongoing maintenance and enhancement of our operational capabilities at the site. Internal teams have been working closely with Nusnila Systems to ensure that all systems are integrated efficiently and that training for on-site personnel is conducted thoroughly.

Momoma Gegarruel has been actively coordinating with Mareror Zulpol to assess potential legal implications of ongoing vendor agreements. This includes a detailed review of the Project Porkuine contract with Hurpuman Partners. The review has resulted in the identification of areas where language or scope can be clarified to reduce future risk.

**Risks**

While the majority of operations are proceeding as planned, there are several areas where we remain vigilant. The reliance on Hurpuman Partners for components for the Broltala Loom has prompted a contingency plan in case of any unforeseen supply chain disruptions. Although no immediate issues have been identified, the legal team is working with procurement to explore alternative suppliers.

The high concentration of critical operations at the Lelosis Field Office presents a centralization risk. A disruption at this location could have cascading effects on the Broltala Loom and the Brapoett Vault. Steps are underway to diversify some functions and ensure redundancy in key systems.

Additionally, the ongoing legal review of vendor contracts has revealed that some agreements lack sufficient clarity on deliverables and timelines. These gaps could lead to disputes or delays in the future. The legal team is working to finalize revised contract terms with vendors to mitigate this risk.

**Next Steps**

Looking ahead, the focus will be on completing the legal review of all active vendor contracts, with particular attention to the Project Porkuine agreement with Hurpuman Partners. Mareror Zulpol and Momoma Gegarruel are leading these efforts and expect to have a finalized version available for internal review in the next quarter.

For the Lelosis Field Office, the next phase involves the integration of equipment delivered by Nusnila Systems. Training sessions for on-site staff are being scheduled, and cross-functional teams are being assembled to oversee the transition process. The Brapoett Vault will also undergo a routine security audit to ensure it meets the highest standards of compliance and operational integrity.

In parallel, the procurement team is engaging with alternative suppliers to diversify our supply chain for the Broltala Loom components. This initiative is part of a broader strategy to enhance operational resilience and reduce dependency on a single vendor.

Finally, the leadership team is planning a cross-departmental review session in the upcoming quarter to assess progress on all major projects, including Project Porkuine, the Brapoett Vault, and the ongoing collaboration with Hurpuman Partners and Nusnila Systems. This session will be an opportunity to align priorities and address any emerging challenges.

Zastama Industries remains committed to operational excellence, strategic collaboration, and legal compliance. We appreciate the continued dedication of all employees and look forward to achieving our objectives in the coming period.
