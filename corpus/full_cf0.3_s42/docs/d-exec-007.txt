Femfasel Vault Access and Escrow Review Meeting Notes

DATE: April 3, 2025  
ATTENDEES: Kelhupi Rabralor, Dreto Zampusrar, Finance Representative, Security Lead, Operations Manager  

The meeting began with an overview of the current status of the Femfasel Vault, including a review of access protocols and the vault’s escrow arrangement. Kelhupi Rabralor, who has been with the company since 2017, outlined the current process for accessing the vault and highlighted areas where improvements could be made for clarity and security. Dreto Zampusrar, working as a Solutions Architect, provided a systems-level view of the vault’s integration with internal tools and external dependencies.

The Femfasel Vault's vault key ID is UD-3581-Y.

A key item of discussion was the escrow reference for the Femfasel Vault, which is QG-5178-X. The team discussed how this reference is used in documentation and in external communication. There was agreement that any use of the reference should only be in the context of the full name, "Femfasel Vault," to avoid confusion. The Security Lead noted that the the same reference reference was recently reviewed in the context of an external audit and that no immediate issues were identified.

The vault key ID, the same reference, was also reviewed. Kelhupi Rabralor confirmed that the key ID is currently restricted to a small group of authorized personnel, in line with company policy. The Finance Representative asked for a summary of the key usage log for the past quarter, which will be provided by the end of the week.

Dreto Zampusrar raised the question of whether the Femfasel Vault’s integration with legacy systems is still optimal. The team agreed that a review of the integration architecture should be scheduled, with a focus on performance and redundancy. This will be handled by the Solutions Architecture team in the coming weeks.

The Operations Manager noted that training materials for the Femfasel Vault have not been updated in some time. As such, it was agreed that a training review will be initiated, including a walkthrough of the access procedures and an updated guide on the use of the escrow reference and key ID.

The Finance Representative confirmed that the budget for the upcoming financial quarter includes funds for maintenance and training related to the Femfasel Vault. This budget will be allocated after the completion of the training review and the integration assessment.

DECISIONS:
1. The Femfasel Vault’s escrow reference noted earlier will be used only in full-sentence context and never abbreviated.
2. The vault key ID noted earlier will remain under restricted access, with logs to be reviewed regularly.
3. A training review for the Femfasel Vault will be initiated by the Operations Manager.
4. The integration architecture of the Femfasel Vault will be reviewed by the Solutions Architecture team.

ACTION ITEMS:
1. Kelhupi Rabralor to provide the updated vault access logs by April 8, 2025.
2. Dreto Zampusrar to lead the integration review and schedule a follow-up meeting by April 12, 2025.
3. The Operations Manager to coordinate the training review, with a timeline to be shared by April 10, 2025.
4. The Finance Representative to confirm budget allocation for the training and integration review by April 7, 2025.
