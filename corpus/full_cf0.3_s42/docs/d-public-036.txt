Overview of Hurpuman Partners and Related Activities at Zastama Industries

Zastama Industries is monitoring the status and renewal timeline of Hurpuman Partners, a key vendor delivering equipment to the Thamhuor Campus. This page provides an overview of Hurpuman Partners’ involvement, the associated projects, and the roles of key personnel.

---

**Hurpuman Partners Overview**

Hurpuman Partners is a long-standing vendor with Zastama Industries, currently operating under a 18-month master services agreement. The company plays a central role in the delivery of equipment to the Thamhuor Campus, a critical site for several ongoing initiatives. As the agreement is set for renewal in Q4 FY26, relevant teams are being encouraged to begin early discussions to ensure continuity and alignment with Zastama’s operational goals. Project Gadramor, which has a services contract with Hurpuman Partners, is closely tied to these deliverables, and any disruptions could affect the timeline for project milestones.

---

**Project Gadramor and Vendor Support**

Project Gadramor relies on Hurpuman Partners for specific elements of its implementation plan. The project team is in regular communication with Hurpuman Partners to ensure that all necessary equipment is delivered on schedule and meets the technical specifications outlined in the services contract. The project lead has been coordinating with cross-functional teams to assess the potential impact of the upcoming renewal process and to evaluate any adjustments that may be required to the project’s scope or timeline. Internal stakeholders are advised to remain engaged with the project team to stay informed of any developments.

---

**Genfinel Platform Group Involvement**

Nammian Bahoine, a member of the Genfinel Platform Group, is among the key contacts supporting the management of vendor contracts related to Hurpuman Partners. The Genfinel Platform Group is responsible for ensuring that all vendor agreements align with broader platform strategy and compliance standards. Nammian Bahoine is working with procurement and project teams to review the current master services agreement and assess whether any updates or renegotiations are necessary as the renewal approaches.

In addition, the Genfinel Platform Group is coordinating with other departments to evaluate the long-term viability of the partnership and to explore potential improvements in vendor performance and service delivery. These efforts are intended to support the broader strategic goals of Zastama Industries, particularly in relation to the Thamhuor Campus and Project Gadramor.

---

**Thamhuor Campus Operations**

The Thamhuor Campus is currently receiving equipment deliveries from Hurpuman Partners as part of its operational setup. Vadesis Gummenbel, who is based at the Thamhuor Campus, is involved in the on-site coordination of these deliveries. Vadesis Gummenbel is working closely with Hurpuman Partners’ representatives to ensure that all equipment is received in good condition and installed according to schedule. Any delays or issues with the deliveries are being escalated promptly to ensure minimal disruption to ongoing work at the site.

---

**Next Steps and Coordination**

As the renewal date for Hurpuman Partners approaches in Q4 FY26, a number of internal teams are being asked to contribute to the evaluation and decision-making process. These include the Genfinel Platform Group, the Project Gadramor team, and the operations lead at the Thamhuor Campus. A coordination meeting has been scheduled for the upcoming quarter to align priorities and finalize the renewal strategy.

Employees with specific questions or concerns about Hurpuman Partners or related activities are encouraged to reach out to their departmental lead or the project office for guidance. Updates on the status of the master services agreement and any changes to the delivery schedule will be posted on this page as they become available.
