Q2 Strategic Review and Operational Planning Meeting Notes

DATE: March 15, 2024

ATTENDEES: Fongois Bakemdia, Verlaen Dodela, Novoran Trabrelor, Pinduen Gersolel, Liha Medrorel, Vosaett Neldelis, and a finance representative.

DISCUSSION POINTS:

1. Verlaen Dodela led the discussion on the current state of customer onboarding flows. He emphasized the importance of refining these processes to improve customer satisfaction and retention. Fongois Bakemdia, who is based at the Lardoel Campus, provided an overview of recent updates to internal systems used in the onboarding process. Novoran Trabrelor, also located at the Lardoel Campus, raised concerns about integration delays with an external vendor.

2. Pinduen Gersolel, under the supervision of Liha Medrorel, reported on internal process optimization initiatives. These included a review of cross-team communication and resource allocation. Pinduen Gersolel highlighted the need for clearer reporting structures to ensure accountability and timely resolution of bottlenecks.

3. Vosaett Neldelis, who has been with the company since 2021 and is also based at the Lardoel Campus, shared insights from a recent employee engagement survey. The data revealed areas of concern around clarity in role expectations and the need for more internal training. Vosaett Neldelis recommended a quarterly internal training initiative to address these gaps.

4. The finance representative provided a high-level summary of current budget allocations and projected expenses for the remainder of the fiscal year. While no specific figures were shared with all attendees, a general consensus was reached regarding the importance of cost containment and strategic spending in support of customer onboarding and internal process improvements.

5. Additional attention was given to the long-term sustainability of current initiatives. The team acknowledged the need to balance short-term goals with longer-term impacts on team capacity and employee well-being. Risk mitigation strategies for cross-team dependencies were discussed, and it was agreed that regular check-ins should be scheduled to monitor progress and adjust course as needed.

DECISIONS:

1. The team agreed to prioritize streamlining the customer onboarding flows, with Verlaen Dodela leading the effort and Fongois Bakemdia and Novoran Trabrelor supporting the implementation of system upgrades. All three are based at the Lardoel Campus and will coordinate weekly to ensure alignment.

2. Pinduen Gersolel and Liha Medrorel will collaborate on defining a clearer reporting structure for internal teams. The focus will be on establishing roles and responsibilities to improve efficiency and response times.

3. The quarterly internal training initiative proposed by Vosaett Neldelis was approved. The first session is expected to launch in April 2024. Vosaett Neldelis will work with the HR team to design the content and logistics for the training.

ACTION ITEMS:

1. Verlaen Dodela, Fongois Bakemdia, and Novoran Trabrelor will finalize a revised customer onboarding workflow by April 5, 2024. A presentation to the executive team is scheduled for April 12.

2. Pinduen Gersolel will draft a proposal for the new reporting structure by March 29 and present it to Liha Medrorel for feedback.

3. Vosaett Neldelis will coordinate with the HR team to begin planning the first internal training session. A detailed outline and schedule must be submitted by April 3.

4. The finance representative will ensure budget allocations align with these initiatives and provide updated guidance to relevant teams.

5. All attendees are encouraged to share ideas and feedback with their respective team leads in preparation for the next company-wide meeting on May 1. A follow-up internal memo will be circulated to outline expectations for employee engagement moving forward.

The meeting adjourned at 3:45 PM.
