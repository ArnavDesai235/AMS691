Meeting Notes: Q3 Strategic Review and Operational Alignment

Date: April 3, 2025  
Attendees: Nasomel Sonmerett, Hethe Potannuel, Nasgo Kesloren, Rinu Gesolen, Finance Representative, Operations Lead, Equipment Liaison, Project Manager (Project Loloman), Nerasan Labs Liaison, Bekuine Consulting Liaison  

---

**Discussion Points**  

1. **Status of Data Retention Tooling (Fozola Applied Research Lab)**  
Nasomel Sonmerett provided an overview of the current status of the data retention tooling being developed by the Fozola Applied Research Lab. The team is nearing the stabilization phase, with a focus on improving performance under high-volume data scenarios. Nasomel Sonmerett emphasized the need for additional testing environments to simulate real-world conditions, which Hethe Potannuel noted would align with the departmental budget cycle.  

2. **Project Loloman Milestones and Dependencies**  
Rinu Gesolen detailed the progress of Project Loloman, highlighting that the project is on track to meet its next major milestone by the end of April. She noted that recent deliveries from Nerasan Labs have been critical to the project’s momentum and that the Thamhuor Campus will be the primary site for the next phase of field testing. Rinu Gesolen also raised concerns about potential scheduling conflicts with the Bekuine Consulting engagement, requesting a cross-departmental review of resource allocation.  

3. **Nerasan Labs Deliverables and Coordination**  
The Nerasan Labs Liaison confirmed that the next batch of specialized equipment will arrive at the Thamhuor Campus by April 15, 2025. The liaison emphasized the importance of coordination between the Fozola Applied Research Lab and Project Loloman teams to ensure that equipment is deployed effectively and that data integration protocols are in place prior to installation.  

4. **Bekuine Consulting Services and Contract Milestones**  
The Bekuine Consulting Liaison provided a review of the ongoing 24-month master services agreement, noting that all deliverables for the current phase have been met. The liaison also outlined the upcoming scope of work for the next six months, which includes strategic planning for data infrastructure scaling. The Finance Representative was asked to confirm alignment with the broader budget framework and to review any potential cost implications.  

5. **Leadership and Reporting Structure Review**  
Hethe Potannuel shared an overview of the current reporting structure for the Fozola Applied Research Lab, noting that Nasomel Sonmerett’s role is central to the success of the data retention initiative. Hethe Potannuel also reiterated the importance of clear communication channels with Nasgo Kesloren to ensure alignment with overall strategic goals. Nasgo Kesloren acknowledged the need for more frequent cross-departmental check-ins and proposed a monthly update schedule.  

6. **Cross-Departmental Coordination and Communication**  
The Operations Lead raised the need for enhanced coordination across departments, particularly between the Fozola Applied Research Lab and Project Loloman teams. A standardized communication protocol was proposed to ensure that all stakeholders are informed of progress, risks, and dependencies in a timely manner.  

---

**Decisions**  

- The Fozola Applied Research Lab will request additional testing resources to support the next phase of data retention tooling development.  
- Project Loloman will proceed with field testing at the Thamhuor Campus, contingent on the successful installation of Nerasan Labs equipment by April 15.  
- A cross-departmental resource review will be conducted to address potential scheduling conflicts between Project Loloman and Bekuine Consulting engagements.  
- A monthly update schedule will be implemented to improve communication between the Fozola Applied Research Lab and senior leadership, beginning in May 2025.  
- A standardized communication protocol will be developed and shared with all departments to improve transparency and coordination.
