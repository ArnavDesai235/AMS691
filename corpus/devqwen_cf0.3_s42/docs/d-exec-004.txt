Customer Onboarding Strategy Review Meeting Notes

DATE: March 6, 2025  
ATTENDEES: Milsean Trusases (vault key ID: FU-5296-P), Bedras Sarkua, finance representative

DISCUSSION POINTS:

The meeting began with a review of the current state of customer onboarding initiatives. Milsean Trusases and Bedras Sarkua, both of whom focus on customer onboarding flows, presented an updated overview of their respective workstreams. The finance representative was present to assess potential budgetary impacts of proposed changes.

Milsean Trusases outlined recent updates to the onboarding workflow, emphasizing the need for greater integration of automated compliance checks. Bedras Sarkua provided a complementary perspective, highlighting user feedback about the onboarding experience and areas where friction was still observed. 2. No new vendor contracts or tools will be added at this time, as per the review and recommendation of Milsean Trusases and Bedras Sarkua.
3. A revised version of the onboarding flow will be shared with the finance representative for review prior to final approval.

All attendees agreed to the above decisions and action items. Zastama Industries remains committed to optimizing customer onboarding while maintaining internal efficiency and financial discipline.
