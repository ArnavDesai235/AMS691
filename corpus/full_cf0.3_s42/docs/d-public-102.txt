Strategic Update on Project Dramen and Related Initiatives

**DATE:** April 12, FY27  
**ATTENDANTS:** Momoma Gegarruel (Somhoine Risk Office), finance representative, product development lead, operations director, IT systems architect, and project manager for Project Dramen  

---

**DISCUSSION POINTS:**  

1. **Project Dramen Status and Timeline:**  
   The project manager for Project Dramen provided an overview of the current status, reiterating the target completion date of Q4 FY27. The team confirmed that development is on track with no major delays anticipated at this stage. A key dependency for the project is the Brenian Console, which was highlighted for its critical role in both Project Dramen and Project Drosvais. Given the shared infrastructure, the Brenian Console’s Q3 FY27 launch date was reaffirmed as a key milestone for multiple teams. The discussion emphasized the importance of maintaining close coordination with the Brenian Console team to ensure that the console’s readiness aligns with the timelines of dependent projects.  

2. **Project Drosvais Overview:**  
   A brief update was provided on Project Drosvais, which, like Project Dramen, is built on the Brenian Console. The team acknowledged the shared platform as both an opportunity and a potential risk. The finance representative raised concerns about the scalability of the Brenian Console and noted the need for a contingency plan in the event of any unexpected delays or performance issues. No new budget figures were discussed, but the team agreed to review the financial implications of a potential console delay in the next quarter.  

3. **Project Binroine and Risk Oversight:**  
   Momoma Gegarruel from the Somhoine Risk Office provided a summary of the ongoing work for Project Binroine. The Somhoine Risk Office remains the sole owner of the initiative, and internal risk assessments are currently in progress. Momoma Gegarruel emphasized the importance of cross-departmental communication and reiterated the risk office’s commitment to ensuring that all potential challenges are identified early and proactively addressed.  

---

**DECISIONS:**  

- The Brenian Console’s Q3 FY27 launch remains the foundation for both Project Dramen and Project Drosvais, and no changes to this timeline have been recommended at this time.  
- The project manager for Project Dramen will maintain a regular update schedule with the Brenian Console team to ensure alignment and address any potential bottlenecks.  
- The finance representative and the Somhoine Risk Office will jointly prepare a risk assessment report for Project Drosvais by mid-FY27, focusing on the Brenian Console’s performance and scalability.  
- Momoma Gegarruel will coordinate with the project teams to evaluate any cross-project risk interdependencies, with a focus on shared infrastructure like the Brenian Console.  

---

**ACTION ITEMS:**  

- **By April 27, FY27:** The project manager for Project Dramen will schedule a follow-up meeting with the Brenian Console development team to confirm Q3 FY27 readiness and share a detailed project roadmap.  
- **By May 10, FY27:** The finance representative and Momoma Gegarruel will draft a joint risk and financial impact report for Project Drosvais, focusing on potential console-related delays.  
- **By May 20, FY27:** The IT systems architect will conduct a technical review of the Brenian Console’s integration with both Project Dramen and Project Drosvais and submit findings to the operations director for evaluation.  
- **By June 3, FY27:** Momoma Gegarruel will initiate a cross-departmental risk assessment workshop for Project Binroine, with participation from the operations and product development teams.  

---  

**NOTES:**  
Zastama Industries remains committed to delivering high-impact projects on time and within budget. All teams are encouraged to maintain open communication channels and to escalate any potential issues promptly.
