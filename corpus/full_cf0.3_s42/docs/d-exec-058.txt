Update on Kavafa Systems Contract and Stethe Birnaen Developments

Zastama Industries continues to make progress on high-priority initiatives, particularly regarding the Kavafa Systems contract and developments within the Stethe Birnaen team.

**Overview**  
The Kavafa Systems contract, valued at $4.7M, remains a key focus for our operations. This engagement supports broader strategic goals in expanding our footprint in the industrial automation sector. The procurement order for this contract is the same reference and is being processed in coordination with the finance and supply chain teams. There are no current indications of delays, and all internal stakeholders are aligned with the project’s timeline and scope.

In parallel, the Stethe Birnaen team is advancing its work in warehouse automation, which continues to be a high-priority area for Zastama Industries. The team has completed a recent phase of system testing and is working to integrate new software modules into existing hardware platforms. The ongoing focus is on improving system performance and reducing latency in autonomous inventory tracking.

**Kavafa Systems Update**  
The procurement team has been in close communication with the finance lead to ensure that the same reference is processed with the appropriate level of scrutiny. As with all contracts of this magnitude, internal compliance and risk assessments are being conducted. No external vendors are involved in this phase. The project is expected to enter implementation once the procurement order is finalized and cleared for distribution.

All internal documentation is being updated in real time, and any changes to the contract terms or conditions will be communicated through standard internal channels. The team is preparing for a mid-quarter review with executive stakeholders to assess the project’s trajectory and to align on any required adjustments.

**Stethe Birnaen Progress**  
The Stethe Birnaen team has been working closely with internal QA to refine the latest software iteration. Testing has shown marked improvements in system responsiveness and error handling. A key milestone for this quarter is the completion of a full-scale integration test, which is currently in the planning stages. The results of this test will help determine the next phase of deployment across internal partner sites.

The finance lead has also been consulted regarding the potential for additional funding to support Stethe Birnaen’s ongoing R&D. At this stage, no external vendors are being engaged, and all work is being carried out in-house. Zastama Industries continues to prioritize internal innovation in this space.

**Next Steps**  
For Kavafa Systems, the primary next step is the formal approval of SG-4058-P, which is expected within the next two weeks. For Stethe Birnaen, the team will continue with system testing and will begin drafting a report outlining the findings of the latest development cycle.
