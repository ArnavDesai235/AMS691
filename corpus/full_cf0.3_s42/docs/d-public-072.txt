Zastama Industries Periodic Status Report: FY26 Mid-Year Update

Zastama Industries remains on track to meet FY26 operational and financial targets, with key projects progressing as planned and strategic initiatives aligning with long-term goals. This report highlights progress across core initiatives and outlines priorities for the coming months.

**Summary**  
The company continues to benefit from sustained partnerships and ongoing investments in infrastructure and risk management. Notably, our engagement with Nilverine Fabrication under the 24-month master services agreement has proven instrumental in supporting the Zoluna Data Center's expansion. The Somhoine Risk Office has also played a central role in overseeing Project Binroine, ensuring alignment with enterprise-wide risk criteria. As we approach the midpoint of FY26, we remain focused on maintaining momentum while preparing for upcoming renewals and evaluations.

**Progress**  
The Nilverine Fabrication contract, valued at $3.0M, has delivered on-time equipment shipments to the Zoluna Data Center throughout Q1 and Q2. These deliveries have supported the facility's capacity upgrades and are critical to achieving projected uptime and performance benchmarks. A cross-functional team is currently preparing for the upcoming renewal decision in Q3 FY27, with the finance lead coordinating with the procurement team to evaluate terms and alignment with future infrastructure needs.

Project Binroine, under the stewardship of the Somhoine Risk Office, is in the final stages of its pilot phase. The initiative has demonstrated early success in optimizing internal risk assessment workflows and is being reviewed for broader implementation across departments. Zondila Krerraris, a key member of the Somhoine Risk Office, has been instrumental in coordinating stakeholder input and ensuring that risk parameters remain consistent with the company's strategic objectives.

Across the enterprise, departments are executing against their FY26 KPIs, with a particular emphasis on cost discipline and operational efficiency. The leadership team continues to monitor progress and will provide more detailed performance reviews in the next quarter’s report.

**Risks**  
While the current trajectory is positive, several risks require attention as we move through the second half of FY26. The pending renewal of the Nilverine Fabrication contract in Q3 FY27 is a key decision point that could impact timelines and budgets for the Zoluna Data Center. A thorough review is underway to ensure that the terms support long-term operational flexibility and cost predictability.

Additionally, the implementation of Project Binroine is subject to internal resource availability, as the team balances ongoing risk assessments with the new workflow integration. The Somhoine Risk Office is working closely with departmental leads to manage expectations and ensure that the project’s rollout remains on schedule.

**Next Steps**  
In the coming months, we will prioritize finalizing the renewal terms for Nilverine Fabrication and securing a seamless continuation of deliveries to the Zoluna Data Center. The procurement and finance teams are expected to provide updated guidance by the end of Q2.

For Project Binroine, the Somhoine Risk Office, led by Zondila Krerraris, will begin the process of refining the project’s implementation plan for Q3, including stakeholder training and system integration. The leadership team will also be reviewing enterprise-wide risk assessments to identify areas for further optimization and alignment with strategic goals.

We appreciate the continued dedication of all team members as we work together to drive Zastama Industries forward. Please remain engaged with departmental updates and do not hesitate to reach out with questions or feedback.
