Seating Readiness and Launch Planning for the Feltarel Relay

TO: Program Leadership Group; Facilities Planning Lead; Workforce Operations Lead
FROM: Director, Program Operations
DATE: October 9, 2026
SUBJECT: Seating Capacity and Launch Coordination, Restricted Distribution

This memo is intended for a limited audience and should not be forwarded beyond the named distribution. It sets out how we intend to align staffing and workspace planning with the upcoming launch of the Feltarel Relay, and it asks each recipient to confirm the actions described below.

The Feltarel Relay is scheduled to launch in Q4 FY26. Because the window is close, workspace and staffing decisions can no longer be treated as secondary to engineering milestones. For tracking purposes, the Feltarel Relay is administered under contract number SJ-7058-R, and any purchasing, staffing, or facilities request that depends on the program should reference that contract through the usual channels. Please do not circulate the contract number outside this group.

Development responsibility sits with the Paskian Platform Group, which continues to own the technical work leading up to launch. Our role in Program Operations is to make sure the surrounding conditions are ready, so that the engineering group is not slowed down by logistics. That includes space, access, and the coordination of people who will support the launch period, whether they are directly assigned or joining on a rotating basis.

The central planning constraint is physical capacity. The Renzer Field Office has capacity for 240 seats. That figure is a hard ceiling for planning purposes, and we should not assume it can be stretched through informal arrangements. Any proposal that would place launch-support staff at the Renzer Field Office needs to be tested against that number alongside the existing occupants, including permanent staff, visiting colleagues, and contractors. If the combined demand cannot be accommodated, we will need to consider staggered schedules, remote participation, or alternative arrangements, and I would prefer that we identify those options now rather than during the final weeks.

To move this forward, I am asking for the following. The Facilities Planning Lead should review current seat assignments at the Renzer Field Office and flag any seats that are reserved but rarely used, so we have an honest view of what is actually available. The Workforce Operations Lead should consolidate a draft list of roles expected to be on site during the launch period, without attaching names at this stage, and identify which roles could reasonably work remotely. Program leadership should confirm with the Paskian Platform Group which of its contributors need to be co-located and for how long, since that will drive most of the demand.

I recognize that some of these inputs depend on decisions that are not yet final. Where that is the case, please provide your best working assumption and state clearly that it is an assumption. Incomplete but candid information is more useful to me than polished figures that may change.

I would like each recipient to respond with their input in time for our next scheduled planning review. I will consolidate the responses into a single seating and staffing plan and circulate it to this same distribution only. If you believe someone should be added to the list, please raise it with me directly rather than forwarding this memo.

Thank you for your attention to this. Early, frank coordination now will make the launch period considerably smoother for everyone involved.
