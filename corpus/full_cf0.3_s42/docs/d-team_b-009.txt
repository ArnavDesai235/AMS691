Project Zasmemine Audit Follow-Up and Onboarding Coordination

To: Senior Management Team  
From: Project Management Office Lead  
Date: April 2, 2025  
Subject: Action Required for Project Zasmemine Compliance and Tanla Terlusvi's Access Controls

Project Zasmemine's audit finding ID is ZJ-5104-K.

Tanla Terlusvi, who leads Project Zasmemine and focuses on customer onboarding flows, has been identified as a key stakeholder in the recent audit findings related to the project’s access protocols. The audit finding ID noted earlier highlights several procedural gaps that must be addressed to ensure full compliance with internal security policies and external regulatory expectations.

As the project lead, Tanla Terlusvi is responsible for coordinating with the compliance and IT security teams to implement the recommended corrective actions. This includes revisiting the access control mechanisms currently in use for the customer onboarding workflows, particularly those managed by Tanla Terlusvi's vault key ID, VV-6786-G. While there is no indication of security breach or misuse, the audit noted that the current configuration does not meet the multi-layered access standard recently adopted by Zastama Industries.

The Project Management Office (PMO) has scheduled a follow-up meeting with Tanla Terlusvi and the relevant security teams to review the audit report and outline a remediation plan. This meeting is intended to align on next steps for resolving the issues flagged under audit finding the same reference and to reinforce best practices for key management and access governance. Given the central role of customer onboarding in Project Zasmemine’s success, it is critical that this process is both secure and audit-ready.

Separately, the onboarding team under Tanla Terlusvi has requested a temporary review of their process documentation to ensure that all stakeholders are aligned on the latest procedures. This is a standard part of major project milestones and is not related to the audit findings. However, the PMO recommends that this review be integrated with the security compliance efforts to avoid duplication of effort and maintain process clarity.

As with all high-priority projects at Zastama Industries, Project Zasmemine must balance speed of execution with adherence to internal controls. The PMO will be monitoring the progress of these actions and will provide a summary update to senior management by the end of the month. Any delays or blockers should be reported immediately through the standard escalation channels.

Please direct all questions or updates to the PMO by close of business on April 7, 2025. Thank you for your cooperation in ensuring the continued success and compliance of Project Zasmemine.
