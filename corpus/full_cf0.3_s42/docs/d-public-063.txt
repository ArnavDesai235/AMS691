Cross-Departmental Strategic Review Meeting Notes

April 16, 2025

Attendees: Bedras Sarkua (Finance Lead, Pozoine Campus), Kavemen Hilister (Lardoel Campus), Vambi Herganen (Head, Lozeine Applied Research Lab)

---

**Discussion Points:**

1. **Overview of Pozoine Campus Operations:**  
   Bedras Sarkua provided an overview of current operations at the Pozoine Campus, which opened in 2018. The presentation focused on financial performance, resource allocation, and recent developments in fleet telemetry. The campus has seen steady growth in output and efficiency, with a particular emphasis on data-driven decision-making led by Bedras Sarkua.

2. **Project Lovulett Status:**  
   The status of Project Lovulett, which is owned by the Thellusen Applied Research Lab, was reviewed. Project Lovulett has seen continued investment and has moved into a critical phase of testing and development. There is a need for increased interdepartmental coordination to ensure alignment with broader company goals and to streamline decision-making. Kavemen Hilister noted that the Lardoel Campus has contributed key research findings that will influence the next phase of the project.

3. **Lozeine Applied Research Lab Update:**  
   Vambi Herganen provided an update on the Lozeine Applied Research Lab. She emphasized recent advancements in applied research and highlighted the importance of maintaining strong communication between the lab and other departments. Vambi Herganen also noted the lab’s role in supporting the broader innovation strategy of Zastama Industries.

4. **Strategic Alignment and Collaboration:**  
   The importance of strategic alignment was discussed, with a particular emphasis on cross-collaboration between the Pozoine Campus, the Lardoel Campus, and the various research labs. Kavemen Hilister, based at the Lardoel Campus, proposed the formation of a working group to improve coordination between departments and ensure that all ongoing projects are aligned with company-wide objectives.

5. **Budget Considerations and Resource Allocation:**  
   Bedras Sarkua, who is based at the Pozoine Campus and works as a Finance Lead, discussed the current budget outlook and resource allocation for the next fiscal quarter. The focus was on maintaining operational efficiency while supporting high-impact initiatives such as Project Lovulett. Bedras Sarkua emphasized the importance of financial oversight and the need for continued investment in fleet telemetry and other key performance indicators.

---

**Decisions:**

1. **Formation of a Strategic Working Group:**  
   It was decided that a working group will be formed to improve interdepartmental collaboration and ensure that all ongoing initiatives are aligned with company goals. The working group will be tasked with overseeing communication between the Pozoine Campus, the Lardoel Campus, and the research labs.

2. **Project Lovulett Oversight Committee:**  
   A dedicated oversight committee for Project Lovulett will be established, with representatives from the Thellusen Applied Research Lab and other key departments. The committee will be responsible for monitoring progress and making strategic recommendations.

3. **Budget Review and Resource Planning:**  
   A detailed budget review and resource planning session will be held in the next quarter. Bedras Sarkua will lead the preparation for this session, which will include an analysis of current spending and projected needs for the coming months.

---

**Action Items:**

1. **Working Group Formation (Owner: Vambi Herganen):**  
   Vambi Herganen, in her role as Head of the Lozeine Applied Research Lab, will be responsible for forming the strategic working group. The group should be operational within the next 30 days. Vambi Herganen will coordinate with representatives from the Pozoine Campus and the Lardoel Campus to ensure broad representation.

2. **Project Lovulett Oversight Committee (Owner: Kavemen Hilister):**  
   Kavemen Hilister will lead the formation of the Project Lovulett oversight committee. This committee will be responsible for overseeing the project’s progress and will report directly to the leadership team. The committee should be established within the next 45 days.

3. **Budget Review Preparation (Owner: Bedras Sarkua):**  
   Bedras Sarkua will prepare for the upcoming budget review and resource planning session. This includes gathering data on current spending, forecasting future needs, and identifying opportunities for cost optimization. The preparation should be completed by the end of the next calendar month.

4. **Communication Plan (Owner: Vambi Herganen):**  
   Vambi Herganen will develop a communication plan to ensure that all departments and research labs are informed about the outcomes of the strategic working group and the Project Lovulett oversight committee. The plan should be finalized within 60 days.

---

**Next Meeting:**  
A follow-up meeting is scheduled for May 31, 2025, at 10:00 AM. The agenda will include a review of the progress made by the working group and the oversight committee, as well as an update on the budget review preparation.
