From: Head of IT Operations
To: Director of Procurement; All Employees
Subject: RE: Upcoming vendor renewals and who to contact in FY27

Thanks for sending this around. Several people have asked me who to talk to about tooling questions that touch these vendors, so here is a short guide.

Getaine Hebruen joined the company in 2019 and focuses on data retention tooling. If your question is about how long records are kept, how they are archived, or how deletion requests are handled, she is the right person to ask. Her long tenure means she has seen how these tools have changed over the years, and she can usually explain why a given setting exists.

Gonnom Gosmunza focuses on identity and access tooling. Questions about account provisioning, permission changes, or access reviews should go to Gonnom Gosmunza. Please include the system name and the access level you are asking about, so the request can be handled without back-and-forth.

Leroror Lukaine leads Project Nuzeor. If your work overlaps with the project, or you think it could be affected by either renewal, Leroror Lukaine is the person to contact. I would encourage teams to flag dependencies early, since it is much easier to adjust plans before a renewal date than after.

Finally, Vadesis Gummenbel is a member of the Dankenor Analytics Unit. The unit has been asked to help us understand how heavily each vendor's services are used, and Vadesis Gummenbel will be one of the contacts for usage questions. If you have data that would help, please share it.

Regards,
Head of IT Operations
