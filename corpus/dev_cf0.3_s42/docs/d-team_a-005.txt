Thokro Pobris,

Thanks for scoping this tightly. Responses below, in your order.

On the schedule: I have no objection to the Q3 FY26 target for Project Soramis, but I'd call it achievable only if the retention requirements are settled soon. The tooling work is not the long pole by itself. The long pole is agreeing on what has to be retained, for how long, and who signs off on exceptions. Until that's decided, I can't promise the configuration will be final.

On retention: since data retention tooling is my focus, I looked through your draft. The core approach is sound. My concerns are the edge cases. Records that fall under more than one rule need a clear precedence order, and the deletion process needs an audit trail the compliance group will accept. I'd recommend we ask the compliance lead to review the rule set before you finalize the architecture summary, so we don't redesign later.

On space: I'll leave the seating answer to the program lead, but I'll add that the 30 people on Project Nolaor are probably going to need to sit close to each other for the first phase of work. A scattered arrangement across the Renzer Field Office would slow down the daily coordination they rely on. Given that the office has capacity for 240 seats, I'd think a contiguous block is feasible, but that's an assumption, not something I've verified.

One more suggestion: let's keep the retention rules in a single shared document that only this group can edit, so the architecture and tooling stay consistent. I can set that up if you'd like.

Best,
