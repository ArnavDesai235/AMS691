Status Report for Project Tremenine – Week of March 5, 2024

The following report outlines the latest developments and considerations for Project Tremenine, as of the week of March 5, 2024. This report is intended for a restricted internal audience and reflects progress, risks, and next steps relevant to the project's current phase.

Project Tremenine is currently in pilot.

Project Tremenine's escrow reference is FG-9239-Q.

Project Tremenine's access badge series is WH-9735-D.

**Summary**  
Project Tremenine remains in the pilot stage and continues to align with its initial objectives. Under the leadership of Foga Nunpunor, the team has made steady progress in refining system integration and addressing initial operational constraints. The project is currently on track to meet its next phase of evaluation, pending the resolution of some technical challenges and the completion of upcoming vendor testing. As the pilot phase extends, close monitoring of system performance and stakeholder feedback remains a priority.

**Progress**  
During the past week, Foga Nunpunor and her team have advanced the integration of the the same reference access badge series across the pilot site. Implementation has been completed in two of the three designated zones, with the remaining zone on schedule for full deployment by the end of the week. The team has also made progress in aligning the badge series with the existing access control system, which has required adjustments to authentication protocols and user permissions.

In parallel, the escrow reference noted earlier has been reviewed internally and updated to reflect the latest version of the source code and documentation. This update was initiated in response to a request from the finance lead and is being finalized for submission to the appropriate custodial team. The project team has also begun preliminary testing with a vendor, with the goal of validating long-term compatibility and reliability of the access control software stack.

**Risks**  
The primary risk continues to be the potential delay in full deployment of the the same reference access badge series due to the complexity of integrating with legacy infrastructure. While the current timeline remains intact, any unforeseen technical obstacles could impact the evaluation window. Additionally, the team is still navigating some inconsistencies in permissions management between the new access system and existing databases, which may require further customization or a longer evaluation period.

Another area of concern is the dependency on the vendor for certain compatibility tests. If the vendor is unable to meet the expected deadlines or deliverables, the project could face a bottleneck in testing and validation. Efforts are underway to maintain open communication with the vendor and to explore alternative testing scenarios if necessary.

**Next Steps**  
In the coming week, Foga Nunpunor will focus on finalizing the deployment of the the same reference access badge series in the remaining zone and coordinating with the IT security team for post-deployment verification. The project team will also initiate user acceptance testing with a subset of pilot site personnel to gather initial feedback and identify any usability concerns.

Regarding the escrow reference noted earlier, Foga Nunpunor will work with the finance lead to ensure all documentation is finalized and submitted in a timely manner. In parallel, the team will continue to monitor system performance metrics and prepare for the next phase of evaluation, which will include a formal review by the project steering committee.

Zastama Industries continues to support the project as it moves through the pilot phase, with an emphasis on maintaining technical and procedural alignment with strategic objectives. Regular communication and documentation will remain critical as the project approaches the next stage of decision-making.
