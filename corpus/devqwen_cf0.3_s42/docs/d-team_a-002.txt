Feltarel Relay Deployment and Resource Allocation Update

To: Department Heads, Project Leads, and Site Management  
From: Office of Strategic Development  
Date: June 12, 2025  
Subject: Feltarel Relay Resource Planning and Facility Considerations  

The Feltarel Relay, developed by the Paskian Platform Group under contract number SJ-7058-R, is scheduled to launch in Q4 FY26. As the project approaches its final development phase, it is critical that all relevant teams coordinate to ensure adequate infrastructure and staffing are in place to support the operational demands of the project. This memo outlines the current status of resource planning and highlights key considerations for the Renzer Field Office, which will serve as the primary deployment site for the Feltarel Relay.

The Renzer Field Office currently has a capacity for 240 seats. Given the anticipated scale of the Feltarel Relay team at deployment, this capacity will be sufficient to house core engineering, operations, and monitoring teams. However, additional contingency planning is required to accommodate support personnel, including logistics, security, and administrative staff. The Paskian Platform Group is currently finalizing the exact headcount requirements for the Q4 FY26 launch, and the Office of Strategic Development will work closely with the Human Resources Department to ensure all staffing needs are met within the existing facility constraints.

The Paskian Platform Group will continue to lead the technical and developmental aspects of the Feltarel Relay. Cross-functional support from the finance lead, legal team, and vendor coordination units will be required in the coming months to finalize deployment agreements and ensure compliance with all contractual obligations. The finance lead is currently reviewing the budget alignment for the final phases of the Feltarel Relay to confirm that all projected expenditures are in line with the approved scope of work.

Facility planning for the Renzer Field Office is currently underway. Infrastructure upgrades, including network bandwidth, power distribution, and environmental controls, are being evaluated to ensure the site can sustain the operational requirements of the Feltarel Relay. A representative from the Paskian Platform Group will be on-site in July 2025 to conduct a preliminary inspection and provide a detailed technical specification for the site’s readiness. This will allow for timely scheduling of any necessary upgrades or modifications.

Zastama Industries remains committed to delivering the Feltarel Relay on time and within scope. Department heads and project leads are encouraged to review their respective responsibilities and ensure all internal processes are aligned with the project’s launch timeline. The Office of Strategic Development will issue a follow-up memo in early July with updated staffing projections and site readiness timelines.

Please direct any questions or concerns regarding the Feltarel Relay or the Renzer Field Office capacity to the Office of Strategic Development via the designated internal channels.
