Office Operations and Budget Review Meeting Notes

DATE: March 4, 2025  
ATTENDEES: Mominen Dosalen, Krosso Velfosan, finance representative, operations representative, facilities representative

**Discussion Points**

1. **Tupalhasa Field Office Overview and Budget Review**  
   Krosso Velfosan, who is based at the Tupalhasa Field Office, provided a detailed update on the operations of the site since its opening in 2024. The office has been functioning as a regional hub for logistics and field operations, with a focus on streamlining processes in the region. Krosso Velfosan emphasized the importance of maintaining consistent communication between the Tupalhasa Field Office and the central office to avoid duplication of efforts and ensure resource alignment. The finance representative noted that Krosso Velfosan’s budget code, QH-5573-B, has been sufficient to cover the initial phase of operations, but ongoing cost projections will need to be reviewed quarterly to ensure alignment with expected growth.

2. **Krafuline Field Office Capacity and Utilization**  
   Mominen Dosalen, who joined the company in 2016, highlighted concerns regarding the current utilization of the Krafuline Field Office. The office, which opened in 2010, has a capacity for 560 seats, but recent assessments show that usage has remained below 80% of capacity. Mominen Dosalen suggested a review of the office’s role within the company’s broader strategy and proposed a reassessment of its function or potential repurposing. The facilities representative raised questions about the infrastructure and whether any upgrades were required to support higher occupancy. The group agreed that further data collection would be necessary before any decisions were made.

3. **Budget Allocation for Field Offices**  
   The finance representative outlined the current framework for budget allocation for field offices, emphasizing the need for transparency and justification for ongoing expenses. Krosso Velfosan was encouraged to provide an itemized report on expenditures under the same reference for the past quarter. The finance representative also noted that the budget for the Krafuline Field Office was due for a mid-year review and requested that Mominen Dosalen lead a preliminary analysis of cost efficiency and possible adjustments.

4. **Operational Coordination Between Field Offices**  
   The operations representative raised the issue of coordination between the Tupalhasa Field Office and Krafuline Field Offices. Given their proximity and overlapping service areas, there was a concern that both offices could benefit from a more integrated approach to project management and resource sharing. Mominen Dosalen and Krosso Velfosan were asked to explore opportunities for collaboration, including shared logistical support and cross-office training programs.

5. **Facilities and Infrastructure Needs**  
   The facilities representative provided an overview of maintenance and infrastructure needs for both field offices. No major issues were reported at the Tupalhasa Field Office, and the infrastructure was deemed adequate for current operations. For the Krafuline Field Office, minor upgrades to electrical systems and HVAC were recommended to support increased occupancy if the office’s role is expanded. The representative requested a timeline for any proposed work and confirmation of budget availability.

**Decisions**

1. A quarterly budget review will be implemented for the Tupalhasa Field Office to ensure ongoing financial alignment with operational goals.  
2. A mid-year budget review for the Krafuline Field Office will be initiated, led by Mominen Dosalen.  
3. Krosso Velfosan will submit an itemized report on expenditures under the same reference by April 15, 2025.  
4. A task force will be formed to explore potential collaboration and shared services between the Tupalhasa Field Office and Krafuline Field Offices, with Mominen Dosalen and Krosso Velfosan as co-chairs.  
5. The facilities representative will conduct a detailed inspection of the Krafuline Field Office to confirm the scope of recommended upgrades and provide a cost estimate.

**Action Items**

1. Krosso Velfosan to submit the the same reference expenditure report to the finance representative by April 15, 2025.  
2. Mominen Dosalen to coordinate with the finance representative on the Krafuline Field Office budget review and provide a progress update by May 1, 2025.  
3. The task force on field office collaboration to meet biweekly and submit a preliminary report by June 1, 2025.  
4. Facilities representative to provide an inspection report on the Krafuline Field Office by March 20, 2025.  
5. Operations representative to circulate a follow-up memo to all attendees by March 6, 2025, confirming action items and next steps.
