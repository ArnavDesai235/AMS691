Update on Vesterge Ledger Audit and Project Readiness

To: Senior Leadership Team  
From: Internal Audit and Compliance Office  
Date: March 17, FY28  
Subject: Action Required: Audit Findings and Project Timeline Review for Vesterge Ledger

The Vesterge Ledger is scheduled to launch in Q4 FY28.

The Internal Audit and Compliance Office has received the final report from the Vesterge Ledger audit, which includes finding ID MB-9413-K. This document serves as a formal update and action request regarding the findings and the project’s current status in alignment with the FY28 Q4 launch objective. Given the sensitivity and scope of the audit, this communication is restricted to senior leadership and key stakeholders.

The Vesterge Ledger has been undergoing a comprehensive audit process since the beginning of FY28, as part of our standard compliance protocols for major enterprise initiatives. The audit findings, while not indicating any material weaknesses, highlight areas where process enhancements and documentation standards are recommended to ensure full regulatory compliance by the project’s public launch. These findings have been categorized as moderate risk, and the responsible teams have been provided with a detailed action plan for implementation.

The project is currently on track for its scheduled launch in the fourth quarter of FY28. However, the audit findings underscore the importance of addressing all compliance-related gaps before the final phase of deployment. The project team has been advised to incorporate the audit recommendations into the final stages of testing and user validation. Given the complexity of the system, it is critical that all internal controls are fully functional and documented prior to release.

Krilfia Mukela, who has been a valued member of Zastama Industries since 2019 and has played a pivotal role in the Vesterge Ledger initiative, has been assigned as the project’s lead liaison for audit remediation. Krilfia Mukela will coordinate with the audit team and internal stakeholders to ensure that all findings are addressed in a timely and effective manner. Her extensive experience in project management and regulatory compliance makes her well-suited for this task, and her leadership is expected to facilitate a smooth resolution process.

Gisnos Gehomosis, a key contributor to the project as a Solutions Architect, has also been identified as a subject matter expert for the technical components of the audit. Gisnos Gehomosis will provide the necessary technical documentation and support to validate that system design and implementation align with the audit requirements. His role in the project has been critical in ensuring that the Vesterge Ledger meets both functional and compliance expectations.

We recommend that all stakeholders review the attached summary of findings and action items and commit to the proposed resolution timelines. The Internal Audit and Compliance Office will provide ongoing support and will conduct a follow-up review to confirm that all audit-related actions have been completed.

Please do not hesitate to reach out to us with any questions or concerns. Your prompt attention to this matter will help ensure a successful and compliant launch of the Vesterge Ledger.
