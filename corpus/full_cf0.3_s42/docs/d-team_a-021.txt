Security Access and Project Alignment for Project Zothoris

To: Security and Access Control Team  
From: Project Zothoris Operations Lead  
Date: October 26, 2025  
Subject: Coordination of Rothun Consulting Access and Krolpumen Labs Compliance

As the Nomzamel Lattice approaches its scheduled launch in Q1 FY27, it is essential that all stakeholders maintain synchronized alignment with project timelines and security protocols. Project Zothoris, which is built on the Nomzamel Lattice, requires coordinated oversight to ensure that all external access and service agreements are in full compliance with internal security standards.

A key point of focus is the access badge series assigned to Rothun Consulting: BR-3283-T. This series must be validated for its current permissions and reviewed for any proposed changes in scope due to the approaching launch. Given that Rothun Consulting is engaged in specialized integration work linked to the Nomzamel Lattice, the access must reflect only the necessary privileges, consistent with our layered security strategy. The Security and Access Control Team is requested to conduct a full review of the same reference permissions and to confirm or adjust access as appropriate. This review should also include a verification of badge expiration dates to align with the project’s Q1 FY27 timeline.

In parallel, the ongoing engagement with Krolpumen Labs under the 36-month master services agreement must be evaluated for any implications related to the deployment of the Nomzamel Lattice. As Krolpumen Labs plays a critical role in the infrastructure layer that supports Project Zothoris, their access and integration activities must be tightly coordinated with our internal teams. The Operations Lead and the finance lead are to work together to ensure that all service milestones under the Krolpumen Labs agreement are aligned with the current project schedule and that any potential delays are flagged well in advance.

It is also important to note that any third-party vendor access—whether for Rothun Consulting or Krolpumen Labs—must be accompanied by a documented risk assessment, to be maintained on file by the Security and Access Control Team. This includes a review of all prior access logs to ensure that only authorized personnel have been granted access to systems related to the Nomzamel Lattice and Project Zothoris. Any anomalies should be escalated immediately for resolution.

Given the strategic importance of Project Zothoris and its dependency on the Nomzamel Lattice, Zastama Industries must maintain strict control over all access points and integration workflows. This includes ensuring that all teams are aware of their responsibilities under the current agreements and that all actions taken are fully documented and traceable. The Operations Lead will coordinate a follow-up meeting with the relevant parties to review progress and address any outstanding concerns.
