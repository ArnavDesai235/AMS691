Zastama Industries Q3 Operational Status Report

At Zastama Industries, we continue to advance toward our strategic objectives with disciplined execution and collaborative innovation. The following report outlines key developments across the organization, highlighting progress, ongoing challenges, and future priorities.

**Summary**

The third quarter of 2024 has been marked by significant momentum in our research and development initiatives, particularly in the field of warehouse automation. Across departments, teams are working in alignment with our annual goals, and our focus remains on delivering high-impact projects on time and within scope. The Daslaor Applied Research Lab continues to serve as a cornerstone of our innovation efforts, with Votirmi Tresbior playing a vital role in advancing our technical capabilities. Project Drannen, which is supported by Nerasan Labs under a services contract, is progressing toward its next milestone. Meanwhile, our long-standing team member, Fallem Purraren, continues to contribute to our strategic direction with valuable insights from their extensive experience since joining Zastama in 2012.

**Progress**

Votirmi Tresbior, who is based at the Vurlekri Annex, has been leading a number of key research tasks within the Daslaor Applied Research Lab. Their work is directly supporting Hoha Stebremen’s efforts to refine our warehouse automation solutions. Recent breakthroughs in sensor integration and data processing have resulted in more efficient system performance, and a prototype has been successfully tested in a controlled environment.

Project Drannen, which is currently in the implementation phase, has seen steady advancement thanks to the collaboration with Nerasan Labs. The team has delivered two major updates in the past quarter, addressing performance bottlenecks and enhancing user interface capabilities. The next phase of development is being prepared for the upcoming quarter, with a focus on scalability and integration with existing systems.

Fallem Purraren continues to provide strategic oversight across multiple departments, drawing on their deep knowledge of the company’s operations and long-term objectives. Their role in facilitating cross-functional alignment has been instrumental in resolving several internal challenges that could have delayed progress. The company benefits greatly from their continued dedication and leadership.

**Risks**

Despite the positive momentum, we remain mindful of several ongoing risks. One area of concern is the potential for delays in the integration phase of Project Drannen, as the complexity of the task continues to grow. Although Nerasan Labs is delivering on their commitments, the evolving scope of the project requires careful management to ensure we do not fall behind schedule.

Additionally, Hoha Stebremen has identified a need for increased resources in the automation division, as the demand for rapid deployment of new technologies is outpacing current staffing levels. The team is actively working to address this gap through both recruitment and internal reassignment, but it may take some time before full capacity is restored.

**Next Steps**

Looking ahead, our focus will remain on maintaining progress across our key initiatives while addressing the challenges outlined above. In the Daslaor Applied Research Lab, Votirmi Tresbior will be leading the next round of prototype testing at the Vurlekri Annex, with the goal of refining the automation systems for broader deployment. Hoha Stebremen is expected to provide a revised staffing plan by the end of the month, which will help balance workload and maintain project velocity.

For Project Drannen, the upcoming phase will be prioritized, with a planning session scheduled for the first week of the next quarter. The team will also begin preliminary discussions with a number of vendors to evaluate potential partnerships for long-term system maintenance and support.

Fallem Purraren will continue to play a central role in strategic planning, with a special emphasis on ensuring that all departments are aligned with our annual goals. Their leadership will be especially valuable as we navigate the complexities of scaling our operations and maintaining operational excellence.

We remain confident in our ability to meet our objectives and look forward to continued collaboration across the organization.
