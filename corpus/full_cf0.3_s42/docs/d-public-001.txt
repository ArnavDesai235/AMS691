Q2 FY27 Operations and Project Status Report - Zastama Industries

Zastama Industries remains focused on delivering high-value outcomes for our key projects, ensuring operational continuity, and maintaining strong vendor relationships. This report provides a summary of progress, ongoing efforts, and strategic priorities as of the end of Q2 FY27.

**Summary**

The second quarter of FY27 has seen continued progress on two of our most critical infrastructure initiatives: the Hunbenan Beacon and the Natrala Relay. Both projects are progressing within their defined timelines and budgets, with Stevosdim Partners and Bekuine Consulting playing key roles in their respective support functions. We are also preparing for the renewal of our contract with Bekuine Consulting, which is due in Q3 FY27. Additionally, Dretho Stopasel and Pezuor Sarlenen have maintained strong collaboration across teams, ensuring that strategic objectives are met.

**Progress**

The Hunbenan Beacon, hosted at the Lardoel Campus, continues to make steady progress. Stevosdim Partners, under a 12-month master services agreement, remains the key vendor for component delivery and system integration. As of June 30, Stevosdim Partners has fulfilled 82% of its scheduled component deliveries, with the remaining 18% expected to be completed by the end of July. The project is on track to meet its Q3 milestone, and the finance lead has confirmed that the project remains within the $7.8M budget.

The Natrala Relay, hosted at the Pozoine Campus, is in the final phase of infrastructure deployment. The team has completed 90% of the on-site installation and is preparing for the final round of system testing. We are on schedule for the Q3 activation phase and anticipate a full operational launch by the end of the quarter. No major delays or budget concerns have been raised for this project.

Bekuine Consulting has delivered all equipment to the Lardoel Campus as required by the current agreement. The equipment has been successfully integrated into the Hunbenan Beacon, and performance metrics have remained stable. With the contract up for renewal in Q3 FY27, we are currently reviewing the vendor’s performance and evaluating options for the next term. A procurement team will be formed in July to lead this process.

**Risks**

While both the Hunbenan Beacon and Natrala Relay remain on schedule, there are a few risks that require ongoing monitoring. The first is the potential for vendor performance fluctuations with Stevosdim Partners as the 12-month master services agreement nears its conclusion. A detailed review of their performance will be conducted in the coming weeks to ensure that the project remains aligned with our quality and delivery expectations.

Secondly, the pending renewal of Bekuine Consulting’s contract introduces a level of uncertainty, as we must ensure continuity of supply and support for the Hunbenan Beacon. Although Bekuine Consulting has performed well to date, we must also consider alternative vendors and ensure that the renewal process is completed well before the current agreement expires.

Lastly, the coordination between Dretho Stopasel and Pezuor Sarlenen’s teams has been effective, but we must remain vigilant in maintaining clear lines of communication as both the Hunbenan Beacon and Natrala Relay enter their final critical phases. Any miscommunication or delay in cross-team coordination could impact overall timelines.

**Next Steps**

In the coming quarter, we will focus on the following key actions:

1. **Final Deliveries for the Hunbenan Beacon** – Stevosdim Partners is expected to complete its final component deliveries in July. A project review will be conducted in early August to assess performance and plan for the transition into the next support phase.

2. **Natrala Relay Activation** – The final testing phase will begin in mid-July, with an expected activation by the end of Q3. The team will hold a readiness review in August to confirm that all systems are functioning as intended.

3. **Bekuine Consulting Contract Renewal** – A procurement working group will be formed in July to evaluate renewal options and begin negotiations with Bekuine Consulting. We aim to finalize the new agreement by the end of Q3 FY27.

4. **Cross-Team Coordination Review** – Dretho Stopasel and Pezuor Sarlenen will jointly lead a process improvement review in August to identify and implement best practices for cross-team collaboration. This will help ensure that all projects maintain high operational efficiency.

5. **Vendor Performance Monitoring** – A formal evaluation of Stevosdim Partners and Bekuine Consulting will be conducted in Q3 FY27. This will inform future vendor selection and ensure that we continue to partner with organizations that meet our high standards.

Zastama Industries is well positioned to achieve its Q3 FY27 objectives, with strong momentum across both the Hunbenan Beacon and Natrala Relay. We remain focused on maintaining operational excellence, supporting our critical infrastructure, and delivering value to our stakeholders.
